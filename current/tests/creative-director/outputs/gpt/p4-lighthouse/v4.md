# BORROWED LIGHT
**Director’s shooting script — 120 seconds**

**Visual approach:** Painterly compositions; soft grain; restrained camera movement. Dawn is pearl-gray and faded rose. Night is indigo and charcoal. The shell gives off warm, opalescent light—the film’s only saturated color.

**Audio approach:** Score only: felt piano, low strings, and bowed-glass harmonics. **No dialogue, narration, or diegetic sound effects.** The shell’s “hum” is expressed through a sustained musical tone synchronized with its glow.

**On-screen text:** None throughout. The title above is not shown in the film.

All transitions are included in the timings below.
