# BORROWED LIGHT
**Short film · 2 minutes · No dialogue**

*Painterly images: slate-blue sea, bruised clouds, pale gold light. Soft edges, lingering compositions.*

*Sound: instrumental score only—sparse piano and low strings. The shell’s hum is expressed as a sustained musical tone. No voices or sound effects.*

### 0:00–0:20 — EXT. BEACH — DAWN

The lighthouse stands above a deserted shore, its beam fading against the morning.

Below it, a woman in a weathered keeper’s coat walks the tide line.

Among dark stones: a shell glowing softly, as though holding the last light of a sunset.

She crouches. Holds her hand above it.

Warm gold touches her worn fingers.

She lifts it.

*A single piano note, left to decay.*

### 0:20–0:35 — INT. KEEPER’S COTTAGE — MORNING

She sets the shell beside a chipped cup.

Its glow washes across the table. Across her face.

A small smile almost forms.

**MATCH CUT:** The same composition, hours later. The cup untouched. The window black.

### 0:35–0:55 — INT. KEEPER’S COTTAGE — NIGHT

Rain streaks the glass.

The shell begins to pulse.

*A low, unwavering tone enters the score.*

Through the window, the lighthouse beam passes once.

Then darkness.

The keeper rises sharply.

Far offshore, two tiny ship lights approach the headland. Lightning reveals the black teeth of the reef below.

She looks from them to the shell.

Takes it in both hands.

### 0:55–1:20 — EXT./INT. LIGHTHOUSE — NIGHT

She crosses the yard, bent into the storm, sheltering the shell beneath her coat.

Inside: a narrow spiral staircase.

She climbs.

Fragments of movement: boots on wet stone; one hand gripping the rail; gold leaking between her fingers.

Through a slit window, the ship lights draw nearer.

She slips on a step. Catches herself.

For a moment, she presses the shell to her chest.

Then climbs again.

*The strings gather urgency without losing their sorrow.*

### 1:20–1:40 — INT. LANTERN ROOM / EXT. SEA — NIGHT

The great lens turns around a dead lamp.

She works the controls. Nothing.

She opens the lamp housing and places the shell inside.

A breath of darkness.

Then gold blossoms through the glass.

The beam sweeps across the storm—revealing the reef directly ahead of the nearest ship.

Its lights turn.

Beyond it, the second vessel changes course.

From high above: two small ships pass safely into open water.

The keeper rests her forehead against the lantern frame.

*The score opens into a brief, aching warmth.*

### 1:40–2:00 — EXT. BEACH — DAWN

Pale morning. The sea lies quiet.

The keeper walks back to the place where she found the shell. It is dim now, cupped in her hands.

She kneels and sets it among the stones.

Her fingers linger.

A thin wash of water reaches it. The glow strengthens faintly beneath the surface.

She withdraws her hand.

**WIDE:** The keeper walks toward the distant lighthouse, alone beneath an immense sky.

In the foreground, the shell waits at the water’s edge.

*The opening piano note returns.*

**CUT TO BLACK.**
