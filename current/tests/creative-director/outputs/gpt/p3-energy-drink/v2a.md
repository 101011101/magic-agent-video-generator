# Video Script Brief: VOLT Energy Drink

## Task
Write a production-ready product-ad script with **exactly six shots**. Preserve all explicit user requirements. Treat the assumptions below as provisional, and do not invent product facts.

## User Requirements — Quoted Exactly
> “product ad for my energy drink, 6 shots, the can has to look exactly like my photo with the logo in every single shot”

These requirements are mandatory:
- Make a product advertisement for the user’s energy drink.
- Use exactly six shots.
- Match the can’s appearance to the uploaded photograph.
- Keep the logo visible in every shot.

## Goal and Core Idea
- **Goal:** Build product recognition and interest in the energy drink.
- **Core idea:** Present the distinctive can as the hero, making its matte-black finish, neon-green lightning-bolt logo, and white VOLT lettering memorable.
- Do not imply specific health, energy, focus, endurance, or performance benefits without user-provided, approved claims.

## Audience and Platform
**Not specified by the user. Provisional assumptions:**
- **Audience:** Adults aged 18+ who are potential energy-drink customers.
- **Platform:** Short-form social advertising suitable for Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts.
- Avoid platform-specific features until the intended platform is confirmed.

## Format, Duration, and Aspect Ratio
- **Format:** Six-shot product commercial.
- **Duration:** Assume 15 seconds total, approximately 2.5 seconds per shot.
- **Aspect ratio:** Assume vertical 9:16.
- The six-shot count is mandatory; duration and aspect ratio are assumptions.

## Tone and Style
**Assumed creative direction:**
- Bold, sleek, high-energy, and product-focused.
- Use strong contrast and neon-green accents to complement the packaging.
- Create energy through editing, lighting, camera movement, and sound—not unsupported product claims.
- Use music and sound effects; voiceover is optional and must not introduce unverified facts.

## Upload and Required Use
**One product photo, described as:**
> “a matte black 355 ml can with a neon green lightning-bolt logo and the word VOLT in white capital letters, on a plain white background.”

Use the uploaded photograph as the authoritative visual reference:
- Preserve the can’s shape, proportions, matte-black finish, logo design, lettering, placement, and packaging details.
- Do not redesign, recolor, stylize, or invent packaging features.
- Keep the lightning-bolt logo clearly visible and recognizable in **all six shots**. Also keep the VOLT lettering visible wherever possible.
- Plan framing, camera movement, lighting, and any props so they do not obscure, crop out, distort, or turn the logo away from the viewer.
- Avoid views requiring invented, unseen packaging details.
- The photo’s white background is a reference background, not a mandatory setting for the advertisement.
- If the original photo is unavailable to the scriptwriter, flag it as required for precise visual direction; the written description alone cannot establish an exact match.

## Assumptions Made
- VOLT is the brand name, inferred from the can’s lettering.
- Brand awareness is the primary objective because no specific sales objective was supplied.
- The audience, platforms, 15-second runtime, vertical aspect ratio, visual style, and sound direction are provisional choices.
- No price, flavor, ingredients, benefits, offer, website, or retailer information is assumed.

## Missing Information Only the User Can Supply
- Intended audience, market, and publication platform.
- Preferred duration, aspect ratio, and creative direction.
- Confirmed brand name and any brand guidelines.
- Approved product claims, flavor information, and required disclaimers.
- Desired call to action and its destination, such as a website or retailer.
- Any offer, price, launch date, or mandatory wording.

Do not invent these details. Use clearly marked placeholders where necessary.

## Required Script Output
Provide a table with exactly six numbered shots, including:
1. Time range.
2. Visual action and setting.
3. Camera framing and movement.
4. How the reference-matched can and logo remain visible.
5. On-screen text, if any.
6. Voiceover, music, and sound effects.

End with a brief check confirming six shots, the assumed total duration, exact-reference packaging direction, and visible logo placement in every shot.
