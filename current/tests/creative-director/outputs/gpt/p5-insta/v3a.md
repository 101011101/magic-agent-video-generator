The city is almost asleep. Rain glistens on the pavement, shop shutters are pulled down, and a tiny corner store spills fluorescent light onto an otherwise empty street.

A young adult in an oversized charcoal jacket steps outside, carrying a paper cup. Their white sneakers are spotless against the wet asphalt. They pause beside a puddle reflecting a flickering pink sign.

Then the reflection flickers differently.

A bright pink fish swims through the reflected letters. The person crouches, curious, and touches the water with one fingertip. A ripple spreads beyond the puddle, rolling across the pavement like liquid light.

Pink becomes violet, violet becomes electric blue. Color climbs the brick walls. Painted flowers bloom across the shutters, and the white stripes of a pedestrian crossing lift gently from the road, becoming a floating staircase.

The person smiles, sets down their cup, and climbs.

Above the rooftops, the sleeping city has become a luminous playground. Tiny constellations drift between chimneys. Laundry sways in an unfelt breeze, each shirt glowing a different color. The person reaches the highest step and sits, sneakers dangling over the sparkling streets.

The corner-store sign clicks off.

Everything settles back into place. The person stands beside the ordinary puddle again. They retrieve their cup and start walking home.

Only their shoelaces have changed: one pink, one electric blue, leaving faint sparks with every step.
