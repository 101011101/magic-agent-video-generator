## Production plan

**Format:** A 60-second Instagram Reel in 15 sequential chunks. No added narration, dialogue, or captions. The “OPEN” wording on the store sign is a production choice; it provides consistent lettering for the reflection.

### Chunk 1 — City Almost Asleep
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** Slow street-level push toward a tiny corner store. Wet pavement glistens; neighboring shop shutters are closed. Fluorescent light spills from the store onto the empty street.
- **On-screen text:** “OPEN” on the small pink corner-store sign. No overlays.
- **Audio:** No voice. Quiet nighttime city ambience, occasional water drips, fluorescent hum. A restrained ambient-electronic score begins.
- **Entities:** Corner store with a pink “OPEN” sign; closed metal shutters; wet brick buildings; nearby puddle and pedestrian crossing. Maintain this street layout throughout.
- **Environment:** Late night, almost deserted urban street. Cool charcoal and blue-gray palette, fluorescent white light, isolated pink accent.
- **Depends on:** None.
- **Source:** “The city is almost asleep. Rain glistens on the pavement, shop shutters are pulled down, and a tiny corner store spills fluorescent light onto an otherwise empty street.”

### Chunk 2 — Stepping Outside
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** The young adult exits the store carrying a paper cup in their left hand. Track downward briefly to show spotless white sneakers against wet asphalt.
- **On-screen text:** “OPEN” on the store sign when visible. No overlays.
- **Audio:** No voice. Door movement, soft footsteps on wet ground, continuing fluorescent hum and score.
- **Entities:** Person: young adult, medium-brown skin, short dark curls, oversized charcoal jacket, black trousers, spotless white sneakers with white shoelaces. Plain kraft-paper cup with an off-white lid.
- **Environment:** Same street and lighting. Store light edges the jacket and shoes.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 1 for store appearance, street geography, lighting, and sign.
- **Source:** “A young adult in an oversized charcoal jacket steps outside, carrying a paper cup. Their white sneakers are spotless against the wet asphalt.”

### Chunk 3 — An Unusual Reflection
- **Duration:** 3 seconds
- **Visual:** The person stops beside the puddle. Over-the-shoulder framing reveals the pink sign reflected in the water. Its reflection briefly flickers out of rhythm with the physical sign.
- **On-screen text:** “OPEN,” mirrored in the puddle; the physical sign may appear above it. No overlays.
- **Audio:** No voice. Score thins momentarily. Electrical flicker, a faint irregular synthetic pulse.
- **Entities:** Same person and cup, still held in the left hand. Same puddle and pink sign; distinguish the ordinary sign flicker from the anomalous reflection.
- **Environment:** Cool nighttime street; pink reflected light dominates the puddle.
- **Depends on:** Chunks 1–2 for geography, person identity, wardrobe, and cup.
- **Source:** “They pause beside a puddle reflecting a flickering pink sign. Then the reflection flickers differently.”

### Chunk 4 — Fish in the Letters
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** Close-up of the puddle. A bright pink fish swims through the reflected letters, weaving between them. Cut or tilt to the person’s curious expression as they crouch.
- **On-screen text:** Mirrored “OPEN” within the reflection. No overlays.
- **Audio:** No voice. A delicate watery shimmer joins the score; subtle jacket movement as the person crouches.
- **Entities:** Same person, holding the cup upright in their left hand. One luminous pink fish exists only within the reflection, never above the water.
- **Environment:** Same puddle location. Pink illumination softly reaches the person’s face.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 3 for puddle framing, reflected lettering, and person position.
- **Source:** “A bright pink fish swims through the reflected letters. The person crouches, curious, and touches the water with one fingertip.”

### Chunk 5 — Touch and Ripple
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** Tight shot of the person’s right index fingertip touching the water. A luminous ripple forms, crosses the puddle’s edge, and rolls across the wet pavement. Pull back enough to reveal that the ripple is no longer confined to water.
- **On-screen text:** None; frame away from readable signage.
- **Audio:** No voice. Small water-contact sound, followed by an expanding liquid-light whoosh. The score begins to swell.
- **Entities:** Same person and cup. Right index finger creates the ripple; cup remains in the left hand. Ripple begins pink.
- **Environment:** Existing street geometry remains unchanged as luminous energy spreads over it.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 4’s crouched pose and hand positions; Chunk 3’s puddle location.
- **Source:** “The person crouches, curious, and touches the water with one fingertip. A ripple spreads beyond the puddle, rolling across the pavement like liquid light.”

### Chunk 6 — Color Climbs the City
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** Follow the traveling light as pink becomes violet, then electric blue. Color climbs the brick walls. Flat, painted flower motifs unfurl across the closed shutters.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Audio:** No voice. Rising synth layers, soft blooming tones, and a shimmering sweep moving with the light.
- **Entities:** Same brick walls and shutters. Flowers remain painted graphics on the shutters, not physical plants. Preserve the ordered transition: pink → violet → electric blue.
- **Environment:** Nighttime street transitions from muted realism into luminous color while retaining its original architecture.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 5’s outward-moving ripple; Chunk 1’s building and shutter designs.
- **Source:** “Pink becomes violet, violet becomes electric blue. Color climbs the brick walls. Painted flowers bloom across the shutters…”

### Chunk 7 — A Crossing Becomes Stairs
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** Low-angle view of the pedestrian crossing. Its existing white stripes lift gently from the road, one after another, forming an ascending floating staircase toward the rooftops.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Audio:** No voice. Airy lift sounds and soft resonant pulses synchronized to each rising stripe. Music gains a gentle beat.
- **Entities:** The established pedestrian crossing. Each step retains the white rectangular form of an original crossing stripe; no added railings or supports.
- **Environment:** Same transformed street, with pink, violet, and electric-blue light reflected on wet asphalt.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 1’s crossing placement and Chunk 6’s completed color transformation.
- **Source:** “…and the white stripes of a pedestrian crossing lift gently from the road, becoming a floating staircase.”

### Chunk 8 — Leaving the Cup Behind
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** The person rises, smiles, and places the cup upright on the sidewalk beside the puddle. They step onto the first floating stripe and begin climbing.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Audio:** No voice. Gentle cup-placement tap, sneaker contact with the first step, brighter musical lift.
- **Entities:** Same person and unchanged wardrobe. White shoelaces remain white. The same cup is left at a clearly established retrieval position beside the puddle.
- **Environment:** Glowing street and floating staircase; original store remains below.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 7’s staircase position; Chunks 4–5’s person and cup continuity.
- **Source:** “The person smiles, sets down their cup, and climbs.”

### Chunk 9 — Above the Rooftops
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** Follow behind the person as they climb above roof level. Reveal the sleeping city transformed into a luminous playground, with sparkling streets below and small constellations drifting between chimneys.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Audio:** No voice. Airy rooftop ambience, delicate star-like chimes, expanding electronic score.
- **Entities:** Same person and floating crossing-stripe stairs. Existing rooftops and chimneys; tiny floating constellation clusters.
- **Environment:** Night sky above the same neighborhood. Luminous pink, violet, and electric-blue accents; city geometry remains recognizable.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 8’s climbing motion and Chunk 7’s stair geometry.
- **Source:** “Above the rooftops, the sleeping city has become a luminous playground. Tiny constellations drift between chimneys.”

### Chunk 10 — Glowing Laundry
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** Drift past a rooftop clothesline. Shirts sway gently despite the otherwise still air, each glowing a different color. Tiny constellations move between chimneys in the background; the person continues climbing farther back in frame.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Audio:** No voice. Quiet fabric rustle, small bell-like accents, continuing beat and ambient synths.
- **Entities:** Same person, staircase, chimneys, and constellations. Clothesline with distinct shirts; assign each shirt a fixed glow color and retain it until the reset.
- **Environment:** Rooftop nightscape. Still surrounding air; only the laundry exhibits the unfelt breeze.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 9’s rooftop layout, constellation design, and climbing direction.
- **Source:** “Tiny constellations drift between chimneys. Laundry sways in an unfelt breeze, each shirt glowing a different color.”

### Chunk 11 — The Highest Step
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Visual:** The person reaches the highest stripe and sits. Start with a side view of their sneakers dangling, then widen to reveal the sparkling streets below. Hold on their quiet delight.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Audio:** No voice. Music reaches its warm, luminous peak. Soft fabric movement and faint city ambience underneath.
- **Entities:** Same person and highest floating stripe. Spotless white sneakers still have white shoelaces. Cup remains below beside the puddle.
- **Environment:** Above the rooftops, surrounded by the established luminous city.
- **Depends on:** Chunks 9–10 for rooftop geography and magical elements; Chunk 8 for cup location.
- **Source:** “The person reaches the highest step and sits, sneakers dangling over the sparkling streets.”

### Chunk 12 — The Sign Clicks Off
- **Duration:** 2 seconds
- **Visual:** Cut to the corner-store sign. It gives one final flicker and switches off.
- **On-screen text:** “OPEN” before the switch-off; afterward, the same letters remain unlit. No overlays.
- **Audio:** No voice. A distinct electrical click. Musical peak abruptly drains into a soft sustained tail.
- **Entities:** The original pink “OPEN” sign. Do not introduce a switch operator or imply that the person turns it off.
- **Environment:** Original corner-store exterior. Interior fluorescent spill remains; only the sign switches off.
- **Depends on:** Chunks 1–3 for exact sign design and location.
- **Source:** “The corner-store sign clicks off.”

### Chunk 13 — Everything Settles
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** Magical color recedes. Painted flowers disappear, constellations fade, shirts return to ordinary fabric, and crossing stripes settle back onto the road. Use a gentle visual match transition to reveal the person standing beside the ordinary puddle again, without a fall.
- **On-screen text:** None; keep the unlit sign outside the main framing.
- **Audio:** No voice. Soft descending shimmer, settling sounds, then ordinary nighttime ambience. Music becomes minimal.
- **Entities:** Same person, restored street, ordinary puddle, and cup still upright at its established location. Reflected fish is gone. Keep shoelaces indistinct until the final reveal.
- **Environment:** Return to the initial charcoal and blue-gray nighttime palette, with fluorescent store light. Pink sign stays off.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 12’s switch-off; Chunks 6–11 for the elements being reset; Chunk 8 for cup placement.
- **Source:** “Everything settles back into place. The person stands beside the ordinary puddle again.”

### Chunk 14 — Walking Home
- **Duration:** 3 seconds
- **Visual:** The person retrieves the cup with their left hand and starts walking away from the store. Frame above shoe level to reserve the lace reveal.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Audio:** No voice. Cup pickup, natural footsteps, subdued score with a small returning melodic motif.
- **Entities:** Same person, jacket, trousers, and cup. Store, puddle, and crossing are ordinary again.
- **Environment:** Same quiet wet street, late-night fluorescent spill fading behind the person.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 13’s standing position and reset environment; Chunk 8’s precise cup location.
- **Source:** “They retrieve their cup and start walking home.”

### Chunk 15 — A Little Magic Remains
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Visual:** Low tracking close-up of the walking sneakers. Reveal a pink lace on the left shoe and an electric-blue lace on the right. Each footstep leaves faint, short-lived sparks over the wet pavement. End as the shoes move farther into the dark street.
- **On-screen text:** None.
- **Audio:** No voice. Wet-ground footsteps with delicate spark crackles. A final shimmering musical accent resolves the score.
- **Entities:** Same spotless white sneakers. Only the shoelaces have changed: left pink, right electric blue. Sparks are subtle and temporary; they do not transform the city again.
- **Environment:** Ordinary nighttime street in the original muted palette; localized lace color and faint sparks provide the only remaining magic.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 14’s walking direction and pace; Chunk 2’s original sneaker design; Chunk 13’s restored environment.
- **Source:** “Only their shoelaces have changed: one pink, one electric blue, leaving faint sparks with every step.”

## Global requirements

- **Total duration:** 60 seconds; chunk durations sum exactly to 60.
- **Aspect ratio:** Proposed Instagram Reel format: **9:16 vertical**, delivered at **1080 × 1920**, **24 fps**. Keep important action clear of interface-covered edges.
- **Identity:** Use one original, consistently recognizable young adult throughout. Lock face, skin tone, hair, body proportions, jacket, trousers, and sneaker design before generating shots. No uploaded identity reference is available.
- **Object continuity:** Maintain the same cup, store, sign, puddle, crossing, shutters, and street layout. Cup stays beside the puddle during the rooftop sequence. Shoelaces remain white before the reset, then pink on the left and electric blue on the right.
- **Music:** One continuous, original or appropriately licensed ambient-electronic track. Progress from subdued nighttime atmosphere to wonder, peak above the rooftops, fall away when the sign clicks off, and resolve softly at the lace reveal.
- **Voice:** No narration or dialogue. Tell the story through visuals, music, and sound effects.
- **Style:** Cinematic magical realism: believable materials, wet reflections, natural human movement, restrained luminous effects. The magical city must remain recognizably the original city.
- **Color:** Begin muted and cool. Introduce magic in the exact order **pink → violet → electric blue**. Restore the ordinary palette before revealing the changed shoelaces.
- **Text:** No captions, titles, calls to action, watermarks, or added branding. The only readable text is the diegetic “OPEN” sign and its mirrored reflection.
- **Editing and continuity:** Match action, camera direction, object positions, and light between adjacent chunks. Use character, location, and prop reference frames throughout. The reset must not depict the person falling.
- **Story fidelity:** Preserve all story events and their order. No extra characters, dialogue, explanations, or additional magical consequences.
