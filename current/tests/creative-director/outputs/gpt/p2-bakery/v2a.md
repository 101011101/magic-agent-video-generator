# Video Script Writing Prompt

## Task and Goal
Write a complete **20-second video advertisement** for a bakery. Make the bakery feel appealing and encourage nearby viewers to visit. The key takeaway is the bakery’s name, opening hours, and address, communicated through the required end card.

## User Requirements — Quoted Exactly
- “ad for my bakery”
- “20 sec”
- “the end card must say exactly 'Sweet Crumbs — Open 7am–3pm, 12 Elm St'”
- “dont spell anything wrong”

## Audience and Platform
- **Assumed audience:** Nearby residents and potential customers looking for a local bakery.
- **Assumed platform:** Instagram Reels or a similar short-form social platform.
- These are working assumptions, not user-confirmed details.

## Format, Duration, and Aspect Ratio
- **Format:** Short promotional video with bakery-themed visuals, concise on-screen copy, and optional voiceover.
- **Total duration:** Exactly 20 seconds, including the end card.
- **Assumed aspect ratio:** Vertical, 9:16.
- **Assumed end-card timing:** Final 4 seconds, from 00:16 to 00:20.

## Tone and Style
- **Assumed tone:** Warm, welcoming, appetizing, and upbeat.
- **Assumed style:** Simple, polished visuals with readable text and gentle pacing. Avoid an overcrowded screen.

## Mandatory End Card
Display this text exactly, without quotation marks:

Sweet Crumbs — Open 7am–3pm, 12 Elm St

Preserve every character, spelling, capitalization, space, and punctuation mark, including:
- The em dash in “Sweet Crumbs — Open”
- The en dash in “7am–3pm”

Keep the end card clearly readable. Do not add any other text to it.

## Uploads and Asset Use
- **Uploads:** None.
- Do not refer to uploaded footage, photos, logos, or audio.
- Suggest bakery-themed visuals that could be filmed or sourced later. Do not present illustrative imagery as actual footage of this business.
- Do not invent a logo or assume brand colors.

## Assumptions and Content Boundaries
- “Sweet Crumbs” is assumed to be the bakery’s name, based on the required end card.
- The supplied hours and address are to be used as written.
- Any music, voiceover, visual direction, and pacing are creative recommendations, not user requirements.
- Specific products, prices, promotions, awards, ingredient claims, and business history are unknown. Do not invent them.
- If a shot depends on an actual product or business asset, label that dependency clearly.

## Missing Information Only the User Can Supply
Not required to draft the script, but needed to tailor or produce it:
- Intended platform and preferred orientation.
- Specific customers or locality to target.
- Actual products to feature and any approved promotional claims.
- Available bakery footage, product photos, logo, and brand guidelines.
- Any required voiceover, music, or production preferences.

## Required Script Output
Provide a timestamped script covering exactly 20 seconds, with columns for:
1. Time range
2. Visuals/action
3. On-screen text
4. Voiceover/audio

Proofread all written and spoken copy. Verify the final end-card text character for character against the supplied wording.
