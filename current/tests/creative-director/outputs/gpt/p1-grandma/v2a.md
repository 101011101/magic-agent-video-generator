# Task
Write a production-ready script for a short image-to-video animation using the uploaded photograph. Do not generate the video itself.

## Goal and Core Idea
Bring the user’s photograph of their grandmother gently to life: she transitions from a neutral expression to a warm smile and gives a small, natural wave toward the camera. The moment should feel like a friendly personal greeting, without inventing dialogue or a backstory.

## User Requirements — Exact Quote
> “animate this photo of my grandma so she smiles and waves, like 5 seconds”

All stated requirements must be fulfilled:
- Animate the uploaded photograph of the user’s grandmother.
- Show her smiling and waving.
- Keep the video approximately five seconds long.

## Audience and Platform
- **Audience:** Not specified. Assume the user and their family or close friends.
- **Platform:** Not specified. Treat this as a platform-neutral video suitable for personal sharing; do not add platform-specific elements.

## Format, Duration, and Aspect Ratio
- **Format:** A single, continuous image-to-video shot.
- **Duration:** Approximately 5 seconds.
- **Aspect ratio:** Preserve the uploaded photograph’s original aspect ratio and framing. Its numerical aspect ratio is not provided; do not assume one or crop unnecessarily.

## Tone and Style
Warm, gentle, affectionate, and naturalistic. Use subtle, photorealistic motion rather than exaggerated cartoon animation.

## Upload and Required Use
**One uploaded photograph**, described as:
> “an elderly woman with short white hair, round glasses and a green cardigan, sitting in an armchair in a sunlit living room, looking at the camera with a neutral expression.”

Use this photograph as the sole visual reference and starting frame.
- Preserve her recognizable facial features, age, short white hair, round glasses, and green cardigan.
- Retain the armchair, sunlit living room, lighting, and original composition.
- Animate her expression into a gentle smile and add a small wave with one hand.
- Keep her seated and looking toward the camera.
- Keep hand movement anatomically plausible and consistent with the available framing. If her hands are initially outside the frame, introduce the waving hand naturally without changing the shot.
- Avoid facial distortion, identity drift, extra fingers, clothing changes, or unnecessary background movement.

## Assumptions for Unstated Details
These are production defaults, not additional user requirements:
- The camera remains stationary, with no cuts, zooms, or transitions.
- The smile develops smoothly, followed by or overlapping with a brief wave.
- The final moment holds the warm smile.
- The video is silent, with no dialogue, music, captions, titles, or added graphics.
- No other people, objects, or narrative events are introduced.

## Missing Information Only the User Can Supply
The user has not specified:
- The intended audience or sharing platform.
- Any required output aspect ratio that differs from the photograph.
- Whether sound, music, or text is desired.

These are not blockers; proceed using the assumptions above unless the user supplies different preferences.

## Required Script Output
Provide a concise, timecoded script covering approximately 0–5 seconds. For each beat, describe her expression, gaze, and hand movement. Include a brief production note identifying the uploaded photo as the visual reference and the identity-preservation constraints. Do not invent spoken words or personal history.
