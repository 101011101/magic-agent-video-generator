# Golden Hour Loop — Operational Chunks

- **Character — The woman** (locked once introduced): young woman, loose white shirt, sunglasses, hair that moves in the breeze; sits cross-legged on the rooftop ledge holding a cold drink (glass with ice cubes, condensation on the outside).
- **Environment:** city rooftop — concrete ledge, a row of potted plants, small rooftop pool with a rippled surface reflecting the skyline; two glass towers with the sun sinking between them.
- **Time of day:** golden hour, the very end of the day.
- **Lighting:** low warm sun, everything it touches turned honey-gold; warm hazy air.
- **Palette:** honey gold, warm amber, soft concrete greys.
- **Style:** stylized cinematic, dreamy, slightly surreal; every movement cut/timed to the beat.
- **Audio:** distant traffic blurred into a soft hum; laid-back beat with a deep bassline.
- **On-screen text:** none.
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (Instagram Reels).
- **Total duration:** not specified in the request — ~15 seconds, built as a seamless loop.
- **Loop requirement:** the last frame must match the first frame.
