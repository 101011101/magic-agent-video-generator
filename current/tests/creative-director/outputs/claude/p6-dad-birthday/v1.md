**Format:** 9:16 vertical (easy to send by text/WhatsApp or post); can switch to 16:9 for a TV
**Tone:** Warm, celebratory, a little sentimental, a little fun
**Music:** Upbeat, feel-good acoustic track (or a song your dad loves — just tell me which)

To put your dad in the video, I'll need **3–6 photos of him** — ideally a mix of old (younger years) and recent, plus at least one clear, front-facing photo of his face. A couple of family photos are a great bonus. The script below shows where each one goes.
