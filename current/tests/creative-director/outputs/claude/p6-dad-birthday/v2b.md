# Script: "Happy 60th, Dad" (20 s)

> **Before I can finish this:** I need **photos or video clips of your dad**. You asked for him to be in it, and I won't generate a made-up "dad" in his place. The script below is ready to go, with his shots marked as placeholders. Send 3–5 clear photos (a mix of recent and older ones works beautifully) and I'll drop them in.

**Format:** Short celebratory video, music + on-screen text
**Duration:** 20 seconds
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical; his face and all text kept in the central safe area so it still works letterboxed on a TV at the party
**Audio:** Upbeat, feel-good instrumental (acoustic/light pop, ~110 BPM). No voiceover. No copyrighted "Happy Birthday" recording.
**Look:** Warm golden light, gold and cream balloons, soft confetti, candle glow, a recurring gold "60" motif.

---

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Opening burst (0:00–0:04)
**Visual:** Warm, softly blurred party background with string lights. A burst of gold confetti falls. Two big gold balloons — a "6" and a "0" — float up into frame and settle side by side.
**Text:** None (the balloons carry the "60").
**Sound:** Music starts with a bright strum; light confetti "pop".

### Shot 2 — Dad, moment one (0:04–0:07.5)
**Visual:** **[PHOTO/VIDEO OF DAD 1 — a recent, clear, smiling photo]**
Presented in a soft gold frame with a gentle push-in and subtle parallax. Light confetti drifts in front.
**Sound:** Music builds.

### Shot 3 — Dad, moment two (0:07.5–0:11)
**Visual:** **[PHOTO/VIDEO OF DAD 2 — an older/nostalgic photo, e.g. younger dad or dad with the family]**
Slight warm vintage tint, slow pan across the photo.
**Sound:** Music continues.

### Shot 4 — Dad, moment three (0:11–0:14)
**Visual:** **[PHOTO/VIDEO OF DAD 3 — doing something he loves, or a candid laugh]**
Gentle push-in. A few sparkles drift across the frame edges (never over his face).
**Sound:** Music lifts toward the finish.

### Shot 5 — End card (0:14–0:20)
**Visual:** Close-up of a birthday cake with lit "6" and "0" candles, flames flickering, warm bokeh behind. At 0:15, text fades in over the top. At 0:18, a final burst of gold confetti. Hold to the end.
**Text (overlay, clean gold script or bold serif):**
> Happy 60th Birthday, Dad!

**Optional sign-off line beneath:** *[Love, NAME / From all of us]*
**Sound:** Music lands on a final upbeat chord with the confetti burst.
