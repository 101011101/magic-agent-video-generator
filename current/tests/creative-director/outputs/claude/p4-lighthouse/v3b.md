# The Keeper and the Shell: Operational Chunks

**Recurring entities (referenced below):**
- **Keeper:** woman in her fifties, grey hair loose at the temples, long wool coat, knitted scarf. Same face, hair, coat and scarf colors in every chunk. Soaked in Chunks 7–10.
- **Shell:** spiral shell, pale pearl color, with its own inner blue-gold light that pulses like a heartbeat. Same shape and size everywhere. Glow intensity changes by chunk: soft on the beach, fainter in the cottage, brightening at night, blazing in the lamp room, very faint and tired at the end.
- **Lighthouse:** white tower weathered to bone color, on a spur of black rock above the beach. Lamp room with a great lens in a brass frame. Iron door at the base. Spiral stairs inside.
- **Cottage:** small, at the foot of the tower. Kettle, a single chair, a table with a logbook and a cracked cup, a windowsill facing the sea.
- **Ships:** three, seen only as small lights on the water.
- **Find spot:** a specific spot on the tideline among seaweed and broken driftwood. The same spot is used in Chunks 3 and 11.

---

**Chunk 1: Dawn shore**
- **Duration:** 8 s (0:00–0:08)
- **Visual:** Wide establishing shot. Grey-rose dawn over a cold northern shore. Long slow waves hiss across dark sand. The lighthouse stands on black rock above the beach, its lamp room dim.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Waves, wind. No music yet, or a held breath of silence before the cello.
- **Entities:** Lighthouse.
- **Environment:** Northern beach, dawn. Grey and rose sky, heavy clouds lit from within. Painterly oil look, soft edges.
- **Depends on:** None. Sets the style frame for the whole film.
- **Source:** "Dawn comes in grey and rose over a cold northern shore. The sea is quiet, folding in long slow waves that hiss across dark sand. Above the beach, on a spur of black rock, stands the lighthouse — white paint weathered to the color of bone, its lamp room dim now that the night is over. Everything looks as though it were painted in oils: soft edges, heavy skies, light that seems to come from inside the clouds."

**Chunk 2: The keeper walks**
- **Duration:** 8 s (0:08–0:16)
- **Visual:** The keeper walks the tideline slowly, wrapped in her coat, scarf pulled up against the wind. She is small against the big sea.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Score begins: a single cello, low and patient. Waves and wind under it.
- **Entities:** Keeper. Lighthouse (in background).
- **Environment:** Same beach and dawn as Chunk 1.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 1 (same beach, same light, same lighthouse).
- **Source:** "The keeper walks the tideline as she does every morning. She is in her fifties, wrapped in a long wool coat, a knitted scarf pulled up against the wind, grey hair loose at her temples. She moves slowly, carefully, a person used to being alone. The score begins with a single cello, low and patient."

**Chunk 3: The glowing shell**
- **Duration:** 12 s (0:16–0:28)
- **Visual:** A glow among seaweed and driftwood. She stops. Close-up: the pearl spiral shell half-buried in wet sand, pulsing blue-gold. She kneels and lifts it, and the glow warms her cupped hands. She looks out at the sea for a long moment, then slips the shell into her coat pocket.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Cello continues. Soft wave wash.
- **Entities:** Keeper. Shell (first appearance, establishes its design). Find spot.
- **Environment:** Same beach and dawn. The shell's blue-gold is the only saturated color.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 2 (same keeper, same light).
- **Source:** "Among the seaweed and broken driftwood, something glows. She stops. Half-buried in the wet sand lies a spiral shell, pale as pearl, and inside its curve there is a soft light — not reflected, but its own, a faint blue-gold pulse like a heartbeat. She kneels and lifts it. The glow warms her cupped hands. She looks out at the sea for a long moment, as if asking permission, and then she slips the shell into her coat pocket."

**Chunk 4: Morning in the cottage**
- **Duration:** 10 s (0:28–0:38)
- **Visual:** Plain, small cottage interior: kettle, single chair, table with logbook and cracked cup. She sets the shell on the windowsill, where its glow is fainter. She drinks tea and watches it.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Strings gather into a gentle, uncertain melody. Kettle, cup on table.
- **Entities:** Keeper (coat off or open, same scarf and hair). Shell (dimmer glow). Cottage.
- **Environment:** Cottage interior, morning. Cool window daylight, muted palette.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 3 (same shell, same keeper).
- **Source:** "Inside the keeper's cottage at the foot of the tower, the morning is plain and small: a kettle, a single chair, a table with a logbook and a cracked cup. She sets the shell on the windowsill. Its light is fainter here, away from the water. She watches it while she drinks her tea, and the strings gather into a gentle, uncertain melody."

**Chunk 5: Night, the shell hums**
- **Duration:** 10 s (0:38–0:48)
- **Visual:** Night. Rain hits the window. The lamp beam sweeps over the black sea, its light passing over the cottage. On the sill, the shell's glow rises and falls in long slow waves. The keeper wakes in her chair and turns toward it.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Wind, first rain. The shell's low resonant hum blends into the score.
- **Entities:** Keeper. Shell (brightening). Cottage. Lighthouse beam.
- **Environment:** Cottage interior, night, storm starting. Dark blues, with the sweeping lamp beam and blue-gold shell glow.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 4 (same cottage layout, same windowsill position of the shell).
- **Source:** "Night falls. Wind rises off the sea, and the first rain strikes the window. The great lamp turns above the cottage, its beam sweeping out across the black water. On the windowsill, the shell begins to hum — a low, resonant tone that blends into the score until it is hard to tell which is which. Its glow brightens and dims in long, slow waves. The keeper wakes in her chair and turns toward it."

**Chunk 6: The lamp goes out**
- **Duration:** 10 s (0:48–0:58)
- **Visual:** The beam stutters, flickers twice and goes out. Exterior: the dark tower against a storm-torn sky. Far out on the water, three small ship lights rock in the swell.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Hum and score drop away to storm sound, then tension rises.
- **Entities:** Lighthouse (dark). Ships (three lights).
- **Environment:** Exterior, night, storm. Black and slate palette, no beam.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 5 (same beam, same night).
- **Source:** "Then the beam above her stutters. It flickers once, twice, and goes out. The tower stands dark against a sky now torn by storm. Far out on the water, small lights rock in the swell — ships, three of them, struggling toward a coast they can no longer see."

**Chunk 7: Into the storm**
- **Duration:** 9 s (0:58–1:07)
- **Visual:** She takes the shell from the sill, pulls on her coat and runs out into the rain. Wind tears at her scarf. Waves explode white on the rocks. Lightning flares the sea silver, then black. She pushes open the iron door of the tower.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Storm, thunder. Score builds.
- **Entities:** Keeper (coat on, scarf, getting soaked). Shell (glowing in her hand). Lighthouse (iron door).
- **Environment:** Exterior, night, full storm, lightning flashes.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 5 (cottage), Chunk 6 (dark tower).
- **Source:** "She does not hesitate. She takes the shell from the sill, pulls on her coat, and runs out into the rain. The storm is enormous: wind tearing at her scarf, waves exploding white against the rocks, lightning flaring the whole sea silver for an instant and then plunging it back into black. She pushes open the iron door of the tower…"

**Chunk 8: The climb**
- **Duration:** 9 s (1:07–1:16)
- **Visual:** Spiral stairs, wet and slick, winding up. She climbs. The shell glows brighter with every turn and lights the stairwell blue-gold.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Shell hum rises with the music. Drums enter, deep and urgent, under the strings.
- **Entities:** Keeper (soaked). Shell (growing brighter).
- **Environment:** Tower interior, night. Dark stone or iron, lit mainly by the shell.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 7 (same keeper state, same shell).
- **Source:** "…and climbs. The spiral stairs wind up and up, slick with water, the shell glowing brighter in her hand with every turn, its hum rising with the music — drums now, deep and urgent, under the strings."

**Chunk 9: Relighting the lamp**
- **Duration:** 12 s (1:16–1:28)
- **Visual:** The lamp room is cold and silent, the great lens dead in its brass frame. She holds up the shell. A beat of nothing. Then light pours from the shell's spiral into the lens and floods it with blue-gold fire. The lamp roars back. The beam sweeps out over the storm, broader and warmer than before, cutting a gold path across the waves.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Near-silence for the held breath, then the score surges.
- **Entities:** Keeper. Shell (blazing, then spent). Lighthouse lens and brass frame. The new beam is blue-gold and warmer than the old beam.
- **Environment:** Lamp room, night, storm outside the glass. Shifts from dark to blazing blue-gold.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 8 (same keeper and shell). The beam must look like Chunk 5's beam, but warmer and broader.
- **Source:** "At the top, the lamp room is cold and silent. The great lens sits dead in its brass frame. She holds the shell up to it. For a breath, nothing. Then the shell's light pours out of its spiral and into the glass, flooding the lens with blue-gold fire. The lamp roars back to life. The beam sweeps out over the storm, broader and warmer than it has ever been, cutting a path of gold across the waves."

**Chunk 10: Ships saved**
- **Duration:** 10 s (1:28–1:38)
- **Visual:** At sea, the three ships turn into the beam one by one and slide safely past the point toward the harbor. Intercut with the keeper at the glass, soaked and shaking, watching until the last ship is clear.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Score swells, then softens to a single sustained note.
- **Entities:** Ships (three lights). Keeper. Lighthouse beam.
- **Environment:** Sea at night, storm easing. Gold beam path on dark water.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 6 (same three ships), Chunk 9 (same beam).
- **Source:** "Out at sea, the ships turn. One by one, they find the beam and steer away from the rocks, their small lights sliding safely past the point and on toward the harbor. The keeper stands at the glass, soaked and shaking, watching until the last of them is clear. The score swells, then softens into a single sustained note."

**Chunk 11: Returning the shell**
- **Duration:** 10 s (1:38–1:48)
- **Visual:** Morning. Pale, clean sky. Calm sea. She walks down to the beach with the shell, its glow very faint and tired. She kneels at the find spot and sets it in the wet sand. A small wave wraps around it and pulls back.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Gentle waves. The score is quiet and sparse.
- **Entities:** Keeper (dry coat and scarf). Shell (very faint). Find spot.
- **Environment:** Same beach as Chunks 1–3, morning after the storm. Washed pale palette.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 3 (exact same find spot and framing echo). Chunks 1–2 (same beach).
- **Source:** "Morning again. The storm has passed, and the sky is washed pale and clean. The sea lies calm. The keeper walks down to the beach, the shell in her hands. Its glow is very faint now, tired, as if it gave almost everything it had. She kneels at the same spot on the tideline where she found it and sets it gently in the wet sand. A small wave runs up and wraps around it, then pulls back."

**Chunk 12: Letting go**
- **Duration:** 12 s (1:48–2:00)
- **Visual:** She stays kneeling, then rises and walks back toward the lighthouse without looking back. Behind her, the shell pulses once, softly, and the next wave carries it away. The painted dawn fades to white.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** The cello plays its first melody again, alone. It ends with the fade.
- **Entities:** Keeper. Shell (final pulse). Lighthouse.
- **Environment:** Same beach, pale dawn. Fade to white.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 11 (continuous action). Chunk 2 (the cello melody repeats).
- **Source:** "She stays kneeling for a moment, then rises and walks back toward the lighthouse without looking back. Behind her, the shell's light pulses once, softly, and the next wave carries it away. The cello plays its first melody again, alone, and the painted dawn fades to white."

## Global requirements

- **Total duration:** 2 minutes (120 s). The chunk durations above sum to 120 s.
- **No dialogue:** no spoken words, no narration, no voice of any kind. **Just score** plus natural sound (sea, wind, rain, thunder, the shell's hum blended into the music).
- **Score:** one continuous original score. A solo cello theme opens the film (Chunk 2) and returns alone at the end (Chunk 12). Strings gather in the cottage. Drums enter for the climb. The score swells at the relighting and resolves to a single sustained note.
- **Tone:** melancholy throughout, even in the triumphant beats.
- **Style:** painterly, oil-painting look in every chunk: soft edges, heavy skies, light that seems to come from inside the clouds. Same grade and texture across all chunks.
- **Identity:** the keeper, shell, lighthouse, cottage and three ships stay consistent throughout (see recurring entities). Generate a character sheet for the keeper and a reference image for the shell first, and use them in every chunk.
- **On-screen text:** none (no title card was requested).
- **Aspect ratio:** not specified. Recommend 16:9, or 2.39:1 for a cinematic short-film feel.
- **Light arc:** dawn, then morning, then night storm, then morning again. The blue-gold of the shell is the main color accent throughout.
