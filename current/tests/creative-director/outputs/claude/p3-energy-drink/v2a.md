# Video Script Brief: VOLT Energy Drink Product Ad (6 Shots)

## Goal and core idea
Create a product ad for the user's energy drink, **VOLT**. The ad must make the can the hero of every frame and communicate energy, power and intensity. Core idea: crack open a VOLT and feel a jolt of electric energy. Viewers must leave recognising the can on sight: matte black, neon green lightning bolt, "VOLT" in white capitals.

## Audience and platform
- **Audience:** Assumed 18–34, active and on-the-go: gamers, gym-goers, students, night workers, extreme-sports fans.
- **Platform:** Not specified. Assume social media ads (Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).

## Format, duration and aspect ratio
- **Format:** Product commercial of exactly **6 shots**, cut together into one ad.
- **Duration:** Not specified. Assume ~15 seconds total (about 2–3 seconds per shot).
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (assumed for social). Keep the can and logo within the central safe area so a 1:1 or 16:9 crop also works.

## Tone and style
- High-energy, bold, punchy, premium.
- Visual style: dark, moody backgrounds that let the matte black can and neon green logo pop; neon green accent lighting and electric/lightning effects that echo the logo; crisp, high-contrast product-photography lighting; condensation droplets; fast, dynamic camera moves (push-ins, orbits, speed ramps).
- Audio: driving electronic/bass-heavy track; sound design hits on cuts (can crack, fizz, electric zap).

## User requirements (quoted exactly)
- "product ad for my energy drink"
- "6 shots"
- "the can has to look exactly like my photo"
- "with the logo in every single shot"

## Critical product-consistency rules
- **Exactly 6 shots.** No more, no fewer.
- **The can appears in all 6 shots**, and the **logo is clearly visible in all 6 shots**: the neon green lightning bolt and the word "VOLT" must face the camera or be readable in every shot. No shot may show only the back/side of the can, an extreme close-up that crops out the logo, or a can obscured by hands, liquid or effects.
- The can must match the uploaded photo exactly in every shot:
  - Matte black finish (not glossy, not dark grey, not metallic silver).
  - 355 ml standard slim/regular can proportions as in the photo.
  - Neon green lightning-bolt logo: same shape, size, position and colour.
  - The word "VOLT" in white capital letters: same font, spelling, size and placement. No other words or graphics invented on the can.
- The can must not change design, colour, size or proportions between shots.
- The uploaded photo should be used as the visual reference for every shot (image-to-video or reference-image conditioning), not just the first.
- Avoid hands covering the logo when the can is held; avoid motion blur over the logo at the moment it is on screen.

## Suggested shot structure (for the scriptwriter to refine)
1. **Reveal:** Can emerges from darkness, neon green rim light traces its edges; logo lights up.
2. **Hero detail:** Slow orbit/push-in on the can with condensation; logo and "VOLT" fully legible.
3. **The crack:** Can opened (tab pull), fizz and a burst of green electric sparks; logo facing camera.
4. **Energy:** Can in a high-energy context (gym, gaming setup, skate park, or city at night) held or placed so the logo is visible.
5. **Impact:** Lightning/electric effect strikes or wraps around the can, freeze-frame or speed ramp; logo readable.
6. **Pack shot / end frame:** Can centred, logo forward, on a dark background with neon glow; space for brand name/tagline.

## Uploads and how they must be used
- **Upload:** One product photo — a matte black 355 ml can with a neon green lightning-bolt logo and the word VOLT in white capital letters, on a plain white background.
- **Usage:**
  - This is the single source of truth for the can's appearance and must be used as the reference in all 6 shots.
  - The plain white background is not part of the brand look; place the can into new environments. The can itself must not be altered.
  - If a shot requires a can angle not shown in the photo (e.g. top/tab or back), keep the logo side visible as well, or avoid that angle.

## Assumptions (not stated by the user)
- Total duration ~15 seconds.
- Vertical 9:16 for social.
- Brand name "VOLT" may appear as an on-screen text overlay on the final shot; no other text, claims or tagline unless supplied.
- No health, nutrition, or performance claims (e.g. caffeine content, "boosts focus").
- People, if shown, are adults; faces are secondary to the can.
- Music only, no voiceover.
- No flavour information is shown (flavour unknown).

## Missing information only the user can supply
- Desired total length and where the ad will run (sets aspect ratio and pacing).
- Tagline, slogan, or call to action (e.g. website, "Available at…", promo code).
- Flavour, caffeine content, or any product facts they want shown (and confirmation they are compliant to state).
- Target audience and setting preference (gym, gaming, sport, nightlife, work).
- Photos of the back/side of the can or any other packaging, if other angles are needed.
- Brand guidelines: exact logo files, fonts, and colour values (e.g. hex code for the neon green).
- Music preference or licensed track.
