# "Hello, Love" — Shooting Script

**Runtime:** 5 seconds
**Source:** Your uploaded photo (elderly woman, short white hair, round glasses, green cardigan, armchair, sunlit living room)
**Aspect ratio:** Match the original photo
**Guiding rule:** She must stay recognizably herself. Same face, same glasses, same cardigan, same chair, same room, same light. Only the expression and the hand move.

---

## SCENE 1 — INT. LIVING ROOM — DAY (one continuous shot)

### Shot 1 — Medium shot, static with a very slow push-in

| | |
|---|---|
| **Timing** | 0.0 s – 5.0 s |
| **Shot type** | Medium shot, framed exactly as the uploaded photo (seated, waist up, armchair visible) |
| **Camera movement** | Locked-off for the first second, then a barely perceptible push-in (about 3–5% zoom over the remaining 4 seconds). No pan, no tilt. The frame should feel like the photo coming to life, not a camera moving through the room. |
| **On-screen text** | None |
| **Dialogue / narration** | None |

**Action, beat by beat:**

- **0.0 – 1.0 s — Stillness into life.** The image holds on her neutral expression, exactly as in the photo. Subtle signs of life begin: a slow breath lifts her shoulders slightly, a natural blink at around 0.8 s. Dust motes drift gently in the sunlight.
- **1.0 – 2.2 s — The smile.** Her eyes warm first: a slight crinkle at the corners behind the round glasses. Then the smile spreads, cheeks lifting, lips parting just a little. It should be a soft, genuine, closed-to-slightly-open smile, the kind you give someone you're happy to see. Her head tilts a few degrees to one side.
- **2.2 – 4.2 s — The wave.** Her right hand rises from the armrest or her lap to about shoulder height, palm facing the camera. She waves two or three times with a gentle, unhurried side-to-side motion from the wrist and forearm. The movement is slow and relaxed, as fits her age. Fingers stay natural; the cardigan sleeve moves with her arm.
- **4.2 – 5.0 s — Settle.** Her hand lowers slightly and comes to rest near her chest or back toward her lap. She holds the warm smile, looking straight into the lens. Gentle final blink at around 4.6 s. End on the smile.

**Lighting:** Keep the original sunlight direction and warmth. Allow faint, natural shifts as she moves (light catching her glasses, highlights on her white hair), but no change in time of day or color.

**Sound and music:**
- **Ambience (0.0 – 5.0 s):** Quiet room tone. A distant clock tick and faint birdsong outside the window.
- **Music (0.8 – 5.0 s):** A soft, warm piano or music-box phrase. Three or four notes that rise as she smiles and resolve on the final hold. Low volume, intimate.
- **Foley:** The faint rustle of the cardigan sleeve as her arm lifts (around 2.3 s).
- **Out:** Music note rings out and fades over the last half second.

---

## Continuity and quality checklist

- Face shape, features, and age match the photo in every frame. No smoothing, no de-aging.
- Round glasses stay on, same frame shape, no warping during the head tilt.
- Short white hair keeps its shape and texture.
- Green cardigan keeps its color, texture, and buttons.
- Hand has five fingers and natural proportions throughout the wave.
- Armchair, room, and background objects do not drift, morph, or move.
- Motion is slow and natural. No sudden or exaggerated movements.

**Optional variants if you'd like options:**
- **Silent version:** Ambience only, no music. Feels more like a real home movie.
- **Seamless loop:** End frame returns to the neutral expression from frame one, so it can loop as a living photo.
