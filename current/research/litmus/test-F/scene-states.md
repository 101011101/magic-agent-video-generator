# Scene states (test F)

**Request:** A short film, 2 minutes. A lighthouse keeper finds a glowing shell on the beach at dawn. She brings it home. At night it hums and the lamp goes out. She climbs the tower in a storm and uses the shell to relight it. Ships are saved. Morning again, she puts the shell back on the beach. Melancholy, painterly, no dialogue, just score.

**Style:** Painterly digital animation in pearl-grey, faded blue and muted amber, with brushed textures and soft, diffuse dawn light.

**Assets:**
- @keeper (character): Human woman, 1.68 metres tall, approximately sixty years old, with narrow shoulders, weathered hands, a lined face and grey hair tied back; wearing a heavy charcoal coat, dark trousers and practical boots.
- @shell (prop): Spiral seashell, 6 centimetres long, with an ivory surface, fine amber striations and a slightly chipped lip.
- @beach (location): Intertidal beach, approximately 300 metres long and 30 metres wide, with wet sand, embedded rock shelves, shallow tidal depressions and a rising landward path.
- @lighthouse (location): Lighthouse tower, 24 metres tall and 6 metres across, with salt-stained white masonry, narrow windows and a glass lantern enclosure.
- @cottage (location): Single-storey cottage, 7 metres long and 6 metres wide, attached to the base of the tower, with a landward entrance and a 4-by-3-metre kitchen containing faded blue plaster walls, worn wooden flooring and a shore-facing window.
- @table (prop): Kitchen table, 1.2 metres long, 75 centimetres wide and 74 centimetres high, made of worn dark wood with four square legs.
- @cup (prop): Ceramic drinking cup, 9 centimetres tall and 8 centimetres across, with off-white glaze, a blue rim, one handle and a chip beside the handle.
