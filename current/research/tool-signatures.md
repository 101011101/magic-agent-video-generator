# Tool Signatures

- Machine-readable twin: `current/tests/fixtures/tool-signatures.json`. Source: `magic-hour-tools.json` maintains/mutates. Accessed 2026-10-05.
- `atom ← slot`: the output atom comes from that input slot. `makes true`: the tool sets the atom. `drops`: input atom with no output guarantee; the solver gives no credit.
- `?` = optional slot. Entity atoms apply per entity. "all other atoms" = every in-scope atom not listed elsewhere on the line.
- Atoms the prompt names win over pass-through for AI Image Editor and AI Video Editor.

| Atom | Scope | Definition |
| :- | :- | :- |
| identity | entity | Who the entity is: face and recognizable likeness (for a product or logo: its brand identity). |
| head_hair | entity | Hair and head shape outside the face. |
| body | entity | Body build, skin and proportions. |
| wardrobe | entity | Clothing and accessories the entity wears. |
| expression | entity | Facial expression and eye gaze. |
| pose_action | entity | Body and head pose, and the motion or action over time. |
| speech_sync | entity | Mouth motion matches the speech_audio track. |
| voice | entity | Voice timbre and speaking manner of the entity. |
| background | scene | Setting and environment behind the entities. |
| lighting | scene | Light direction, quality and colour on the scene. |
| props | scene | Objects in the scene that are not entities or wardrobe. |
| camera | scene | Framing, shot size, angle and camera motion. |
| style | scene | Visual rendering: art style, palette, colour grade. |
| text | layer | On-screen text and graphic overlays: captions, titles, logos. |
| speech_audio | layer | Spoken-word audio: words, language, timing. |
| music | layer | Music track. |
| sfx | layer | Sound effects and ambient sound. |
| media_type | format | Output media: image, video, audio or data. |
| duration | format | Length in seconds. |
| aspect | format | Aspect ratio. |
| resolution | format | Pixel dimensions. |
| frame_rate | format | Frames per second. |

**Video output**

- **Image-to-Video** [mixed]: image_file_path: all image atoms; end_image_file_path?: all image atoms; style.prompt?: pose_action, expression, camera → video (identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, background, lighting, props, style ← image_file_path (t=0; end_image_file_path at t=end when given); aspect ← image_file_path; makes true: media_type, pose_action, expression, camera, duration, resolution; drops: image_file_path.text, end_image_file_path.text)
  - Nature: Generates motion that starts from a still frame and fills the time to an optional end frame, guided by a natural-language prompt. The prompt can steer subject action, expression, camera moves (pan, zoom, orbit) and environmental effects (wind, weather, light changes); it can likely animate an element that appears, fades or moves within the frame, with approximate timing only. It cannot keep exact on-screen text or pixel-exact logos, gives no alpha or transparency, gives no frame-exact timing, and the subject's identity can drift in later frames.
- **Text-to-Video** [mixed]: style.prompt: identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style, text; references?: identity → video (identity ← references (inferred; field undocumented; when given); makes true: media_type, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style, duration, aspect, resolution; no guarantee: text, speech_sync, voice, speech_audio, music, sfx, frame_rate)
  - Nature: Generates a complete new video clip from a natural-language prompt, with no prior image or video. The prompt can steer any visual content: entities, action, setting, lighting, weather, camera moves, transitions within the shot and art style; optional reference images may set subject identity (field semantics undocumented). It cannot reproduce a specific real person or brand without a reference, does not keep characters consistent across separate calls, renders text and logos unreliably, and gives no alpha or frame-exact timing; audio=true gives uncontrolled audio.
- **AI Talking Photo** [mixed]: image_file_path: all image atoms; audio_file_path: all audio atoms; style.prompt?: background, camera, pose_action → video (all other atoms ← image_file_path; voice, speech_audio, music, sfx ← audio_file_path; duration ← audio_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type, speech_sync, expression, pose_action; drops: image_file_path.text, image_file_path.resolution)
  - Nature: Animates a single portrait photo so the face speaks a supplied audio track, moving lips, head and expression in sync with the audio. In prompted mode a prompt can also change background, camera and gesture, at slightly lower likeness; realistic mode keeps the photo's scene and framing. It needs a clear frontal face, accepts no text script (speech must exist as audio first), does little body or scene motion in realistic mode, and caps output at 720p.
- **Lip Sync** [mixed]: video_file_path: all video atoms; audio_file_path: all audio atoms → video (all other atoms ← video_file_path; voice, speech_audio, music, sfx ← audio_file_path; duration ← video_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type, speech_sync)
  - Nature: Re-animates only the mouth region of a person in an existing video so the lips match a new audio track; everything else in the frames stays. It has no prompt; settings only pick the time range, frame-rate cap and quality tier. It cannot change the voice, words, expression beyond the mouth, or any visual content, and quality drops with profile faces, occlusion, extreme head motion, several speakers or audio and video of different lengths.
- **Face Swap Video** [mixed]: video_file_path: all video atoms; image_file_path?: identity; face_mappings[].new_face?: identity → video (identity ← image_file_path (all-faces) | face_mappings[].new_face (individual-faces, per entity); all other atoms ← video_file_path; duration ← video_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type)
  - Nature: Replaces the face region of one or more people in an existing video with a face from a reference image, keeping the original expression, head motion and lighting. It has no prompt; settings pick all-faces or per-face mapping and the model version. It does not change hair, head shape, body, wardrobe, voice or scene, and quality drops with small faces, occlusion or extreme head motion; v1 keeps identity weakly.
- **Video-to-Video** [mixed]: video_file_path: all video atoms; style.prompt?: style → video (all other atoms ← video_file_path; duration ← video_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type, style, frame_rate; drops: video_file_path.voice, video_file_path.text, video_file_path.speech_audio, video_file_path.music, video_file_path.sfx, video_file_path.resolution)
  - Nature: Re-renders every frame of an existing video in a new visual style (preset art style plus optional prompt), keeping the original motion, structure, timing and camera. The style and prompt can steer look, palette, colour grade and rendering medium (anime, oil paint, 3D, cyberpunk). It does not add, remove or move content, can break on-screen text and drift identity detail, degrades with fast motion or unstable lighting, and halves frame rate by default; audio pass-through is undocumented.
- **AI Video Editor** [mixed]: video_file_path: all video atoms; style.prompt: props, background, style, wardrobe, lighting → video (all other atoms ← video_file_path; duration ← video_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type, props, background, style, resolution; drops: video_file_path.voice, video_file_path.text, video_file_path.speech_audio, video_file_path.music, video_file_path.sfx, video_file_path.frame_rate)
  - Nature: Edits an existing video clip from a natural-language instruction, re-generating the regions or properties the prompt names and carrying the rest from the source. The prompt can add, remove or replace objects, recolour, change wardrobe, background, lighting, weather or style; it can likely handle other local visual changes a prompt can describe. It is weak with several unrelated edits in one call and on fast-moving details, does not guarantee text or audio pass-through, gives no precise timing, and limits clip length per model (3-45 s).
- **AI Video Translator** [inferred]: video_file_path: all video atoms → video (all other atoms ← video_file_path; duration ← video_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type, speech_audio, speech_sync, resolution; drops: video_file_path.music, video_file_path.sfx, video_file_path.frame_rate)
  - Nature: Translates the speech in an existing video into a target language and replaces the spoken audio (documented); keeping the visuals, voice timbre and lip re-sync is inferred and unverified. Its only steering setting is the target language. It does not translate on-screen text, does not change any visual content, and accepts clips of 1-30 s only.
- **Animation** [mixed]: style.prompt?: background, props, pose_action, lighting; style.art_style_custom?: style; audio_file_path?: all audio atoms; image_file_path?: all image atoms → video (voice, speech_audio, music, sfx ← audio_file_path; makes true: media_type, background, props, pose_action, lighting, style, camera, duration, aspect, resolution, frame_rate; drops: image_file_path.identity, image_file_path.head_hair, image_file_path.body, image_file_path.wardrobe, image_file_path.expression, image_file_path.text)
  - Nature: Generates a stylized morphing animation frame by frame from a prompt or audio, with an optional start image as the first frame only. Presets steer art style and camera effect (zoom, pan, tilt, audio-synced moves), the prompt steers scene content, and transition speed sets how fast content morphs. It does not give photoreal continuous motion, does not keep a subject's identity across the clip, and cannot render exact text or logos.
- **Audio-to-Video** [mixed]: audio_file_path: all audio atoms; image_file_path?: all image atoms; style.prompt?: background, props, style, pose_action, camera → video (identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, lighting, aspect ← image_file_path (initial frame; when given); voice, speech_audio, music, sfx ← audio_file_path; duration ← audio_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type, background, props, style, pose_action, camera, expression, resolution; drops: image_file_path.text)
  - Nature: Generates video whose visuals and timing follow an audio track, with an optional reference image as the initial frame and an optional prompt for scene and style. The prompt can steer setting, props, style, action and camera. Lip sync to speech is not documented, identity is guaranteed only at the first frame, a prompt that conflicts with the reference image degrades output, and audio pass-through is unverified.
- **Character Replace** [mixed]: video_file_path: all video atoms; image_file_path: all image atoms → video (identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe ← image_file_path; all other atoms ← video_file_path; duration ← video_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type, resolution; drops: video_file_path.voice, video_file_path.text, video_file_path.speech_audio, video_file_path.music, video_file_path.sfx, video_file_path.frame_rate, image_file_path.text)
  - Nature: Replaces the main person in an existing video with a character from a reference image, keeping the scene, background and motion (replace mode); animate mode instead transfers the video's motion onto the reference character. Settings pick the mode, model and which person (point selection, wan-animate only). It has no prompt for scene or style changes, breaks under occlusion, needs point selection when several people appear, limits kling-3.0 clips to 3-10 s (replace), and does not document audio pass-through.
- **Auto Subtitle Generator** [mixed]: video_file_path: all video atoms → video (all other atoms ← video_file_path; duration ← video_file_path [start_seconds, end_seconds]; makes true: media_type, text)
  - Nature: Transcribes the speech in an existing video and burns styled captions into the frames; all other content passes through. Templates or a custom config steer font, size, colours, stroke and position. It cannot write arbitrary text unrelated to the speech, has no language setting, gives no SRT/VTT file, and loses accuracy with noise, music or several speakers.
- **AI GIF Generator** [mixed]: style.prompt: identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style, text → video (makes true: media_type, identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style; no guarantee: text, speech_sync, voice, speech_audio, music, sfx, duration, aspect, resolution, frame_rate)
  - Nature: Generates a short new animation (gif, mp4 or webm) from a prompt of up to 500 characters, with no input image. The prompt can steer any visual content and style of the loop. It has no duration, aspect or resolution control, no audio, cannot use a reference for identity, and renders text unreliably.

**Image output**

- **AI Image Generator** [mixed]: style.prompt: identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style, text → image (makes true: media_type, identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style, aspect, resolution; no guarantee: text)
  - Nature: Generates a complete new still image from a natural-language prompt, with no input image. The prompt can steer any visual content, composition, lighting and style, and tool presets target logos, posters and thumbnails. It cannot reproduce a specific real person or exact brand asset, does not keep identity consistent across calls, renders exact text unreliably, and gives no transparency.
- **AI Image Editor** [mixed]: image_file_paths[0]: all image atoms; image_file_paths[1..]?: identity, wardrobe, props, background, style; style.prompt: wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style → image (identity, head_hair, body ← image_file_paths[0] (or image_file_paths[n] when the prompt says so); text ← image_file_paths[0]; makes true: media_type, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style, aspect, resolution)
  - Nature: Edits one input image, or combines up to 10, from a natural-language instruction; it re-generates what the prompt names and carries the rest from the first image. The prompt can change wardrobe, expression, pose, background, lighting, props, camera framing and style, and can take elements from extra reference images. It is weak with several edits in one call, can drift a person's identity, does not reliably write or keep exact text or pixel-exact logos, and gives no transparency.
- **Face Swap Photo** [mixed]: target_file_path: all image atoms; source_file_path?: identity; face_mappings[].new_face?: identity → image (identity ← source_file_path (all-faces) | face_mappings[].new_face (per entity); all other atoms ← target_file_path; makes true: media_type; drops: target_file_path.resolution)
  - Nature: Replaces the face region of one or more people in a target image with a face from a source image, keeping expression, lighting, pose and scene. It has no prompt; settings pick all-faces or per-face mapping. It does not change hair, head shape, body or wardrobe, fails when no source face is found, and degrades with small or obscured faces, extreme angles or lighting mismatch.
- **Head Swap** [mixed]: body_file_path: all image atoms; head_file_path: identity, head_hair, expression → image (identity, head_hair, expression ← head_file_path; all other atoms ← body_file_path; makes true: media_type, resolution)
  - Nature: Places the whole head (face, hair, head shape) from one image onto the body in another image, keeping the body, pose and scene. It has no prompt; one setting caps resolution. It does not change body, wardrobe or scene, and degrades with angle, lighting or proportion mismatch, hair, hands or hats that cover the head, and cropped heads.
- **Body Swap** [mixed]: person_file_path: identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action; scene_file_path: all image atoms → image (identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression ← person_file_path; background, lighting, props, camera, style, text, aspect ← scene_file_path; makes true: media_type, pose_action, resolution)
  - Nature: Places the person from one image into a scene image, replacing the scene's person and keeping the scene's background, composition and lighting. It has no prompt; only resolution is set. The pose may adapt to the scene without control, and it degrades with occlusion, pose, angle or lighting mismatch and low-quality inputs.
- **AI Clothes Changer** [mixed]: person_file_path: all image atoms; garment_file_path: wardrobe → image (wardrobe ← garment_file_path (region per garment_type; other regions from person); all other atoms ← person_file_path; makes true: media_type; drops: person_file_path.resolution)
  - Nature: Dresses the person in an image with a garment from a reference image (virtual try-on), changing only the selected clothing region. A setting picks the region: full outfit, upper body or lower body. It cannot design clothing from text, does not change pose, face, lighting or background, and degrades when arms cover the clothing, lighting is poor or the background is busy.
- **AI Headshot Generator** [mixed]: image_file_path: all image atoms; style.prompt?: background, wardrobe, lighting, camera, style → image (identity ← image_file_path; makes true: media_type, background, wardrobe, lighting, camera, style; drops: image_file_path.head_hair, image_file_path.body, image_file_path.expression, image_file_path.pose_action, image_file_path.props, image_file_path.text, image_file_path.aspect, image_file_path.resolution)
  - Nature: Re-generates a photo of one person as a professional headshot, keeping facial identity and replacing background, attire, lighting and framing. An optional prompt steers background, attire, lighting and style. It needs exactly one detectable face, does not keep hair, expression, pose or props from the input, and degrades with sunglasses, masks or profiles.
- **AI Face Editor** [mixed]: image_file_path: all image atoms → image (all other atoms ← image_file_path; makes true: media_type, expression, pose_action; drops: image_file_path.resolution)
  - Nature: Adjusts the face in an image with numeric sliders for expression, eye gaze, mouth shape and head pose, keeping identity, lighting and scene. It has no prompt; each slider moves one facial feature by a set amount. It cannot change anything outside the face and head pose, and large slider values look unnatural.
- **AI Image Upscaler** [mixed]: image_file_path: all image atoms; style.prompt?: style → image (all other atoms ← image_file_path; makes true: media_type, resolution)
  - Nature: Increases the resolution of an image 2x or 4x and recovers detail, keeping content, composition and aspect. Mode steers how much detail it invents (preserve, balanced, creative); creative mode accepts a prompt and can reinterpret detail and style. It cannot recover detail that is not there, amplifies JPEG artifacts and is weak on text-heavy images.
- **Image Background Remover** [mixed]: image_file_path: all image atoms; background_image_file_path?: background → image (all other atoms ← image_file_path; background ← background_image_file_path (when given; else transparent alpha); makes true: media_type; drops: image_file_path.props, image_file_path.text)
  - Nature: Cuts out the main subject of an image and outputs it with a transparent alpha background, or composites it onto a supplied background image. It has no prompt. It does not relight the subject to match a new background, loses props and text in the old background, and degrades with fine hair on busy backgrounds, transparent objects and low subject-background contrast.
- **Photo Colorizer** [mixed]: image_file_path: all image atoms → image (all other atoms ← image_file_path; makes true: media_type, style)
  - Nature: Adds realistic colour to a black-and-white or grayscale photo, keeping content and structure. It has no prompt or settings, so the palette is the model's choice. It cannot apply a chosen colour grade or style, and results vary on complex scenes and damaged scans.
- **AI Meme Generator** [mixed]: style.topic: text → image (makes true: media_type, text, identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, background, lighting, props, camera, style, aspect, resolution)
  - Nature: Writes panel text for a topic onto a fixed meme template image. The topic steers the text; a setting picks the template. It cannot use the user's own images or entities, cannot change the template's visuals, and gives weak text for vague topics.
- **AI QR Code Generator** [mixed]: content: text; style.art_style: style, background → image (makes true: media_type, text, style, background; no guarantee: identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, lighting, props, camera, aspect, resolution)
  - Nature: Generates an artistic QR code image that encodes a given URL or text, rendered in a chosen preset or free-text art style. The style prompt steers the look and background. Scannability is a goal, not a guarantee (busy or low-contrast styles can break it), and it does not place readable text, logos or entities.

**Audio output**

- **AI Voice Generator** [documented]: style.prompt: speech_audio → audio (makes true: media_type, speech_audio, voice; no guarantee: music, sfx, duration)
  - Nature: Converts a text script into speech in one of 1266 preset voices. The script sets the words and the voice preset sets timbre and manner. It cannot clone a real person's voice, has no language setting, makes no music or sound effects, and its duration follows the script length (max 1000 characters).
- **AI Voice Cloner** [documented]: audio_file_path: all audio atoms; style.prompt: speech_audio → audio (voice ← audio_file_path; makes true: media_type, speech_audio; drops: audio_file_path.music, audio_file_path.sfx, audio_file_path.duration)
  - Nature: Speaks a new text script in the voice cloned from an audio sample, keeping the sample's timbre, intonation and speaking patterns. The script sets the words. It needs one clean speaker of at least 3 s, degrades with noisy or multi-speaker samples, has no language setting, and makes no music or sound effects.

**Data output**

- **Face Detection** [inferred]: target_file_path: all image atoms → data (identity ← target_file_path (one face crop per detected entity))
  - Nature: Analyses an image or video and returns a list of the faces it finds as crops. A confidence threshold steers how many faces it keeps. It changes no media; its only use is to feed per-face mappings to Face Swap Video and Face Swap Photo.
- **Generate Asset Upload URLs** [inferred]: none → data (no atom change)
  - Nature: Returns upload URLs and file paths so local files can be used as inputs to other tools. It changes no content; the uploaded file keeps all its properties. Uploads expire after 7 days.

**Not offered by Magic Hour**

- **music_score** (unbound) [inferred]: brief: music → audio (makes true: media_type, music, duration; no guarantee: voice, speech_audio, sfx)
  - Nature: Intended: generates a music track from a text brief; no provider is bound yet. The brief and settings are intended to steer mood, tempo and duration. Intended to make music only: no speech, voice or sound effects, and no guarantee of beat-exact sync to picture.
- **sound_effects** (unbound) [inferred]: brief: sfx → audio (makes true: media_type, sfx, duration; no guarantee: voice, speech_audio, music)
  - Nature: Intended: generates sound effects or ambient sound from a text brief, with cue times; no provider is bound yet. The brief is intended to steer the kind of sound and the cue times its placement. Intended to make sound effects only: no speech or music.
- **text_overlay** (ours, deterministic) [inferred]: base: all video atoms; text: text → video|image (all other atoms ← base; text ← base (existing) + text slot (added))
  - Nature: Intended: deterministically draws given text onto an image or video (e.g. ffmpeg drawtext), with no generative model. Settings set font, size, colour, position and start and end times exactly, so text is pixel-exact and timing is frame-exact. It does not change any other content and cannot make text interact with the scene (no relighting, occlusion or 3D tracking).
- **logo_overlay** (ours, deterministic) [inferred]: base: all video atoms; logo: text → video|image (all other atoms ← base; text ← base (existing) + logo (added))
  - Nature: Intended: deterministically composites a supplied logo image onto an image or video (e.g. ffmpeg overlay), with no generative model. Settings set position, scale and start and end times exactly, so the logo stays pixel-exact. It does not change any other content and cannot animate the logo beyond placement and timing unless combined with fade or transform.
- **fade** (ours, deterministic) [inferred]: base: all video atoms → video|image (all other atoms ← base)
  - Nature: Intended: deterministically changes the opacity of a whole image or video, or of one overlay layer, over a time range (fade in, fade out, fade to black). Settings set target layer, curve and start and end times exactly. It does not change content; it cannot fade one element inside the generated pixels, only a whole frame or a separate layer.
- **transform** (ours, deterministic) [inferred]: base: all video atoms → video|image (all other atoms ← base; makes true: camera)
  - Nature: Intended: deterministically translates, scales or rotates the whole frame or one overlay layer, optionally over time (digital zoom, pan, Ken Burns move). Settings set the values exactly. It makes no new pixels: a zoom-in loses resolution, it cannot reveal content outside the frame, and it gives no parallax or true 3D camera motion.
- **crop_reformat** (ours, deterministic) [inferred]: base: all video atoms → video|image (all other atoms ← base; makes true: camera, aspect, resolution)
  - Nature: Intended: deterministically crops or pads a frame to a target aspect and resolution. Settings set the crop box, target aspect, target resolution and padding exactly. It makes no new pixels: cropping can cut off text or entities at the edges, and padding adds plain borders, not new scene content.
- **trim** (ours, deterministic) [inferred]: base: all video atoms → video|image (all other atoms ← base; makes true: duration)
  - Nature: Intended: deterministically cuts a video to a start and end time, keeping the content of the kept range. Settings set the times exactly. It does not change content, speed or order, and cannot extend a clip beyond its source length.
- **stitch** (ours, deterministic) [inferred]: segments[i]: all video atoms → video (all other atoms ← segments[i] over its time range [t_i, t_i + duration_i]; media_type ← segments[*]; makes true: duration)
  - Nature: Intended: deterministically joins video segments in time, in a set order, with optional transitions; each segment keeps its own content in its time range. Settings set order and transition type. It does not change segment content, needs segments that share aspect, resolution and frame rate (or a crop_reformat first), and cannot make a seamless continuous shot from unrelated segments.
- **audio_mix_mux** (ours, deterministic) [inferred]: video: identity, head_hair, body, wardrobe, expression, pose_action, speech_sync, background, lighting, props, camera, style, text, media_type, duration, aspect, resolution, frame_rate; speech?: voice, speech_audio; music?: music; sfx?: sfx → video (all other atoms ← video; voice, speech_audio ← speech; music ← music; sfx ← sfx)
  - Nature: Intended: deterministically mixes speech, music and sound-effect tracks and attaches them to a video, keeping all visuals. Settings set gain, ducking and offset exactly. It makes no new audio or visuals, and it does not create lip sync: speech matches the mouth only when the speech track is the audio the video was lip-synced to.

**Join tools**

- stitch joins segments in time: each segment keeps its atoms in its own time range.
- audio_mix_mux joins layers: visual atoms ← video, voice and speech_audio ← speech, music ← music, sfx ← sfx.
- The solver splits a goal into per-segment or per-layer sub-goals and joins the results with these tools.

**Open gaps**

- No atom covers opacity or layer visibility over time (fade).
- Text-to-Video `references` semantics are undocumented.
- AI Video Translator: only the language change is documented; the rest is inferred.
- Audio pass-through is undocumented for Video-to-Video, AI Video Editor and Character Replace; mux the source audio back.
