I ACCEPT HER

1. EXT. THE EXECUTIVE MANSION IN WASHINGTON — DAY

The Executive Mansion stands beyond the two men approaching it.
A title appears: “Washington — March 17, 1862.”
Secretary of War Edwin M. Stanton escorts Cornelius Vanderbilt away from us toward the entrance.

2. INT. THE EXECUTIVE MANSION IN WASHINGTON — CONTINUOUS

Stanton leads Vanderbilt through the doorway at left toward Abraham Lincoln at right.
A caption appears: “Their first personal meeting — as recalled by Vanderbilt, May 3, 1866.”
Lincoln and Vanderbilt shake hands.
                    ABRAHAM LINCOLN
          Can your steamships prevent the Confederate Merrimac from leaving Hampton Roads?
                    CORNELIUS VANDERBILT
          Properly manned, my steamship Vanderbilt could deter the ironclad. Or I would give ten-to-one odds that she could destroy her.
                    ABRAHAM LINCOLN
          Name your price for undertaking the service.
                    CORNELIUS VANDERBILT
          I will not profit from the government’s emergency. I will not name a charter price. I offer the ship outright, as a gift.
                    ABRAHAM LINCOLN
          I accept her.
