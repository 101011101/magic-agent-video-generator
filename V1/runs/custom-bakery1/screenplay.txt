THE CORNER OVEN

1. EXT. QUIET NEIGHBOURHOOD STREET — NIGHT

A quiet neighbourhood street lies beneath a blue, sunless sky.
One shop glows on the right side of the street.
Through its front window, baker stands behind a wooden workbench, wearing a flour-dusted apron.
Baker sets a ball of dough on the workbench nearest us.

2. INT. SHOP — CONTINUOUS   (CUT)

Baker folds the dough over itself.
Baker presses the dough away from us with both palms.
Baker shapes the dough into a loaf.
Beside the flour bowl, plump croissants sit on a baking tray.
Baker opens the oven to the left of the workbench.
Baker lifts the loaf from the workbench.
Baker moves left and slides the loaf into the oven.
Baker lifts the pastry tray from beside the flour bowl.
Baker slides the tray into the oven, away from us.
Baker closes the oven door.

3. INT. SHOP — LATER   (CUT)

The front window brightens.
The oven door opens toward us.
A cloud of steam rolls out through the opening.
Wearing thick mitts, baker draws out a golden loaf and a tray of deeply browned croissants.
Baker sets the croissant tray on the wooden workbench to the right.
Baker lowers the loaf onto a wire cooling rack beside the tray.
The crust crackles against the quiet of the shop.

4. INT. BLUE FRONT DOOR — CONTINUOUS   (CUT)

Baker turns the sign on the blue front door to OPEN.
Neighbour steps through the doorway toward us, carrying a canvas shopping bag.
Parent follows neighbour inside, holding child’s hand.
Child looks past baker toward the croissants on the workbench.
Child smiles.

5. INT. COUNTER — CONTINUOUS   (CUT)

Neighbour, parent and child stand across the counter from baker.
Baker lifts a croissant from the tray on the workbench behind the counter.
Baker slips the croissant into a paper bag on the counter.
Baker passes the bag across the counter to neighbour on the left.
Baker lifts the loaf from the cooling rack.
Baker folds paper around the loaf on the counter.
Parent receives the wrapped loaf from baker on the right.
Baker’s shoulders droop beneath the apron straps.
Neighbour smiles at baker.
Parent smiles at baker over the wrapped loaf.
Baker lifts their head, eyes opening wider as a smile spreads across their face.

6. EXT. QUIET NEIGHBOURHOOD STREET — DAY   (CUT)

Sunlight reaches the shop sign: “The Corner Oven.”
Beneath the name, words appear: “Come in. Your morning starts here.”
