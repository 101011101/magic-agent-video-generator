I ACCEPT HER

1. EXT. EXECUTIVE MANSION IN WASHINGTON — DAY   (CUT)

On screen: “WASHINGTON — MARCH 17, 1862.”
Secretary of War Edwin M. Stanton escorts Cornelius Vanderbilt away from us toward the Executive Mansion.
They pass through the entrance.

2. INT. EXECUTIVE MANSION IN WASHINGTON — CONTINUOUS

Secretary of War Edwin M. Stanton leads Cornelius Vanderbilt toward Abraham Lincoln, standing at the right.
On screen: “Their first personal meeting — as Cornelius Vanderbilt recalled on May 3, 1866.”
Cornelius Vanderbilt stops opposite Abraham Lincoln.
                    ABRAHAM LINCOLN
          Can your steamships prevent the Confederate Merrimac from leaving Hampton Roads?
                    CORNELIUS VANDERBILT
          My steamship Vanderbilt, properly manned, could deter the ironclad—or have ten-to-one odds of destroying her.
                    ABRAHAM LINCOLN
          Name your price for undertaking the service.
                    CORNELIUS VANDERBILT
          I will not profit from the government’s emergency. Instead of naming a charter price, I offer the ship outright as a gift.
                    ABRAHAM LINCOLN
          I accept her.
