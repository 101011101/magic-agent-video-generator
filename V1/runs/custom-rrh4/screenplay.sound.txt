1. EXT. FOREST PATH — DAY
   [AMBIENCE: Birdsong and a light breeze rustling the trees.]
   [MUSIC: Warm flute and plucked strings introduce a gentle woodland theme.]
Little Red Riding Hood’s mother stands beside the forest path with a wicker basket on her arm.
She tucks bread into the basket.   [SFX: Bread wrapping rustles against wicker]
She nestles a small cake beside the bread.
            LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD’S MOTHER: Take these to Grandmother’s cottage, where she keeps her quilt and nightcap on her bed and a needle and thread in her bedside sewing basket, and stay on the forest path, where daisies grow along the edges.
Little Red Riding Hood ties her scarlet hood beneath her chin.
Little Red Riding Hood’s mother passes her the basket.   [SFX: Soft creak of the wicker basket changing hands]
Little Red Riding Hood walks away from us along the forest path.   [SFX: Light footsteps along the earthen path]
Daisies line both edges of the path.

2. EXT. UNDER AN OAK TREE — CONTINUOUS
   [AMBIENCE: Birdsong and wind stirring the oak leaves.]
   [MUSIC: Playful woodwinds give way to sly, low pizzicato strings.]
Little Red Riding Hood walks toward an oak tree beside the path.   [SFX: Light footsteps on packed earth]
A wolf steps from behind the oak onto the path, blocking her way.   [SFX: Heavy pawsteps onto the path]
            WOLF: Where are you going?
            LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD: To Grandmother’s cottage beside the three birch trees.
            WOLF: Why not bring her flowers?
Little Red Riding Hood kneels beside the daisies on the right.   [SFX: Soft rustle of cloak against grass]
She plucks daisies from the edge of the path.   [SFX: Small snaps of daisy stems]
The wolf races away from us along the path while she keeps picking.   [SFX: Rapid pawsteps receding along the path]
Little Red Riding Hood tucks the daisies into her basket.   [SFX: Flower stems brush the wicker basket]

3. EXT. THE COTTAGE’S GREEN FRONT DOOR — DAY
   [AMBIENCE: Birch leaves rustling with scattered birdsong.]
   [MUSIC: Low bassoon and muted strings follow the wolf's hurried approach.]
Grandmother’s cottage stands beside three birch trees.
The wolf runs toward the cottage’s green front door, away from us.   [SFX: Fast pawsteps approaching the door]
He knocks on the door.   [SFX: Knocks on the wooden door]
He calls through the door in a sweet voice.
            WOLF: It’s Little Red Riding Hood!

4. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — CONTINUOUS
   [AMBIENCE: Quiet room tone with faint birdsong outside.]
   [MUSIC: Tense low strings swell into a brief dark flourish, then settle into stealthy pizzicato.]
Grandmother lies beneath a quilt in the bed on the right.
A nightcap rests beside her pillow.
A sewing basket stands beside the bed.
            GRANDMOTHER: Lift the latch.
The latch rises on the green door at the back of the room.   [SFX: Metal latch clicks upward]
The door swings inward.   [SFX: Wooden door creaks open]
The wolf rushes through the doorway toward the bed on the right.   [SFX: Rapid heavy pawsteps across floorboards]
He opens his jaws wide over Grandmother.
Grandmother disappears whole into his mouth.   [SFX: Quick cartoonish wet whoosh]
The wolf swallows.   [SFX: Deep gulp]
He climbs onto the bed.   [SFX: Bed frame creaks under the wolf's weight]
He picks up the nightcap from beside the pillow.
He pulls the nightcap onto his head.
He draws the quilt over himself.   [SFX: Quilt swishes over the bed]
The wolf lies still with his eyes on the green door.

5. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — LATER
   [AMBIENCE: Quiet room tone with faint birdsong outside.]
   [MUSIC: Sparse celesta and hushed strings build to a sharp orchestral sting, then fade out.]
The green door swings inward beneath Little Red Riding Hood’s hand.   [SFX: Wooden door creaks inward]
Little Red Riding Hood enters toward us, carrying her wicker basket.   [SFX: Light footsteps on floorboards]
She places the basket on the wooden table to the left.   [SFX: Wicker basket taps onto the wooden table]
She crosses right toward the bed.   [SFX: Footsteps cross the floorboards]
The wolf’s large ears stick out from under the nightcap.
            LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD: Grandmother, what big ears you have!
            WOLF: All the better to hear you with.
The wolf’s large eyes follow her.
            LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD: What big eyes you have!
            WOLF: All the better to see you with.
Little Red Riding Hood grips her cloak.   [SFX: Cloak fabric tightens with a soft rustle]
            LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD: What big teeth you have!
            WOLF: All the better to eat you with!
The wolf springs up from beneath the quilt.   [SFX: Quilt flaps and bed frame jolts]
Little Red Riding Hood screams.
The wolf opens his jaws over her.
Little Red Riding Hood disappears whole into his mouth.   [SFX: Quick cartoonish wet whoosh]
The wolf swallows.   [SFX: Deep gulp]
He slumps back onto the bed.   [SFX: Heavy flop and a long bed creak]
His eyes close.
Loud snores rumble from his open mouth.   [SFX: Loud, rumbling snores]

6. EXT. OUTSIDE THE COTTAGE — DAY
   [AMBIENCE: Birdsong and wind in the birch leaves.]
A woodcutter walks from left to right past the cottage, shears hanging from his belt.   [SFX: Steady footsteps and shears jingling at his belt]
The wolf’s snores carry through the green doorway.   [SFX: Muffled rumbling snores through the doorway]
The woodcutter stops.   [SFX: Footsteps halt with a scrape of earth]
He turns toward the green door.
He steps through the doorway, away from us.   [SFX: Footsteps cross the wooden threshold]

7. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — CONTINUOUS
   [AMBIENCE: Low, steady wolf snores with faint birdsong outside.]
   [MUSIC: Delicate pizzicato strings and soft flute carry cautious suspense into relief.]
The woodcutter approaches the bed on the right.   [SFX: Measured footsteps on floorboards]
His gaze settles on the wolf’s swollen belly.
He draws the quilt down from the belly.   [SFX: Quilt slides down with a soft swish]
He takes the shears from his belt.   [SFX: Metal shears clink against the belt]
He makes small, careful snips along the wolf’s belly.   [SFX: Small, careful snips of shears]
The cut parts into an opening.
Little Red Riding Hood scrambles through the opening onto the quilt.   [SFX: Quick scrabbling and quilt rustle]
She reaches back into the opening.
Grandmother takes her hands.
Little Red Riding Hood pulls as Grandmother climbs free onto the bed.   [SFX: Bed frame creaks as Grandmother climbs free]
Little Red Riding Hood and Grandmother tremble.
They hug.
The woodcutter returns the shears to his belt.   [SFX: Metal shears clink back onto the belt]

8. EXT. OUTSIDE THE COTTAGE — DAY
   [AMBIENCE: Birdsong and a breeze rustling the birches.]
   [MUSIC: Quiet, purposeful pizzicato strings and bassoon accompany the gathering.]
The woodcutter gathers stones from the ground into his arms.   [SFX: Stones scrape together and clack in his arms]
Little Red Riding Hood gathers stones in the lifted hem of her cloak.   [SFX: Stones knock softly inside gathered cloak fabric]
Grandmother gathers stones against her chest.   [SFX: Stones scrape and clack together]
They carry the stones toward the green front door on the right.   [SFX: Three sets of footsteps with shifting stone clacks]

9. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — CONTINUOUS
   [AMBIENCE: Quiet room tone with faint birdsong outside.]
   [MUSIC: Hushed plucked strings keep a careful, measured rhythm.]
The woodcutter carries his stones through the green door toward the bed.   [SFX: Heavy footsteps on floorboards and stones clacking]
Little Red Riding Hood follows with stones cradled in her cloak.   [SFX: Light footsteps and stones shifting in cloth]
Grandmother follows with stones in her arms.   [SFX: Slow footsteps and stones knocking together]
The wolf snores with his eyes closed.   [SFX: Deep, rumbling snores]
The woodcutter lowers his stones through the opening in the wolf’s belly.   [SFX: Muted clunks of stones settling together]
Little Red Riding Hood tips her stones into the opening.   [SFX: Brief tumble of muffled stone clacks]
Grandmother adds her stones to the belly.   [SFX: A few final muted stone clunks]
Grandmother lifts a needle and thread from her bedside sewing basket.   [SFX: Soft wicker rustle from the sewing basket]
She passes the thread through the needle’s eye.
She stitches the wolf’s belly shut.

10. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — LATER
   [AMBIENCE: Quiet room tone with faint birdsong outside.]
The wolf’s eyes open.
He climbs out of the bed toward us.   [SFX: Bed frame groans and paws land on floorboards]
He stumbles forward under the weight of his belly.   [SFX: Uneven heavy footfalls and muffled stones grinding]
He falls heavily onto the floor.   [SFX: Heavy thud on the floorboards with a dull stone clatter]
The wolf lies motionless, his eyes fixed and his chest still.

11. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — LATER
   [AMBIENCE: Peaceful room tone with birdsong drifting in from outside.]
   [MUSIC: Warm flute, harp, and gentle strings reprise the woodland theme with a peaceful resolution.]
Grandmother, Little Red Riding Hood and the woodcutter sit around the wooden table.
Grandmother lifts the small cake from the wicker basket.   [SFX: Soft wicker creak as the cake is lifted]
She breaks the cake into three pieces.   [SFX: Two soft crumbling breaks of cake]
She gives one piece to Little Red Riding Hood on her left.
She gives another piece to the woodcutter on her right.
She takes the remaining piece.
            LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD: I promise never to wander from the path again.
