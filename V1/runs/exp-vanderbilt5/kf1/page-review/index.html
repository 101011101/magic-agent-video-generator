Story

The source story, I Accept Her (824 characters)
On March 17, 1862, Secretary of War Edwin M. Stanton escorts Cornelius Vanderbilt to the Executive Mansion in Washington to meet Abraham Lincoln. Vanderbilt and Lincoln have not previously been personally acquainted, as Vanderbilt recalled on May 3, 1866. Lincoln asks whether Vanderbilt’s steamships can prevent the Confederate Merrimac from leaving Hampton Roads. Vanderbilt claims that his steamship Vanderbilt, properly manned, could deter the ironclad—or have ten-to-one odds of destroying her. He is making a prediction, not describing a victory. Lincoln asks Vanderbilt to name his price for undertaking the service. Vanderbilt refuses to profit from the government’s emergency: instead of naming a charter price, he offers the ship outright as a gift. Lincoln answers, as Vanderbilt later recalled, “I accept her.”
