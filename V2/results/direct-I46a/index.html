STORY · F-35A Lightning II — First Flight

In the opening two seconds, a widescreen live-action view shows AA-1, the first F-35A Lightning II prototype, lifting off at Lockheed Martin in Fort Worth, Texas, at 12:44 p.m. Central Standard Time on December 15, 2006. The title reads “F-35A Lightning II.” Chief pilot Jon Beesley remains an indistinct silhouette beneath the dark canopy.

The gray aircraft rises nose-high above the pale runway, with its nose gear and both main landing-gear assemblies extended. Its pointed nose, slender test probe, swept wings, side intakes and two outward-canted tail fins match the documented aircraft. From seconds two through five, “One engine” appears; calm English narration says, “The F-35A has one engine.”

From seconds five through eight, the view follows AA-1 flying after departure. “Conventional takeoff” replaces the previous fact label, while the narrator says, “It takes off from a conventional runway.” These successive views show an excerpt of the inaugural development test flight, not its full thirty-five-minute duration.
