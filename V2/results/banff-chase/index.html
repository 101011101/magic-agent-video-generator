Summer sunlight fills a widescreen view of Bow Lake beside the Icefields Parkway in Banff National Park. Turquoise water stretches beneath jagged Rocky Mountain peaks. Dense lodgepole pines border the winding, two-lane road.

From the pine-lined shoulder, the view opens across Bow Lake again. Blue Nine closes to half a car length. Red Seven sweeps into a bend; Blue Nine follows the same tight line. Tires shriek against asphalt.

Bracken, a bull elk, stands among the lodgepole pines beside the next bend. Red Seven’s brake lights flare. Blue Nine’s brake lights flare a heartbeat later. The gap nearly vanishes.

The final wide view holds Bow Lake and the bare, ice-streaked peaks above the pines as both cars sweep past Bracken, their engines dropping to a ragged growl.
