What held across the cut. The place, the people, the light and the camera. Other faults. (1) Clip 1 ignored “one continuous shot, no cuts”. Story

Petal & Stem at Dawn (ad, fiction)

16-second widescreen live-action ad; close-ups and a gentle pullback, with warm, natural dawn light.

Dawn light falls across Petal & Stem on Alder Street. Mara, wearing a green apron, unlocks the Petal & Stem door with a brass key and pushes it open. A brass doorbell gives a bright ring.

Inside Petal & Stem, quick close-ups follow Mara’s hands over a wooden counter. Mara gathers pink tulips and white daisies into a bouquet, folds cream paper around the stems, and ties a blue ribbon around the cream paper. Sunlight catches the petals as Mara lifts the bouquet from the wooden counter.

At the Petal & Stem door, Eli smiles. Mara places the bouquet into Eli’s waiting hands. Eli curls both hands around the cream paper, with the blue ribbon hanging over Eli’s fingers.

The camera draws back onto Alder Street. The final shot holds on the Petal & Stem shopfront in warm dawn light. Above the open Petal & Stem door, the shop sign reads: “Petal & Stem.”
