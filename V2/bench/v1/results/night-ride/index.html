Why the middle feels wrong

The picture hands over cleanly: segment 2’s first frame reproduces segment 1’s last frame (same rider, jacket, bag, helmet, street). The sound does not. Each generation makes its own audio and nothing of segment 1’s audio is fed to segment 2 (only the frame is). The brief asked for no speech, and both models still emit speech-like babble: ours says “They also” in segment 1 and switches to French-then-Japanese-sounding babble in segment 2; the MCP’s single generation says “However,” then “Côte d’Ivoire”. At the join the voice, the “language” and the room tone all change at once, which the ear reads as a cut even though the eye does not.

The same mechanism breaks narration on the long pieces: a model’s speech is faithful for roughly the first 12 to 15 seconds of a generation and then decays into phonetic babble (the chemistry piece: correct English to 15 s, then “sutra rebrigeratugisatatec rituale citrate”, then a clean restart with a new voice when segment 2 begins). The fix is structural: when nobody speaks, generate with audio off and lay an ambience track by code; when a narrator speaks, make the narration by the voice tool (exact words, one voice across every segment) and mix it under the model’s picture; keep the model’s own audio only for lip-synced dialogue, and keep those generations short.
