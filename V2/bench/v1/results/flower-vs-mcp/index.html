What this says


For a 16-second, one-place ad, one generation wins. A single generation keeps the bouquet, the people and the light consistent by itself. Splitting into two 8-second clips gave the model a cut to break continuity across, and the first clip already went wrong inside itself (the floating bouquet). Our structure paid off on the one thing it controls: the shop name is exact in both of our shots, while the MCP's opening sign is misspelled. Its final storefront is correct.
