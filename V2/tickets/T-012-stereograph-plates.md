# T-012 · The stereograph plate problem

status:     open
priority:   medium
subsystem:  assets

what:
In the reference pick, reject stereograph cards, mounts, two-up images, pages of text and anything that is not a single
clean view of the thing; when the pick is a mounted image, crop to the image by code. Add the test to the pick prompt's
criteria and record the rejection reason in the bank entry.

why:
The exterior plate chosen for the Vanderbilt film from Wikimedia Commons is a stereograph: the plate description reads
"two near-identical side-by-side stereoscopic views of the same exterior". A keyframe painted onto it would carry the
doubled view. The to-do item B4 in the archived testing list named this; it was never done.

acceptance:
On the Vanderbilt bank the exterior pick is a single view or is cropped to one; the bank entry says why; no two-up image
survives the pick on a rerun of the procurement.
