# T-010 · Fiction mode end to end

status:     open
priority:   medium
subsystem:  story, assets, screenplay

what:
Run a made-up brief through the pipeline: the gate sets `mode = fiction`, the story call runs without the fact constraint,
every character gets a generated full-length sheet and every place a generated plate against the style reference, the
screenplay keeps its rule of adding no business beyond the story. Verify the medium-as-noun rule holds on the sheets (no
figurines, no cartoons) with the set check from the bakery work.

why:
Everything since 29 October has run on a nonfiction brief with Commons portraits. The bakery ad (fiction) ran on the older
grid and chain pipelines, not on the shooting script, so the fiction path of V2 is untested: the gate's "no research"
branch, the story without citations, generation-only assets.

acceptance:
A 30-second fiction brief reaches keyframe prompts with a bank of generated references that pass the medium check, and the
screenplay passes the code checks; costs of the sheets and plates stated before they are made.
