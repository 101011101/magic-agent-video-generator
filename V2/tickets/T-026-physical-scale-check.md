# T-026 · Sizes must agree with the physical world

status:     open 2026-10-30
priority:   medium
subsystem:  screenplay (check), assets (one field)

what:
Every element gets a physical scale class at the assets stage (handheld, person, vehicle, aircraft, ship, building,
landscape). A screenplay check reads each frame's `size` words against those classes and the depths: a thing of a smaller
class at the same or farther depth than a thing of a larger class may not have a larger `size`. Error, so the writer
fixes it in its revision round. The keyframe prompt also states the ratio where two classes meet in one frame ("a bomb is
a fortieth of the frigate's length").

why:
In the fourth Top Gun screenplay shot 9 puts the bombs at "medium" (a quarter of the frame's height) and the frigate at
"small" (a tenth) in the same frame, with the bombs "immediately above the deck": a 3 m bomb drawn 2.5 times the height of
a 130 m ship. Shot 10 then says bombs "small" beside a "large" frigate, and the painter, with the frame before as its
only picture of the pair, kept them huge. The checks saw nothing because size is checked against depth only.

acceptance:
On the fourth screenplay the check names shot 9; on the Vanderbilt and bakery fixtures it is silent.
