# T-018 · Preset voices: pick a voice per character from a catalogue

- status: built (OpenAI presets) · priority: high · subsystem: assets, sound
- update 2026-10-30: built in src/assets/assets.js (an LLM picks one of the eleven OpenAI TTS presets per character and for the narrator, with a delivery instruction; a sample is rendered once; stored as bank[id].voice). Magic Hour's voice generator is preset-only with several hundred NAMED voices, mostly real people, at 0.1 credit per character: not used, by our rule against impersonating real people's actual voices. Its voice cloner needs a 3-second sample per call and stores no voice id.
- what: a catalogue of ready-made voices to choose from instead of describing one: OpenAI TTS presets (alloy, ash, ballad, coral, echo, fable, onyx, nova, sage, shimmer, verse, steered by an instructions field for pace and tone) and whatever Magic Hour's voice generator offers (preset voices and descriptor voices; see V2/reference/magic-hour-api-sheet.md once written). The cast stage picks the closest preset per character from the research voice notes (age, pitch, accent) and records it in the voice bank (T-013); the user can override. Cloning a real person's voice is not done.
- why: a preset is stable by construction, costs nothing to keep, and removes the per-clip sampling that makes the same man sound different between cuts.
- acceptance: run.json bank entries carry voice.preset (provider, id, instructions); a 5-second sample per character is rendered once for approval; the same preset reads every line of that character.
