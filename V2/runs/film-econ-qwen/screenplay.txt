synopsis: Maya and Leo sit behind their microphones at a walnut table in Cedar Studio, identified as a Keynesian economist and an Austrian-school economist. Maya smiles and tilts her coffee mug toward him, finding agreement on another coffee; Leo smiles and raises his mug toward hers while maintaining his opposition to stimulus.
