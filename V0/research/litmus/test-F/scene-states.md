# Scene states (test F)

**Request:** A short film, 2 minutes. A lighthouse keeper finds a glowing shell on the beach at dawn. She brings it home. At night it hums and the lamp goes out. She climbs the tower in a storm and uses the shell to relight it. Ships are saved. Morning again, she puts the shell back on the beach. Melancholy, painterly, no dialogue, just score.

**Style:** Painterly digital cinema in pearl-grey, faded blue and muted amber, with brushed textures and soft, melancholy natural light.

**Assets:**
- @keeper (character): Human woman, 1.68 metres tall, late middle age, narrow shoulders, weathered skin, grey hair tied in a low bun, wearing a dark wool coat, plain trousers and leather boots.
- @shell (prop): Spiral seashell, 7 centimetres long, with an ivory surface, fine ridges and a translucent inner chamber.
- @beach (location): Rocky tidal beach, approximately 300 metres long, with wet sand, shallow rock pools, scattered shell fragments and ribbons of kelp beneath a sloping headland.
- @lighthouse (location): Lighthouse tower, 24 metres tall, with cylindrical salt-stained white masonry and a glass lantern room above the headland.
- @cottage (location): Keeper's cottage, approximately 8 metres wide and 5 metres high, with stone walls, a slate roof and a shore-facing doorway beneath the tower.
- @kitchen (location): Cottage kitchen, 4 by 5 metres, with faded blue plaster walls, worn wooden floorboards and a sea-facing window.
- @table (prop): Wooden kitchen table, 1.2 metres long, 75 centimetres wide and 74 centimetres high, with a worn rectangular top.
- @cup (prop): Ceramic drinking cup, 9 centimetres high, with an off-white glaze, one handle and a chipped rim.
- @chair (prop): Wooden kitchen chair, 85 centimetres high, with a straight slatted back and a square seat.
- @bread (prop): Piece of rustic bread, initially 12 centimetres long, with a brown crust and pale, coarse crumb.
