AI Video Landscape

Public evidence for judging our lighthouse short. Collected 2026-10-07 from public pages only. No logins, no paid runs.

  • Purpose: show what other systems produce for narrative, multi-shot prompts like ours.
  • Benchmark systems: FocalML, InVideo AI, VideoGen, Genspark, Manus. Reference systems: LTX Studio, Runway, Google Flow/Veo, Kling, Higgsfield, Luma, Sora (discontinued).
  • Caution: official showcase films are curated. A human directs them, picks takes, and edits them, often with many tools. They are a ceiling, not a one-prompt result.
  • Caution: many YouTube reviews are sponsored or use affiliate links. Treat praise as weak evidence.
  • unverified marks a claim from one source that I did not confirm on an official page.

Benchmark systems

FocalML

One-prompt agent. focalml.com

  • Chat agent plans the script, narration and shot list, then generates and edits in one workspace.
  • Models: Veo, Seedance, Kling, Hailuo (video); GPT Image, Flux (image); ElevenLabs, OpenAI (voice).
  • Consistency: "You can generate and re-use characters and locations in your projects."
  • Showcase titles are narrated history pieces ("Oppenheimer: Regret and Atomic Warning"). No direct video links on the site.
  • Price: Free, Starter $10, Standard $30, Pro $100 per month.
  • Risk: one YouTube video says FocalML may shut down unverified. Site and pricing were live on 2026-10-07.
  • Focal AI Video Agent Workflow: Script to Video (AiZen Tools, 7:16): mini documentary on Leyte Gulf from one brief.
  • How I Made a Hollywood-Style Movie Using Free FocalML (Artificicy AI, 7:05).
  • Focal AI Tutorial 2026: Create a Complete AI Video (Dan, Smart Tutorials, 8:01).
  • FocalML: Shutting Down AI Video Tool? My Test (0:50) unverified.

InVideo AI

One-prompt full video. Agent One / Agent Two for film. invideo.io

  • Default mode: script, stock footage (iStock, Storyblocks), voiceover, captions. Generative mode uses Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.5.
  • Agent One keeps project context and character details across scenes. Agent Two adds script-to-reference generation.
  • Failure modes: generic stock look; avatar lip-sync drift in close-ups; chat edits work "about 75% of the time"; stock misses abstract or specific subjects; scripts "safe rather than sharp".
  • Price (official): Starter $20, Plus $36, Max $75 per seat per month, annual. Credits do not roll over.
  • How I Made a 3-Minute AI Film – invideo Agent One (Zenzdesign, 10:56). Closest public match to our task class.
  • I Tested invideo Agent Two so You Don't Have to (Joseph Martin, 11:31): good and bad.
  • How to Create LONG AI Videos in One Prompt (Invideo + Veo 3.1) (Youri van Hofwegen, 9:14).
  • The Future of Filmmaking is Here – invideo Agent One (official, 1:59).
  • Is InVideo AI Worth Paying for? Brutal Honest Review (8:07).

VideoGen

One-prompt explainer and social video. videogen.io

  • Idea, script or voiceover to video. Uses AI b-roll and stock footage, voice, captions. VideoGen 4.0 adds a research and brief agent.
  • Not built for narrative. No character consistency feature stated.
  • User reports: "repeatedly reused the same images to pad out videos"; clips "often completely irrelevant"; 1.2/5 on SoftwareAdvice (5 reviews).
  • Price: from $12/mo (homepage). Pro $24 ($12 annual), Business $149 ($74 annual) unverified.
  • Introducing VideoGen 4.0, AI Agents for Video Creation (official, 0:50).
  • I Tried VideoGen So You Don't Have To (Real Review) (Cisco Diaz, 11:58).
  • Watch this Before getting Videogen.io – Honest Review (9:35).

Genspark

General agent with tool calling. genspark.ai

  • AI Video Generator tool: pick a model (Kling, MiniMax, Gemini/Veo), text or image to video, 5–10 s clips. It writes the shot prompt for you.
  • Workspace 4.0 adds Storyboards and Clip Genius (edits any video from one prompt).
  • No public one-prompt narrative film from Genspark found. Multi-shot assembly not confirmed unverified.
  • Official pages returned HTTP 403 to my fetch. Pricing not retrieved.
  • Genspark AI Tutorial: How to Make AI Videos in Minutes (Darby Rollins, 4:39).
  • Genspark AI Workspace 4.0: 13 Best AI Workflows for Creators (FILM CRUX, sponsored, 5:11).
  • I Built a Faceless Channel Using One AI Tool (Genspark AI) (Money Degree, 19:18).
  • The age of solo filmmaking – How director DESO uses Genspark (blog, 2026-07-28; not read, 403).

Manus

General agent. One sentence to finished, editable video. manus.im/features/video

  • "Manus researches the idea, writes the script, shoots it with Seedance 2.5, and edits a finished video."
  • Output is a project: shots, captions, voiceover and music on separate tracks in Video Editor.
  • Writes code for motion graphics, charts and maps. Checks every render.
  • Consistency method, length limit and pricing not stated on the page.
  • Manus showcase: Emberkeep, The Odyssey, Equilibrium (each shows its prompt).
  • Introducing Manus video generation (official, 1:17).
  • Introducing Manus Video Editor (official, 0:53).

Reference systems

LTX Studio

Prompt or script to storyboard to shots, with editor. ltx.io/studio

  • One prompt generates scenes, characters and camera angles. User then edits per shot.
  • Consistency: character elements, duplicate function, keyframe control.
  • Pricing page failed to load. Not retrieved.
  • The Fallen Leaf (official, 4:07): a girl and an oak tree. Quiet, melancholy tone near ours.
  • Koan (official, 13:24): meditative, every shot made in LTX Studio.
  • Nine: A Sci-Fi Short Film (official, 9:07).
  • Getting consistent characters across shots (official tutorial, 1:10).
  • I Typed One Sentence, AI Turned It Into a Full Video (Apexa AI, 3:24): notes credit burn.

Runway (Gen-4, Gen-4.5)

Shot tool. A human assembles the film. runway.com/gen-4-bts

  • Gen-4 References: one reference image keeps a character, object or location across shots, no fine-tuning.
  • Runway calls storytelling "the new AI benchmark" and made five Gen-4 shorts to test it.
  • Price: Free; Standard $12, Pro $28, Max $76 per month (annual). Gen-4.5: 60 credits per 5 s.
  • Flicker. The lamp that comes with a story. (official, 1:53): object story about bringing light. Nearest theme and length to ours.
  • The Lonely Little Flame (official, 1:44): recurring character, wordless.
  • The Herd (official, 1:49).
  • The Retrieval (official, 1:53).
  • NYC is a Zoo (official, 1:08).
  • Creating an AI Short Film (Full Workflow) (official, 15:27): shows the human work behind a short.

Google Flow / Veo 3.1

Shot tool with Scenebuilder. Flow TV

  • Ingredients to Video: several reference images control characters and style. Frames to Video: start and end frame. Extend: continuous shots of a minute or more.
  • Native audio on all three modes since Veo 3.1.
  • Failure mode: Dave Clark on "The Cleaner": "a few minor details like gloves, etc, change."
  • Price: Google AI Pro (100 generations/month at launch) and Google AI Ultra.
  • THE CLEANER (Dave Clark, 3:14): 100% text to video, recurring character.
  • TEO (a Veo 3.1 only short film) (AiFicFac, 8:13): teddy bear life story, Gemini tools only.
  • Flow TV: community clips with visible prompts.

Kling

Shot tool plus agent workspace. klingai.com

  • Kling 3.0 (Feb 2026); Kling 4.0 with native 4K. Element Library 3.0 for reusable subjects.
  • Homepage claims a 135-minute feature film with all visuals made in Kling (Fountain 0, dir. Ash Koosha).
  • Pricing not retrieved.
  • DRACUS (FILM CRUX, Kling partner, 2:00): fantasy short, recurring heroine.
  • The Thief (fAIkout, 1:11).

Higgsfield

Cinema Studio 4 shot tool; Popcorn storyboards. higgsfield.ai

  • Cinema Studio: camera presets, color, Character Creator, per-scene edit and extend.
  • Popcorn: storyboard frames with one consistent character, then animate.
  • Showcase films are open-sourced: every prompt and asset is public.
  • Many YouTube rankings are Higgsfield-sponsored. Pricing not retrieved.
  • Oneiric (official, 19:49): 20-min drama; full project.
  • ANERNEQ (official, 20:06): Arctic drama, firelight and snow.
  • Passport Rush (official, animated, 4:35).
  • Higgsfield Popcorn Solves AI's Biggest Consistency Problem (AI Geeked, 6:24).

Luma (Dream Machine, Ray3)

Shot tool plus Luma Agents. lumalabs.ai

  • Ray3.2: 1080p, 10 s clips. Also hosts Seedance, Kling, Veo 3.1.
  • Luma Agents carry context from planning to final assembly.
  • Price: Plus $25, Pro $75, Ultra $250 per month (annual).
  • L'HEURE BLEUE (official, 3:20).
  • Remnants (official, Ray3, 0:51).

OpenAI Sora 2 (discontinued)

Shot tool. App shut down 26 Apr 2026; API shut down 24 Sep 2026.

  • Not a live competitor. Keep only as a historical quality point.
  • FROSTBITE – My Very First Sora 2 Short Film (Dave Clark, 2:58): 100% text to video.

Our lighthouse output

  • Prompt: "A short film, 2 minutes. A lighthouse keeper finds a glowing shell on the beach at dawn. She brings it home. At night it hums and the lamp goes out. She climbs the tower in a storm and uses the shell to relight it. Ships are saved. Morning again, she puts the shell back on the beach. Melancholy, painterly, no dialogue, just score."
  • File: n1.mp4. 120.8 s, 1280×720, 24 fps, no audio track.

Known problems:

  • The kitchen renders outdoors.
  • The keeper turns into a man in one shot.
  • A second lighthouse appears near the end.
  • The shell becomes a scallop in one clip.
  • Seams jump where shots are not exact handoffs.
  • No score. The prompt asks for "just score".

Same-subject AI shorts by unknown tools, for tone only: The Man Nobody Thanked – A Lighthouse Film (5:24); THE LAST KEEPER (6:00).
