seam SSIM: similarity of the last frame before the join and the first frame after it (1 = same image). jump: raw pixel change across the join in the final cut; large at a cut by design.
S: brightness-compensated step across the join / median step of both clips (clean 0.7–1.3; below 0.5 = a stall frame). V: speed of the first 10 frames after the join / speed of the last 10 before it (clean 0.8–1.25; below = motion reset). L: |luma step at the join| + |luma ramp over the next 5 frames| (clean below 1.0). Values in brackets are before the seam fix (raw clips).
ramp frames: frames until the clip reaches 0.7× its median speed. head / tail speed: first / last 10 frames vs the clip median. end snap: largest of the last 2 steps vs median (freeze-then-snap shows as a large value). frame 0 luma: how much darker the clip's first frame is than its own level. dropped: head frames removed at a continuous join; luma offsets: added to the first kept frames.
