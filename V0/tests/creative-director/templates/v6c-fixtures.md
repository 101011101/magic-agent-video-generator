Some props in a video's bible are fixtures: they are a fixed part of one location and are present, unchanged, in every shot of that location (for example a lighthouse lamp in its lantern room, a counter in a shop). Fixtures are drawn into the location's reference image, so they keep their real scale.

A prop is not a fixture if a character carries or moves it, if it appears in more than one location as a physical object nearby, or if it appears or disappears during the story (for example debris that a storm leaves).
