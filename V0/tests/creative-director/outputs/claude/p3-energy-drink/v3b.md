# VOLT: Operational Chunks (6 shots)

One chunk = one shot. The request asks for exactly 6 shots, so there are exactly 6 chunks.

**Can reference (applies to every chunk):** a matte black 355 ml can, non-reflective surface, neon green lightning-bolt logo, the word VOLT in white capital letters. It must match the uploaded product photo exactly. The logo must be clearly visible and correct in every shot. Use the product photo as the reference image for every generation. Composite or swap in the real can image where the generated can drifts.

---

**Chunk 1: Ignition**
- **Duration:** ~3 s
- **Visual:** Pure darkness. A thin neon green line flickers like a live wire, then sharpens into the lightning-bolt logo. The matte black can emerges from the shadows around it, upright, logo facing the camera. The white VOLT lettering lights up last.
- **On-screen text:** None. VOLT appears only as the printed logo on the can.
- **Audio:** Silence, then a low electric hum builds. Faint electrical crackle on the flicker.
- **Entities:** VOLT can (see can reference). Front-facing, upright, logo fully visible.
- **Environment:** Black void. The only light is a neon green glow plus a subtle rim light on the can. Palette: black, neon green, white.
- **Depends on:** None. Final frame must show the can and logo clearly.
- **Source:** "Darkness. Then a single thin line of neon green appears, flickering like a live wire. It sharpens into the lightning bolt, and the matte black can emerges around it from the shadows, standing upright. The white letters V-O-L-T catch the light last. A low electric hum builds underneath."

**Chunk 2: Rooftop rain**
- **Duration:** ~3 s
- **Visual:** The can stands on a wet concrete rooftop at night. The city glows far below, out of focus. Fine rain falls in streaks. Drops bead on the can but the surface stays matte, not glossy. The bolt and VOLT face forward.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Hum continues under rain patter. Distant city ambience.
- **Entities:** VOLT can (see can reference). Must stay matte despite the rain. Logo facing the camera.
- **Environment:** Rooftop, night, rain. Cool city bokeh, wet concrete. Palette: deep blue-black, neon green accent, white.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 1 (same can, same orientation logic).
- **Source:** "The can stands on a wet concrete rooftop at night. The city glows far below. Rain falls in fine streaks around it, and drops bead on the matte surface without making it shine. The green bolt and the white VOLT face forward, steady and bright against the dark skyline."

**Chunk 3: The crack**
- **Duration:** ~2.5 s
- **Visual:** Close-up. A hand grips the can low, so the fingers do not cover the logo or the name. The thumb lifts the tab. The can cracks open and a faint mist escapes.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Silence, then a sharp crack and hiss, hard and loud, like a starting gun. Music beat drops right after.
- **Entities:** VOLT can (see can reference). Logo and VOLT fully unobstructed. Hand: neutral, correct anatomy. Grip stays low on the can.
- **Environment:** Rooftop at night, tight framing, background dark. Same palette as Chunk 2.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 2 (same location and lighting).
- **Source:** "A hand reaches in and grips the can, fingers wrapped low so the logo and the name stay fully visible. The thumb finds the tab. A sharp crack and hiss break the silence, and a faint mist escapes from the opening. The sound lands like a starting gun."

**Chunk 4: Energy montage**
- **Duration:** ~4 s
- **Visual:** One shot with fast, energetic movement. Option A: a single continuous move that passes the three actions. Option B: rapid in-camera whip transitions. A skateboarder launches off a concrete ledge, a runner bursts off the line, a dancer spins under strobing green light. The can is visible throughout: on the ledge, in the runner's hand, on the edge of the stage. It is always upright, always facing out, with the bolt pulsing with the rhythm.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Driving electronic beat at full energy. Skateboard pop, sneaker scuff, crowd or strobe whoosh.
- **Entities:** VOLT can (see can reference), upright, logo facing out in each moment. Skateboarder, runner and dancer (unbranded clothing, no competing logos).
- **Environment:** Urban concrete, track and stage. Night. Strobing neon green light. Palette: black, neon green, white.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 3 (the beat drop from the can opening leads into this shot).
- **Source:** "Energy breaks loose. A skateboarder launches off a concrete ledge, a runner bursts off the line, a dancer spins under strobing green light. In the middle of the motion, the can sits on the ledge, in the runner's hand, on the edge of the stage — always upright, always facing out, the neon bolt pulsing with the rhythm."
- **Note:** This is the hardest shot for the "6 shots" rule. To count as one shot, build it as a continuous move or a single shot with in-camera transitions, not as three separate cuts.

**Chunk 5: Suspended hero**
- **Duration:** ~3 s
- **Visual:** The can floats mid-air against pure black. Fine droplets drift around it in slow motion. Arcs of neon green electricity crawl across the background, echoing the bolt shape. The can rotates slightly, then settles facing forward, with the bolt and VOLT perfectly sharp.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Music drops into slow-motion texture. Electrical crackle on the arcs.
- **Entities:** VOLT can (see can reference). Any rotation must end with the logo fully front-facing and in sharp focus.
- **Environment:** Black void, neon green electric arcs, backlit droplets.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 1 (same void and green-arc visual language).
- **Source:** "The can is suspended mid-air against pure black. Fine droplets drift around it in slow motion. Arcs of neon green electricity crawl across the background, echoing the shape of the logo. The can turns slightly, then settles facing forward, the lightning bolt and VOLT perfectly sharp."

**Chunk 6: Packshot**
- **Duration:** ~3 s
- **Visual:** Silence. The can stands alone on a plain white surface, matching the uploaded product photo: matte black, neon green bolt, white VOLT lettering. It holds still to the end.
- **On-screen text:** None (none in the story).
- **Audio:** Music cuts. The hum fades to silence.
- **Entities:** VOLT can, identical to the product photo. Best practice: use the actual product photo (cleaned and extended to the frame) as this shot's source.
- **Environment:** Plain white background, soft even studio light, as in the product photo.
- **Depends on:** The uploaded product photo (direct match). Chunk 5 (the can settles front-facing, then the cut to white).
- **Source:** "Silence again. The can stands alone on a plain white surface, just as in the product photo: matte black, neon green bolt, white VOLT lettering. The hum fades out. The can holds still, clean and confident, as the ad ends."

## Global requirements

- **Shot count:** exactly 6 shots. No extra cuts inside a shot.
- **Can identity (hard requirement):** the can must look **exactly** like the uploaded photo in every shot: matte black, 355 ml proportions, neon green lightning-bolt logo, white capital VOLT. No gloss, no color drift, no altered or misspelled lettering, no added text on the can.
- **Logo in every shot:** the logo must be clearly visible in all 6 shots. It must not be obscured by hands, rain, mist or motion blur. QC every shot against the product photo before delivery.
- **Total duration:** not specified. Estimate ~18 s (6 shots of 2.5–4 s). Adjust to fit a 15 s or 20 s spot if needed.
- **Aspect ratio:** not specified. Default to 16:9. Offer 9:16 and 1:1 cutdowns for social.
- **Music and sound:** an electric hum motif opens and closes the ad. A driving electronic beat drops at the can opening (Chunk 3) and peaks in Chunk 4. Silence at the end. No narration or dialogue.
- **Style:** high-contrast, premium product commercial. Black, neon green and white palette. Crisp focus on the can in every shot.
