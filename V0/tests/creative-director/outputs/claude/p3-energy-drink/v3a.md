**VOLT** — a product ad in six shots

The can is the same in every moment of this film: a matte black 355 ml can, its surface soft and non-reflective, with a neon green lightning-bolt logo and the word VOLT in white capital letters. It looks exactly as it does in the product photo, and the logo is clearly visible in every one of the six shots.

**One.** Darkness. Then a single thin line of neon green appears, flickering like a live wire. It sharpens into the lightning bolt, and the matte black can emerges around it from the shadows, standing upright. The white letters V-O-L-T catch the light last. A low electric hum builds underneath.

**Two.** The can stands on a wet concrete rooftop at night. The city glows far below. Rain falls in fine streaks around it, and drops bead on the matte surface without making it shine. The green bolt and the white VOLT face forward, steady and bright against the dark skyline.

**Three.** A hand reaches in and grips the can, fingers wrapped low so the logo and the name stay fully visible. The thumb finds the tab. A sharp crack and hiss break the silence, and a faint mist escapes from the opening. The sound lands like a starting gun.

**Four.** Energy breaks loose. A skateboarder launches off a concrete ledge, a runner bursts off the line, a dancer spins under strobing green light. In the middle of the motion, the can sits on the ledge, in the runner's hand, on the edge of the stage — always upright, always facing out, the neon bolt pulsing with the rhythm.

**Five.** The can is suspended mid-air against pure black. Fine droplets drift around it in slow motion. Arcs of neon green electricity crawl across the background, echoing the shape of the logo. The can turns slightly, then settles facing forward, the lightning bolt and VOLT perfectly sharp.

**Six.** Silence again. The can stands alone on a plain white surface, just as in the product photo: matte black, neon green bolt, white VOLT lettering. The hum fades out. The can holds still, clean and confident, as the ad ends.
