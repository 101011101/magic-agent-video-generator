# VOLT — 6-Shot Product Ad

**Product:** VOLT energy drink — matte black 355 ml can, neon green lightning-bolt logo, "VOLT" in white capital letters
**Length:** ~15 seconds (6 shots, ~2–3 seconds each)
**Format:** 9:16 vertical (reframe to 16:9 or 1:1 as needed)
**Look:** Dark, high-contrast, neon green accent light that matches the logo. Electric, fast, premium.
**Music:** Driving electronic beat with a bass drop at Shot 4. Electric crackle sound effects.

---

### Product consistency rule (applies to every shot)
The can must match your product photo exactly in every shot:
- Matte black finish (not glossy, not metallic)
- Neon green lightning-bolt logo
- "VOLT" in white capital letters
- Standard 355 ml can shape and proportions
- Logo and "VOLT" text visible and readable in **all six shots** — the can always faces the camera, front-on or at a slight angle, never turned away

Use your product photo as the reference image for every shot.

---

### Shot 1 — The Spark (0:00–0:02)
**Visual:** Pitch black. A single neon green spark crackles, then a bolt of electricity flashes and reveals the VOLT can standing upright, front-facing, centered. Logo glows briefly.
**Camera:** Static, front-on, medium close-up.
**Sound:** Electric crackle, low hum.

### Shot 2 — The Detail (0:02–0:04)
**Visual:** Slow macro push along the matte black surface of the can, ending on the green lightning-bolt logo and the white "VOLT" lettering. Cold condensation drops bead on the matte finish.
**Camera:** Slow dolly-in macro, slight angle, logo fully in frame at end.
**Sound:** Beat begins.

### Shot 3 — The Crack (0:04–0:07)
**Visual:** A hand grips the can (logo facing camera, not covered by fingers) and cracks it open. A burst of mist escapes from the tab. Green rim light outlines the can.
**Camera:** Close-up, front three-quarter angle.
**Sound:** Sharp *pssht*, synced to the beat.

### Shot 4 — The Surge (0:07–0:10)
**Visual:** An athlete/skater mid-motion takes a drink from the can at night under neon city lights. The can is held so the logo faces camera. Green energy streaks trail from the can as the bass drops.
**Camera:** Low angle, slight slow motion.
**Sound:** Bass drop.

### Shot 5 — The Rush (0:10–0:12)
**Visual:** Fast montage feel within one shot: the can sits on a concrete ledge in the foreground, logo forward, while blurred movement (bikes, runners, city traffic light trails) streaks past behind it.
**Camera:** Static on the can, background in motion blur.
**Sound:** Whoosh effects on the beat.

### Shot 6 — The Hero (0:12–0:15)
**Visual:** Can stands centered on a black reflective surface, perfectly front-facing. Green electric arcs circle it once and fade. Text appears below: **VOLT. CHARGE UP.**
**Camera:** Slow push-in, ending on a clean hero frame.
**Sound:** Final electric zap, music hits and cuts out.

### Production notes
- **Logo accuracy:** AI video models often distort small logos and text. For best results, generate each shot from your product photo as a reference, check every frame, and regenerate any shot where the logo or "VOLT" text warps. For the hero shot (Shot 6), you can composite the original product photo directly onto the generated background to guarantee a perfect match.
- **End text:** Add "VOLT. CHARGE UP." as a text overlay in editing, not inside the generated video. The tagline is a placeholder — send me your real tagline if you have one.
- **Hands:** In Shots 3 and 4, keep fingers off the logo.
