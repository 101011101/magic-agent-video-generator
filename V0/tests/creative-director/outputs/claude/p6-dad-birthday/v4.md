# "Sixty Looks Good on You" — 20-Second Birthday Video

**Runtime:** 20 seconds
**Format:** 9:16 vertical (easy to send by text or post to stories); a 16:9 version works with the same shots
**Tone:** Warm, celebratory, a little funny, a little heartfelt.
**Music:** Upbeat, feel-good acoustic or soft-rock track at about 115 BPM, building to a big finish.

> **Before we produce this:** You'd like your dad in the video, but no photos were uploaded. To put him in it, please share **3–6 photos of your dad**: at least one clear, recent, front-facing photo of his face, plus a few older photos (childhood, young adult, with family) if you have them. I've written the script around those photos. Every shot marked **[DAD PHOTO]** needs one. Also helpful: his name or what you call him ("Dad," "Pops"), and a short personal message from you.

---

## SCENE 1 — THE COUNTDOWN (0.0 – 4.0 s)

### Shot 1 — Close-up, slow push-in
- **Timing:** 0.0 – 2.0 s
- **Action:** A birthday cake on a table in a warm, softly lit room. Candles shaped as the numbers **6** and **0** are lit; flames flicker.
- **Camera:** Slow push-in toward the candles.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Dialogue / narration:** None
- **Sound:** Match strike and soft crackle; music begins with a gentle guitar intro.

### Shot 2 — Extreme close-up, static
- **Timing:** 2.0 – 4.0 s
- **Action:** The "60" candles fill the frame.
- **Camera:** Static.
- **On-screen text:** `This Saturday, someone special turns…` (white, center, fades in at 2.2 s)
- **Dialogue / narration:** None
- **Sound:** Music builds with a rising drum roll.

## SCENE 2 — THROUGH THE YEARS (4.0 – 12.0 s)

A quick photo montage, each cut on the beat. Each photo has a gentle Ken Burns move so the stills feel alive.

### Shot 3 — [DAD PHOTO: childhood], slow push-in
- **Timing:** 4.0 – 6.0 s
- **Action:** Your dad as a child. Subtle warm vintage grade.
- **Camera:** Slow push-in on his face.
- **On-screen text:** `The early years` (small, lower third)
- **Sound:** Music kicks in fully on the cut at 4.0 s.

### Shot 4 — [DAD PHOTO: young adult], slow pan left to right
- **Timing:** 6.0 – 8.0 s
- **Action:** Your dad as a young man.
- **Camera:** Slow pan across the photo.
- **On-screen text:** `The legend begins` (small, lower third)
- **Sound:** Music continues.

### Shot 5 — [DAD PHOTO: with family / with you], slow pull-back
- **Timing:** 8.0 – 10.0 s
- **Action:** Your dad with the family or with you.
- **Camera:** Slow pull-back revealing the whole photo.
- **On-screen text:** `Best Dad ever` (small, lower third)
- **Sound:** Music lifts.

### Shot 6 — [DAD PHOTO: recent, front-facing], gentle animation
- **Timing:** 10.0 – 12.0 s
- **Action:** Your dad today. The photo is subtly animated: he breaks into a warm smile and gives a small nod or a thumbs-up. His face, features, and age stay exactly as in the photo.
- **Camera:** Static with a slight push-in.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Sound:** Light "ding" accent as he smiles.

## SCENE 3 — THE BIG REVEAL (12.0 – 20.0 s)

### Shot 7 — Medium, static, with confetti burst
- **Timing:** 12.0 – 16.0 s
- **Action:** Return to the cake from Shot 1. A burst of gold and blue confetti falls across the frame. The candles blow out on their own with a puff of smoke.
- **Camera:** Static, then a small "bump" zoom on the confetti burst at 12.0 s.
- **On-screen text:** `HAPPY 60th BIRTHDAY, DAD!` (big, bold, gold, center, pops in at 12.2 s with a slight bounce)
- **Dialogue / narration:** None
- **Sound:** Party popper and a cheer sound effect; music hits its big chorus.

### Shot 8 — [DAD PHOTO: recent] as closing card, slow push-in
- **Timing:** 16.0 – 20.0 s
- **Action:** Your dad's recent photo inside a soft gold frame, with gently falling confetti.
- **Camera:** Slow push-in.
- **On-screen text:** `We love you! ❤️` (center-lower, fades in at 16.5 s)
  Optional second line: `— [your name / the family]`
- **Dialogue / narration (optional):** A short recorded voice message from you, about 3 seconds, e.g. "Happy sixtieth, Dad. Love you." Record it on your phone and send it with the photos.
- **Sound:** Music resolves on a final warm chord, ringing out to 20.0 s.
