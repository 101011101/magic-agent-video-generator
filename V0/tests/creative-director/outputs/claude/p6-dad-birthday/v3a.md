**Sixty** — a 20-second birthday video for Dad

A warm Saturday morning. Sunlight spills across a backyard patio strung with paper lanterns and a hand-painted banner that reads **Happy 60th, Dad!** A long table is set with mismatched plates, a jug of lemonade, and a cake in the center, its frosting piped with a big candle-shaped **60**. Upbeat, gently nostalgic music plays — something with acoustic guitar and a little swing.

Dad walks out through the back door, still holding his coffee mug. He stops. His eyebrows go up. A surprised laugh breaks out of him, and he shakes his head the way dads do when they are pretending not to be moved.

Memories drift in around him like old photographs coming to life: Dad younger, with darker hair, holding a baby in a hospital blanket; Dad teaching a kid to ride a bike down a quiet street, running behind with one hand on the seat; Dad at the grill on a summer evening, waving a spatula like a conductor's baton. Each moment glows for a beat and then softens back into the present.

Back on the patio, Dad leans over the cake. The candles flicker. He takes a big breath, cheeks puffed, and blows them all out in one go. Smoke curls upward and he grins, proud of himself. Confetti bursts into the air and drifts down around his shoulders.

He raises his mug like a toast, looks right at us, and smiles — a little teary, very happy.

The final words appear across the warm sunlight:

**Happy 60th Birthday, Dad!**

The video ends at twenty seconds.
