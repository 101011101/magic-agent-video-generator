# Video Script Brief: Dad's 60th Birthday Video

## Goal and core idea
Create a 20-second birthday video celebrating the user's father turning 60. The dad must appear in the video. The idea it must get across: a warm, joyful, loving tribute — "Happy 60th, Dad" — that makes him feel celebrated and special on his birthday.

## Audience and platform
- **Audience:** The dad himself, plus family and friends.
- **Platform:** Not specified. Assume it will be sent by phone (WhatsApp/iMessage/family group chat) and possibly posted on social media or played at a birthday gathering on Saturday.

## Format, duration and aspect ratio
- **Format:** Short celebratory video featuring the dad, with a birthday message.
- **Duration:** 20 seconds (stated by user).
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (assumed, for phone viewing and sharing). Keep the dad's face and all text within the central safe area so a 16:9 playback on a TV still works if letterboxed.

## Tone and style
- Warm, celebratory, heartfelt, a little fun.
- Visual style: festive but tasteful — warm light, balloons, confetti, candles, a "60" motif.
- Music: upbeat, feel-good instrumental (no copyrighted "Happy Birthday" recording unless the user supplies one).
- Ends on a clear birthday message.

## User requirements (quoted exactly)
- "make a 20 second birthday video for my dad"
- "with him in it"
- "he turns 60 on saturday"

## Suggested structure (for the scriptwriter to refine)
1. **0–4 s:** Festive opening — confetti/balloons, "60" appears.
2. **4–14 s:** The dad featured — e.g. 2–3 moments of him (photos of him animated or presented with gentle motion, smiling, set into a celebratory scene).
3. **14–20 s:** End card: "Happy 60th Birthday, Dad!" (or his name), with a final celebratory burst.

## Uploads and how they must be used
- **Uploads:** None.
- **This is a blocker.** The user requires the dad to be "in it", but no photos or videos of him were provided. The video cannot show the real dad without them. The script must not invent or generate a stand-in "dad" and present him as the user's father.
- When photos/videos are provided: use them as the only source for the dad's likeness; preserve his identity exactly; animate them subtly (gentle motion, smile, parallax) or place them in celebratory frames. Do not alter his appearance.
- Until then, the script should mark the dad's shots as placeholders ("[PHOTO/VIDEO OF DAD 1]", etc.) so footage can be dropped in.

## Assumptions (not stated by the user)
- On-screen text: "Happy 60th Birthday, Dad!" — to be replaced with his name or the user's preferred wording if supplied.
- No voiceover; music and text carry the message.
- Celebration of milestone age 60 is a key visual (numbers "60" in balloons, candles, or gold text).
- The video should be ready before Saturday.
- Content is family-friendly and sincere, with light humour at most.

## Missing information only the user can supply
- **Photos and/or video clips of the dad** (required; ideally 2–5 clear images of his face, including some recent ones and possibly older/nostalgic ones).
- His name or what the user calls him (Dad, Papa, Baba, etc.) and the exact message they want on screen.
- Who the video is from (e.g. "Love, Sarah" or "From all of us").
- His interests, hobbies, or inside jokes to personalise the video.
- Preferred style: sentimental, funny, or party/hype.
- Music preference, or a specific song meaningful to him.
- Where it will be shown (phone message, social post, party screen) to confirm aspect ratio.
- Language for the text, if not English.
