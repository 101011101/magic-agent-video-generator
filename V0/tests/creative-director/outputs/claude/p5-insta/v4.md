# "Golden Hour Loop" — Instagram Reel Shooting Script

You didn't specify a subject, so I've written a short, visually striking reel that works for almost any account: a seamless-loop "one day in 15 seconds" piece built around light and match cuts. If you tell me what your account is about (food, travel, fitness, a product, your business), I can rewrite it around that.

**Runtime:** 15 seconds, designed to loop seamlessly
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920. Keep key action in the center; leave the bottom 20% clear for the caption and UI.
**Look:** Warm, cinematic, slightly dreamy. Golden light, soft lens flares, rich color.
**Music:** Upbeat lo-fi / chill-house track at about 110 BPM, with cuts landing on the beat. Choose a trending audio from Instagram's library at upload if you want more reach.

---

## SCENE 1 — THE HOOK (0.0 – 2.0 s)

### Shot 1 — Extreme close-up, fast push-in
- **Timing:** 0.0 – 1.0 s
- **Action:** A hand flicks open the blinds. A burst of golden sunlight floods the lens.
- **Camera:** Fast push-in toward the light.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Dialogue / narration:** None
- **Sound:** Blinds snap; beat drops immediately at 0.0 s (no slow intro).

### Shot 2 — Close-up, whip pan transition
- **Timing:** 1.0 – 2.0 s
- **Action:** Sunlight hits a cup of coffee; steam swirls through the light beam.
- **Camera:** Whip pan in from the right, settling on the cup.
- **On-screen text:** `POV: you said yes to the day` (bold white sans-serif, top-center, 1.0 – 3.5 s)
- **Dialogue / narration:** None
- **Sound:** Whoosh on the whip pan.

---

## SCENE 2 — THE MONTAGE (2.0 – 12.0 s)

Each shot cuts on the beat. Use match cuts: each shot's shape or motion carries into the next.

### Shot 3 — Medium, tracking forward
- **Timing:** 2.0 – 3.5 s
- **Action:** Feet in sneakers step out the front door onto a sunlit street.
- **Camera:** Low tracking shot following the feet.
- **Sound:** Footsteps on the beat.

### Shot 4 — Wide, slow tilt up
- **Timing:** 3.5 – 5.0 s
- **Action:** City street or park, trees and buildings backlit by the sun, lens flare across the frame.
- **Camera:** Tilt up from the ground to the sky.
- **Sound:** Light street ambience under the music.

### Shot 5 — Close-up, static (match cut on circle shape: sun → ...)
- **Timing:** 5.0 – 6.5 s
- **Action:** Hand holds up a round orange (or a cookie, a donut, a vinyl record) exactly where the sun was in the previous frame.
- **Camera:** Static, framed to match the sun's position in Shot 4.
- **Sound:** Soft "pop" accent.

### Shot 6 — Medium, handheld orbit
- **Timing:** 6.5 – 8.0 s
- **Action:** Friends laughing at an outdoor table, sun behind them.
- **Camera:** Quick handheld half-orbit.
- **Sound:** Faint laughter mixed under the music.

### Shot 7 — Wide, drone or high angle pull-back
- **Timing:** 8.0 – 10.0 s
- **Action:** Pull up and away from the scene to reveal the skyline or the shoreline at golden hour.
- **Camera:** Smooth rising pull-back.
- **On-screen text:** `the little things >` (bold white, center, 8.5 – 10.0 s)
- **Sound:** Music builds into the chorus.

### Shot 8 — Close-up, speed ramp
- **Timing:** 10.0 – 12.0 s
- **Action:** Silhouette walking against the setting sun, hair and clothes moving in the breeze. Speed ramps from normal to slow motion.
- **Camera:** Static, low angle, sun directly behind the subject.
- **Sound:** Music peaks.

---

## SCENE 3 — THE LOOP (12.0 – 15.0 s)

### Shot 9 — Extreme close-up, fast push-in
- **Timing:** 12.0 – 15.0 s
- **Action:** Back home at dusk. The same hand closes the same blinds from Shot 1. The last sliver of golden light narrows to a line, then disappears.
- **Camera:** Fast push-in toward the blinds, mirroring Shot 1, so the last frame cuts cleanly back to the first.
- **On-screen text:** `again tomorrow?` (small, lower-center, 12.5 – 14.5 s)
- **Dialogue / narration:** None
- **Sound:** Blinds snap shut exactly on the final beat, cutting straight back into the opening snap when the reel loops.
