**Golden Hour Loop** — a short, cool piece for Instagram

A city rooftop at the very end of the day. The sun is sinking between two glass towers, and everything it touches turns to honey: the concrete ledge, a row of potted plants, the rippled surface of a small rooftop pool. The air is warm and hazy. Far below, traffic sounds blur into a soft hum, and a laid-back beat with a deep bassline rolls underneath it all.

A young woman in a loose white shirt and sunglasses sits on the ledge with her legs crossed, a cold drink sweating in her hand. The breeze lifts her hair. She tilts her face up toward the last of the sun and closes her eyes. Nothing is urgent here.

Then the world begins to play. The pool's reflection of the skyline shimmers and starts to ripple upward into the sky, as if the city were melting into liquid gold. Ice cubes in her glass catch the light and scatter it into tiny rainbows across the wall. Birds sweep across the sky in a wide arc, and their flight path leaves a faint trail of light that fades a second later. Every movement lands on the beat.

The sun touches the horizon. For one moment the whole scene glows almost too bright — pure gold, every edge burning. The woman lowers her sunglasses, looks straight out with a small, knowing smile, and raises her glass.

The light dips. The sky turns violet, the first windows in the towers flicker on, and the city lights come up one by one in rhythm with the music. The final frame melts seamlessly back into the opening sunset, so the whole piece loops forever — an endless golden hour, made to be watched twice.
