# Golden Hour Loop: Operational Chunks

**Chunk 1: Rooftop at sunset**
- **Duration:** 3 s (0–3 s)
- **Visual:** Wide, slow push-in on a city rooftop at sunset. The sun sinks between two glass towers. Honey-gold light on the concrete ledge, potted plants and the rippled surface of a small rooftop pool. Warm haze.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Laid-back beat with a deep bassline starts. Distant traffic blurs into a soft hum.
- **Entities:**
  - *Two glass towers* framing the sun (same position in every chunk).
  - *Rooftop*: concrete ledge, row of potted plants, small pool.
- **Environment:** City rooftop, golden hour. Warm honey and amber palette, soft haze.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 5 end frame. The opening frame must match the final frame so the loop is seamless. Generate this frame first and use it as the anchor for both ends.
- **Source:** "A city rooftop at the very end of the day. The sun is sinking between two glass towers, and everything it touches turns to honey: the concrete ledge, a row of potted plants, the rippled surface of a small rooftop pool. The air is warm and hazy. Far below, traffic sounds blur into a soft hum, and a laid-back beat with a deep bassline rolls underneath it all."

**Chunk 2: The woman on the ledge**
- **Duration:** 3 s (3–6 s)
- **Visual:** Medium shot. A young woman sits cross-legged on the ledge in a loose white shirt and sunglasses, holding a cold drink with condensation on the glass. The breeze lifts her hair. She tilts her face up to the sun and closes her eyes.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Beat continues. Soft breeze.
- **Entities:**
  - *Woman*: young, loose white shirt, sunglasses, same face and hair throughout.
  - *Drink*: cold glass with ice cubes, condensation.
- **Environment:** Same rooftop and golden light as Chunk 1.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 1 (same rooftop, same sun position).
- **Source:** "A young woman in a loose white shirt and sunglasses sits on the ledge with her legs crossed, a cold drink sweating in her hand. The breeze lifts her hair. She tilts her face up toward the last of the sun and closes her eyes. Nothing is urgent here."

**Chunk 3: The world begins to play**
- **Duration:** 4 s (6–10 s), quick beat-synced cuts inside the chunk
- **Visual:** Three surreal beats, each cut on the beat:
  1. The pool's reflection of the skyline ripples upward into the sky, as if the city were melting into liquid gold.
  2. Close-up: ice cubes in her glass scatter tiny rainbows across the wall.
  3. Birds sweep across the sky in a wide arc, leaving a faint light trail that fades a second later.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Beat continues. Every visual movement lands on the beat. Subtle shimmer and whoosh sound accents.
- **Entities:** Woman (her glass and hands as in Chunk 2). Pool. Birds. Skyline and towers.
- **Environment:** Same rooftop, golden hour, with a surreal liquid-gold effect.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 2 (same glass, same woman, same light).
- **Source:** "Then the world begins to play. The pool's reflection of the skyline shimmers and starts to ripple upward into the sky, as if the city were melting into liquid gold. Ice cubes in her glass catch the light and scatter it into tiny rainbows across the wall. Birds sweep across the sky in a wide arc, and their flight path leaves a faint trail of light that fades a second later. Every movement lands on the beat."

**Chunk 4: Peak glow and the toast**
- **Duration:** 3 s (10–13 s)
- **Visual:** The sun touches the horizon and the scene blooms almost too bright: pure gold, every edge burning. The woman lowers her sunglasses, looks straight into the camera with a small, knowing smile, and raises her glass.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Musical peak or accent on the glass raise.
- **Entities:** Woman (same as Chunk 2, sunglasses lowered so her eyes are visible). Drink.
- **Environment:** Rooftop, sun on the horizon, overexposed golden bloom.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 2 (same woman, same clothes, same ledge position).
- **Source:** "The sun touches the horizon. For one moment the whole scene glows almost too bright — pure gold, every edge burning. The woman lowers her sunglasses, looks straight out with a small, knowing smile, and raises her glass."

**Chunk 5: Dusk and loop back**
- **Duration:** 2–3 s (13–15/16 s)
- **Visual:** The light dips and the sky turns violet. Windows in the towers flicker on, and city lights come up one by one in rhythm with the beat. The last frame melts seamlessly back into the opening sunset frame of Chunk 1.
- **On-screen text:** None
- **Audio:** Beat continues. The music must also loop: the last bar flows back into the first bar with no gap or pop.
- **Entities:** Towers, city lights, rooftop.
- **Environment:** Dusk, violet sky, city lights, then a transition back to golden hour.
- **Depends on:** Chunk 4 (continuous light change). Chunk 1 start frame (the final frame must match it for the loop).
- **Source:** "The light dips. The sky turns violet, the first windows in the towers flicker on, and the city lights come up one by one in rhythm with the music. The final frame melts seamlessly back into the opening sunset, so the whole piece loops forever — an endless golden hour, made to be watched twice."
