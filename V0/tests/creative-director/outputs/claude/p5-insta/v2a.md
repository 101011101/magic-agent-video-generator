# Video Script Brief: Short Video for Instagram

## Goal and core idea
Create a short, eye-catching video for the user to post on their personal Instagram. The request gives no subject, so the goal is simply to produce something visually striking that stops the scroll and is fun to watch and share. Core idea (assumed): a satisfying, visually surprising loop or transformation that feels "cool" on its own with no context needed.

## Audience and platform
- **Audience:** The user's Instagram followers — general audience, mostly friends and acquaintances; assume young adults.
- **Platform:** Instagram (stated). Assume Reels.

## Format, duration and aspect ratio
- **Format:** Short-form Reel, a few quick shots or one continuous seamless shot designed to loop.
- **Duration:** Not specified. Assume 8–15 seconds.
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (Instagram Reels standard), 1080×1920. Keep key action in the centre so the 4:5 feed preview and 1:1 grid crop still look good.

## Tone and style
- Not specified. Assume: bold, modern, visually satisfying, high-contrast, cinematic.
- Hook in the first 1 second; strong visual payoff; seamless loop at the end so it replays naturally.
- Works with the sound off; pair with an upbeat, trendy-sounding music bed when sound is on.
- No dialogue or voiceover.

## User requirements (quoted exactly)
- "make something cool for my insta"

## Uploads and how they must be used
- **Uploads:** None.
- All visuals must be newly created. Do not show any specific real person, as the user did not provide photos of themselves or anyone else. Do not use real brands, logos or copyrighted characters.

## Assumptions (not stated by the user)
- Subject/concept (pick one and commit; the scriptwriter may propose the strongest):
  - A seamless "infinite zoom" from a city skyline into a single window and out into space, looping back to the city; or
  - A surreal transformation, e.g. an ordinary object morphing through several striking forms; or
  - A dreamy aesthetic scene (neon city at night in rain, golden-hour landscape) with smooth cinematic camera movement.
- Personal (not business) account.
- No on-screen text, or at most a short caption-style line; the user will write their own Instagram caption.
- Music: generic royalty-free upbeat electronic or lo-fi track; the user may swap in a trending Instagram sound.
- Safe for a general audience.

## Missing information only the user can supply
- What the video should be about (a topic, interest, event, place, product, or mood).
- Whether it is for a personal account, a creator account, or a business.
- Whether the user (or anyone else) should appear in it, and if so, photos or videos of them.
- Preferred style or vibe (e.g. funny, aesthetic, cinematic, hype, calm) and any reference Reels they like.
- Desired length.
- Any text, caption, handle, or music they want included.
- Any of their own photos or clips to use as material.
