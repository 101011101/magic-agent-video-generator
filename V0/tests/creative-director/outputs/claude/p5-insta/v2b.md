# Script: "Infinite Window" — Instagram Reel

**Concept:** One seamless infinite zoom. We start on a neon city at night, dive into a single lit window, through a tiny world inside it, out into space — and land back on the same city. It loops perfectly, so people watch it two or three times before they realise it's restarted.

**Format:** Single continuous zoom (built from linked segments with invisible transitions), designed to loop
**Duration:** 12 seconds
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920. All key action dead-centre so the 4:5 feed preview and 1:1 grid crop still work.
**Audio:** Royalty-free upbeat electronic track (~120 BPM) with a riser and drop. Swappable for a trending Instagram sound. No dialogue, no voiceover. Works fully with the sound off.
**On-screen text:** None.

---

## Beat-by-beat

| Time | Visual | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:01** — Hook | Already moving: a rain-soaked neon city at night, seen from above. Pink and cyan signs glow (no readable words), reflections shimmer on wet streets. The camera is already zooming forward fast toward one tall tower. | Music already playing mid-phrase. |
| **0:01–0:03** | The zoom locks onto a single warm-yellow window high on the tower, the only lit one on its floor. Rain streaks past the lens. | Riser begins. |
| **0:03–0:05** | We pass through the glass. Inside: a cosy dark room, and on the desk, a glowing snow globe. The camera doesn't stop — it dives straight into the globe. | Glass "shimmer" whoosh. |
| **0:05–0:07** | Inside the globe: a tiny snowy forest under the northern lights. Green and violet aurora ripples overhead. The camera rushes up into the aurora. | Riser peaks. |
| **0:07–0:09** | The aurora opens into deep space: a field of stars, then a slowly turning planet streaked with pink and cyan light. | **Drop.** Bass hits. |
| **0:09–0:11** | The camera dives toward the planet's night side. The pink and cyan lights resolve into city grids — and then into the same rain-soaked neon city from the opening. | Beat continues. |
| **0:11–0:12** — Loop point | The final frame matches the first frame exactly: same city, same angle, same zoom speed, rain mid-fall. | Music cut on the downbeat so the restart hits on beat. |

## Generation prompts (per segment)

Each segment's last frame becomes the next segment's first frame. Constant forward zoom speed throughout; no cuts.

1. *Aerial view of a rain-soaked neon city at night, pink and cyan glow, wet streets reflecting light, camera zooming fast toward one tall tower, then onto a single warm-yellow lit window. No readable text or logos. Cinematic, high contrast, 9:16.*
2. *Camera passes through a rainy window into a cosy dark room and dives straight into a glowing snow globe on a desk. Continuous forward motion.*
3. *Inside the snow globe: a tiny snowy forest under bright green and violet northern lights; camera rushes upward into the aurora, which opens into a deep field of stars.*
4. *A slowly turning planet in space streaked with pink and cyan lights; camera dives toward the night side as the lights resolve into a rain-soaked neon city seen from above, matching the opening frame exactly.*

**Avoid:** real people, real brands, logos, readable signs or text, copyrighted characters, visible cuts or jumps in zoom speed.

**Loop check:** Play the last 0.5 s into the first 0.5 s. The motion must feel continuous with no jump in colour, angle or speed.
