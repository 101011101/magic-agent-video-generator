# THE KEEPER'S SHELL

*A short film. Approx. 2:00. No dialogue — score only.*

**Visual style:** Painterly, like a moving oil painting. Visible brushstrokes, soft edges, muted blues and greys, with warm amber from the lamp and a cool pale-teal glow from the shell. The shell is the only thing that ever glows that colour.
**Score:** Solo cello and piano at the core, with a soft choir/drone that enters with the shell. Melancholy, restrained, swelling only in the storm.
**Main character:** THE KEEPER — a woman in her late 50s, weathered face, grey hair tied back, heavy navy wool sweater, long dark oilskin coat for the storm. Same look in every scene.
**Location:** A lone white lighthouse with a red lamp room on a rocky point, a small stone keeper's cottage at its base, a grey-sand beach below.

---

## SCENE 1 — DAWN ON THE BEACH (0:00–0:22)

**1.** *Wide.* Dawn. Pale pink and grey sky over a flat sea. The lighthouse stands on the point, its lamp turning slowly, fading against the brightening sky.
*Score: a single low cello note, held.*

**2.** *Medium.* The Keeper walks along the waterline, coat pulled close, collecting driftwood into a canvas bag. Her footprints trail behind her.

**3.** *Close.* Something in the wet sand catches the light — a spiral shell, glowing a faint pale teal from inside.

**4.** *Close on her face.* She stops. Curiosity, a touch of wonder.

**5.** *Insert.* Her weathered hand lifts the shell from the sand. The glow pulses softly once, like a heartbeat.
*Score: a high, soft choir tone enters for the first time — the shell's theme.*

---

## SCENE 2 — BRINGING IT HOME (0:22–0:40)

**6.** *Wide.* She climbs the path up to the cottage, the shell held carefully in both hands.

**7.** *Interior, cottage — medium.* A small, sparse room: a wooden table, a kettle, a window facing the tower. She places the shell on the windowsill. Morning light. The glow is almost invisible in daylight.

**8.** *Montage, soft dissolves.* Her ordinary day: polishing the great lens in the lamp room, writing in the logbook, eating alone at the table. Each time, her eyes drift back to the shell on the sill.
*Score: piano, gentle and lonely.*

---

## SCENE 3 — NIGHT, THE HUM (0:40–1:00)

**9.** *Interior, cottage — night.* Darkness. The shell now glows clearly, teal light spilling across the sill and table. A visible ripple pulses through the glow, as if it is humming.
*Score: the choir tone returns, lower, with a vibrating drone underneath.*

**10.** *Close.* The Keeper wakes in bed, turns toward the light.

**11.** *Close.* The water in a glass on the table trembles in rings.

**12.** *Wide, exterior.* The lighthouse against a darkening sky. The beam sweeps once… flickers… and goes out.
*Score: everything cuts to silence.*

**13.** *Medium.* The Keeper at the window, staring at the dead tower. Wind rises. Rain begins to streak the glass. Far out at sea, the tiny lights of ships, pitching on the swell.

---

## SCENE 4 — THE STORM CLIMB (1:00–1:35)

**14.** *Medium.* She pulls on her oilskin coat. Hesitates. Then takes the glowing shell from the sill.
*Score: low cello pulse, building.*

**15.** *Wide.* She crosses from the cottage to the tower in the storm, bent into the wind, rain lashing, the shell glowing in her fist — the only light in the frame.

**16.** *Interior, tower — low angle.* The spiral staircase winding up into blackness. She climbs. The shell's teal light paints the curved walls as she rises.

**17.** *Close.* Her face — wet, determined, breathing hard.

**18.** *Wide, exterior.* Huge waves smash against the rocks. A ship's silhouette leans dangerously close to the point.
*Score: full strings and choir swell.*

**19.** *Interior, lamp room.* Glass rattling, rain streaming down the windows. The great lens sits dark. She sets the shell into the heart of the lamp.

**20.** *Extreme close.* The shell's glow brightens… and brightens… passing into the lens.

**21.** *Wide.* The lamp blazes back to life — the beam now warm gold edged with teal — and sweeps out across the storm.
*Score: the shell's theme played at full strength.*

**22.** *Wide, sea.* The beam crosses the water. The ship turns away from the rocks. Then another. Their lights move safely into open water.

---

## SCENE 5 — MORNING AGAIN (1:35–2:00)

**23.** *Wide.* Dawn. The storm has passed. Calm sea, pale pink light — mirroring the opening shot. The lamp turns quietly.
*Score: solo piano, slow.*

**24.** *Interior, lamp room.* The Keeper, exhausted, lifts the shell from the lamp. Its glow is softer now.

**25.** *Medium.* She walks down to the beach in the same place she found it.

**26.** *Close.* She kneels and places the shell back in the wet sand. She holds her hand over it for a moment. A small, sad smile.

**27.** *Close.* A gentle wave washes over the shell. When the water pulls back, the glow fades… and the shell is gone.

**28.** *Wide, final.* The Keeper stands alone at the waterline, the lighthouse behind her, as the sun rises fully. She turns and walks back toward the tower. Her footprints trail behind her, as in the first scene.
*Score: the shell's theme, once, very quietly, on solo cello. Fade to black.*

**TITLE CARD:** *The Keeper's Shell*

### Production notes
- **Continuity:** Keep the Keeper's face, hair, sweater and coat identical across all scenes; generate a character reference image first and use it for every shot. Do the same for the shell (spiral shape, pale teal glow) and the lighthouse.
- **Colour logic:** Teal = the shell / the magic. Amber = the lamp / human duty. In the relit beam, both colours combine.
- **Pacing:** 28 shots in 2 minutes means an average of ~4 seconds per shot. Hold longer on the quiet moments (Scenes 1, 2 and 5) and cut faster in the storm (Scene 4).
- **Open question:** Your outline doesn't say *why* the shell makes the lamp go out. I've left it ambiguous (the shell's hum "pulls" the light into itself, then gives it back). If you'd prefer a clearer cause, let me know.
