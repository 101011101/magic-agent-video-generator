# THE SHELL

*A wordless short film — 2:00*

**Format:** Narrative short, no dialogue, score + natural sound
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 (optional 2.39:1 letterbox)
**Style:** Painterly animation — a moving oil/gouache painting. Visible brushstrokes, soft edges, rich atmospheric colour, consistent in every shot.

---

## Consistency bible

**MARGARET (the keeper)** — Woman in her late fifties. Weathered, wind-reddened face, grey-streaked dark hair pinned in a loose bun. Cream cable-knit wool sweater, dark grey wool skirt, brown leather boots. In the storm: a long mustard-yellow oilskin coat with hood. Moves slowly and deliberately; alone for a long time.

**THE LIGHTHOUSE** — White stone tower with a single red band below the lantern room, on a rocky headland. Black iron gallery rail. Brass lamp with a great glass Fresnel lens. Inside: a white spiral stone staircase with an iron handrail.

**THE COTTAGE** — Low stone cottage attached to the tower base. One room: small iron stove, wooden table, one chair, a windowsill facing the sea, an oil lamp.

**THE SHELL** — Palm-sized spiral seashell, pale ivory with an opal sheen. Glows a soft, cool **blue-green**. When it hums, the glow pulses slowly like breathing. The colour, pulse and hum never change across the film.

**COLOUR ARC** — Pale pink/blue dawn → warm amber interior → blue-black night (shell glow) → grey-green storm (gold beam) → washed gold morning.

---

## 1. EXT. BEACH BELOW THE LIGHTHOUSE — DAWN (0:00–0:20)

**SHOT 1** (0:00–0:07) — Wide. Pale pink and blue sky. The lighthouse stands on the headland, its beam making a last slow sweep before it fades into daylight. Small waves fold onto grey sand.
*Score: A single solo cello note, long and low. Soft waves.*

**SHOT 2** (0:07–0:12) — Medium, tracking. MARGARET walks the waterline alone, hands in her sweater sleeves, head down. Her footprints trail behind her.

**SHOT 3** (0:12–0:16) — Her POV. Among wet stones and kelp, something glows faintly blue-green beneath a thin wash of water.

**SHOT 4** (0:16–0:20) — Close-up. She kneels and lifts THE SHELL. The glow lights her palm and the underside of her face. Her expression: wonder, very quiet.
*Score: A high, glassy tone joins the cello — the shell's motif.*

## 2. INT. COTTAGE — DAY INTO EVENING (0:20–0:35)

**SHOT 5** (0:20–0:25) — Wide. Margaret enters the amber-lit cottage, shell cupped in both hands, and closes the door on the wind.

**SHOT 6** (0:25–0:30) — Medium. She places the shell on the windowsill, facing the sea, and adjusts it slightly — as if it should be able to see the water.

**SHOT 7** (0:30–0:35) — Time passes in one held frame: she eats alone at the table; the light outside the window goes from gold to deep blue. The shell glows steadily on the sill.
*Score: Gentle piano joins, a simple, lonely melody.*

## 3. INT./EXT. COTTAGE AND LIGHTHOUSE — NIGHT (0:35–0:55)

**SHOT 8** (0:35–0:41) — Close-up. In the dark cottage, the shell begins to pulse — slowly, like breathing. A low hum rises. Margaret, asleep in the chair, stirs.
*Sound: The shell's hum, low and resonant.*

**SHOT 9** (0:41–0:46) — Wide exterior. The lighthouse beam sweeps the sea. On each pulse of the shell (seen as a faint blue-green glow in the cottage window below), the beam weakens.

**SHOT 10** (0:46–0:51) — Close-up, lantern room. The lamp flickers. Thin threads of golden light drift out of it, as if drawn away. The flame dies.

**SHOT 11** (0:51–0:55) — Wide. The lighthouse stands dark. Only the shell's small glow in the window remains, and beyond it, the black sea.
*Score: Music drops out. Only wind.*

## 4. EXT. SEA / INT. LIGHTHOUSE STAIRS — STORM (0:55–1:25)

**SHOT 12** (0:55–1:01) — Wide, sea. The storm breaks: grey-green waves, slanting rain. Two small fishing boats pitch toward the black rocks, their lanterns tiny and swinging.
*Score: Low strings swell; timpani rumble under the thunder.*

**SHOT 13** (1:01–1:05) — Interior. Margaret wakes, sees the dark tower through the window, then the ships' lights. She looks at the shell.

**SHOT 14** (1:05–1:09) — Fast. She pulls on the mustard oilskin, grabs the shell, and opens the door into the storm. Rain blasts in.

**SHOT 15** (1:09–1:15) — Low angle, spiral stairs. She climbs, one hand on the iron rail, the shell held against her chest. Its blue-green glow lights the curved white walls as she rises.

**SHOT 16** (1:15–1:19) — Exterior. A wave crashes against the tower base; the whole frame shudders.

**SHOT 17** (1:19–1:25) — Close-up. She stops halfway, breathing hard, and looks up the endless spiral. The shell pulses faster. She keeps climbing.
*Score: Building, quickening strings.*

## 5. INT. LANTERN ROOM — STORM (1:25–1:40)

**SHOT 18** (1:25–1:30) — Medium. She bursts into the lantern room. Rain lashes the glass. The great dark lens towers over her.

**SHOT 19** (1:30–1:35) — Close-up. She holds the shell to the cold lamp. The shell's blue-green light flows out in thin threads into the lamp — the reverse of shot 10 — and slowly turns gold.

**SHOT 20** (1:35–1:40) — Wide. The lamp blazes. The lens begins to turn. A gold beam cuts out across the storm.
*Score: Full orchestral swell, the shell's glassy motif inside it.*

## 6. EXT. SEA — STORM (1:40–1:50)

**SHOT 21** (1:40–1:45) — Wide. The gold beam sweeps over the two fishing boats. Their lanterns turn; the boats veer away from the rocks.

**SHOT 22** (1:45–1:50) — Medium, lantern room. Margaret, soaked, watches through the streaming glass. In her hand, the shell's glow is now very faint.
*Score: Strings settle into a soft resolution.*

## 7. EXT. BEACH — MORNING (1:50–2:00)

**SHOT 23** (1:50–1:55) — Wide, mirrors shot 1. Calm, washed-gold morning. The sea is flat. Margaret walks down to the waterline, the shell in her palm, its glow barely there.

**SHOT 24** (1:55–1:58) — Close-up. She kneels and sets the shell in the shallows where she found it. A small wave washes over it. For a moment, it glows blue-green again.

**SHOT 25** (1:58–2:00) — Wide. She stands and watches the tide take the shell. The lighthouse stands behind her. Hold.
*Score: The solo cello returns with the single long note from the opening, then silence.*

**FADE OUT.**
