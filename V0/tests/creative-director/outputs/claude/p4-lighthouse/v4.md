# THE KEEPER'S SHELL — Shooting Script

**Runtime:** 2:00 (120 seconds)
**Format:** 2.39:1 widescreen
**Style:** Painterly. Every frame should feel like a moving oil painting: visible brush texture, soft edges, rich impasto highlights on water and light. Palette moves from pale rose and grey-blue (dawn) to warm amber (home) to deep indigo and slate (night/storm) and back to soft gold (morning).
**Dialogue:** None. **On-screen text:** Title and end card only.
**Score:** Solo cello and piano, with a low string pad. One recurring motif, "the shell theme": a simple four-note rising phrase on celesta or glass harmonica. It belongs to the shell and appears whenever the shell glows.

**Character:** THE KEEPER, a woman in her sixties, weathered face, grey hair tied back, heavy wool sweater, oilskin coat, rubber boots. Same look in every shot.
**The shell:** A palm-sized spiral shell, pale pearl-white, that glows from within with a soft blue-green light. Same shape and glow color in every shot.
**The lighthouse:** White tower with a single faded red band, on a rocky point. A small stone cottage sits at its base.

## SCENE 1 — EXT. BEACH BELOW THE LIGHTHOUSE — DAWN (0:00 – 0:22)

**Shot 1 — Extreme wide, static** | 0:00 – 0:07
- **Action:** Pale dawn over a grey sea. The lighthouse stands on the point, its lamp turning one last slow sweep before it switches off for the day. Tiny in the frame, the Keeper walks along the tideline.
- **Camera:** Locked-off.
- **On-screen text:** Title fades in at 0:02, out at 0:06: **THE KEEPER'S SHELL**
- **Sound:** Soft waves, distant gulls, wind. Score: a single sustained cello note enters.

**Shot 2 — Medium, slow tracking (left to right)** | 0:07 – 0:12
- **Action:** The Keeper walks, head down, scanning the sand. She stops.
- **Camera:** Slow lateral track alongside her, then stops when she stops.
- **Sound:** Boots in wet sand. Piano joins the cello, slow and sparse.

**Shot 3 — Close-up, static, low angle at sand level** | 0:12 – 0:16
- **Action:** Among seaweed and pebbles, the spiral shell pulses with a faint blue-green glow as a wave retreats from around it.
- **Camera:** Static, lens almost touching the sand.
- **Sound:** Hiss of receding water. **Shell theme** plays for the first time, quietly, on celesta.

**Shot 4 — Close-up, slow push-in** | 0:16 – 0:22
- **Action:** Her weathered hands lift the shell. The glow lights her fingers and then her face as she raises it. Her expression: curiosity, a trace of loneliness.
- **Camera:** Slow push-in from hands to face.
- **Sound:** Waves soften. Cello holds under the shell theme.

## SCENE 4 — EXT./INT. LIGHTHOUSE — STORM (0:56 – 1:24)

**Shot 12 — Wide, handheld** | 0:56 – 1:01
- **Action:** She crosses the rocks from cottage to tower in driving rain, coat whipping, the glowing shell clutched to her chest. Waves explode against the rocks behind her.
- **Camera:** Handheld, buffeted, following.
- **Sound:** Full storm: wind howl, rain, crashing surf. Cello begins a driving ostinato.

**Shot 13 — Low angle, static** | 1:01 – 1:04
- **Action:** She hauls the heavy tower door open against the wind and disappears inside.
- **Camera:** Static, looking up the tower.
- **Sound:** Heavy iron door groan and slam.

**Shot 14 — Overhead, looking straight down the spiral stair, static** | 1:04 – 1:10
- **Action:** The spiral staircase coils down into darkness. The only light is the shell's blue-green glow, rising as she climbs, step after step.
- **Camera:** Locked-off top-down.
- **Sound:** Boots on iron steps, her breathing, wind moaning through the tower. Ostinato builds.

**Shot 15 — Medium close-up, tracking up the stairs** | 1:10 – 1:16
- **Action:** She climbs, exhausted, gripping the rail. She slips, catches herself, keeps going. The shell's glow grows brighter with each turn.
- **Camera:** Tracks up the stairwell just ahead of her.
- **Sound:** Thunder shakes the tower. Strings swell. Shell theme threads through the ostinato.

**Shot 16 — Wide, static** | 1:16 – 1:24
- **Action:** The lamp room. Rain lashes the glass on all sides. The great lens is dark. Through the glass, a ship's lights pitch dangerously close to the rocks. She stands before the lens, holding the shell.
- **Camera:** Locked-off, wide enough to show the lens, her, and the ship beyond.
- **Sound:** Storm at full force. Score holds one tense, unresolved chord.

## Continuity notes

- Keeper's face, hair, clothes, and age stay identical in every shot.
- The shell keeps the same shape, size, and blue-green glow throughout. Its glow intensity tells the story: faint (found), soft (home), strong (hum), blazing (relight), dim (return).
- Shots 1/21, 2/22, and 3/23 are mirrored pairs. Match framing, lens, and composition so the ending clearly echoes the opening.
- The painterly texture stays consistent across day, night, and storm.
