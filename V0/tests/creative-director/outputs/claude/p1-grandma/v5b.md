# Grandma's Wave — Operational Chunks

## Baseline (first frame)

The first frame must match the uploaded photo exactly. It is the source image, not a re-creation.

- **Character — Grandma** (must stay identical throughout): elderly woman, short white hair, round glasses, green cardigan buttoned to the middle (slightly fuzzy wool at the cuffs). Same face, same identity, same proportions as the photo. Expression: neutral, looking straight at the camera. Hands resting on the arms of the chair.
- **Objects:** armchair (she sits deep in the cushions).
- **Environment:** sunlit living room, window behind/beside her, dust drifting slowly in a beam of light.
- **Time of day:** afternoon.
- **Lighting:** warm afternoon sun through the window, a warm stripe across the armchair, highlights in her white hair, two small window reflections in her glasses.
- **Palette:** warm golds and soft greens, as in the photo.
- **Style:** photorealistic, matching the photo; static camera (no camera movement, no zoom).
- **Audio:** quiet room tone (no music, no dialogue).
- **Aspect ratio:** same as the uploaded photo (no crop).
- **Total duration:** 5 seconds.

## Chunks

**Chunk 1 — Stillness**
- Quote: "For a heartbeat she is still, looking straight ahead with that calm, neutral expression of someone waiting for the photo to be over."
- Signpost: implicit (opening)
- Changes: none to Grandma. Only ambient motion: dust: static → drifting slowly in the light.
- Type: continuous
- Duration: ~0.8 s
- On-screen text and audio: room tone starts.
- Depends on: none (starts from the baseline / uploaded photo)

**Chunk 2 — The smile**
- Quote: "Then something changes. The corners of her eyes crinkle first, behind the glasses, and the smile follows — slow and genuine, lifting her cheeks and softening her whole face. It is the smile of someone who has just recognized a person she loves."
- Signpost: "Then something changes"
- Changes:
  - eyes: neutral → crinkled at the corners (first)
  - mouth: neutral → slow, genuine smile (after the eyes)
  - cheeks: relaxed → lifted
  - overall expression: neutral → warm recognition
- Type: continuous
- Duration: ~1.2 s
- Depends on: Chunk 1

**Chunk 3 — The wave**
- Quote: "Her right hand rises from the arm of the chair. It lifts to about shoulder height, palm open, and she waves — two or three gentle side-to-side movements, unhurried, the way grandmothers wave from a doorway. Her head tilts very slightly as she does it. The cardigan sleeve slides a little down her wrist."
- Signpost: implicit
- Changes:
  - right hand: resting on chair arm → raised to shoulder height, palm open → 2–3 gentle side-to-side waves
  - head: upright → tilted very slightly
  - right cardigan sleeve: at wrist → slid a little down the wrist
- Type: continuous
- Duration: ~2.0 s
- Depends on: Chunk 2 (smile is held throughout)

**Chunk 4 — Final hold**
- Quote: "She holds the smile as the wave settles, her hand pausing in the air for a final second. The room stays quiet and golden around her. Nothing else moves except the drifting dust and the warmth in her face."
- Signpost: implicit
- Changes:
  - right hand: waving → still, paused in the air at shoulder height
- Type: continuous
- Duration: ~1.0 s
- On-screen text and audio: room tone ends with the video at 5 s.
- Depends on: Chunk 3

Total: ~5 seconds, one continuous shot from the uploaded photo.
