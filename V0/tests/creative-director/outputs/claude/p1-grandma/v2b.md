# Script: "Grandma Waves"

**Format:** Single continuous shot, image-to-video from the uploaded photo
**Duration:** ~5 seconds
**Aspect ratio:** Match the source photo (fallback: closest of 4:3 / 3:4, no cropping of face, hands or glasses)
**Audio:** None (optional: very quiet room ambience only — no music, no voice)
**On-screen text:** None

---

## Shot 1 — Continuous (0:00–0:05)

**Setting (unchanged from photo):** A sunlit living room. An elderly woman with short white hair, round glasses and a green cardigan sits in an armchair, facing the camera. Lighting, colours, grain and framing stay identical to the original photo throughout.

**Camera:** Locked off. At most, an almost-imperceptible slow push-in. No pans, no reveals beyond the original frame.

| Time | Action | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:01** | Opens on the photo exactly as uploaded. She holds her neutral expression, looking into the lens. A soft breath lifts her shoulders slightly. | First frame must be pixel-faithful to the photo. |
| **0:01–0:02** | A slow, natural blink. The corners of her mouth begin to lift; her cheeks rise and the skin around her eyes softens into gentle crinkles behind the glasses. | Smile builds gradually, not all at once. Closed-mouth or a slight open smile — don't invent prominent teeth. |
| **0:02–0:03** | The smile settles into a full, warm expression. She lifts her right hand (or whichever hand is most visible and free) from the armrest/lap to about shoulder height, palm toward camera. | Movement is slow and unhurried, appropriate for her age. Cardigan sleeve moves naturally with the arm. |
| **0:03–0:04.5** | She gives a gentle wave — two or three soft side-to-side motions of the hand, fingers relaxed. A tiny tilt of the head toward the camera as she waves. Eye contact never breaks. | No exaggerated or cartoonish motion. Fingers must stay anatomically correct. |
| **0:04.5–0:05** | Her hand lowers and comes to rest back on the armrest/lap. She holds the warm smile, eyes on the camera. Sunlight shifts very faintly across the room. | Calm, still end frame on the smile — suitable for sharing or looping. |

---

## Generation prompt (single shot)

> Animate this photo. The elderly woman with short white hair, round glasses and a green cardigan stays seated in her armchair in the sunlit living room, looking at the camera. She takes a soft breath, blinks slowly, and a warm, gentle smile spreads across her face. She slowly raises her right hand and gives a soft, friendly wave toward the camera, then lowers her hand to rest and keeps smiling. Subtle, natural, unhurried movement. Photorealistic, faithful to the original photo's lighting, colours and grain. Static camera, very slight slow push-in at most.

**Negative / avoid:** changing her face or identity, altering hair, glasses or clothing, new people or objects, background changes, text or watermarks, talking or lip-sync movement, fast or jerky motion, exaggerated expressions, distorted hands or extra fingers, camera moves that reveal outside the original frame.

---

## Open questions for you

- **Where will you share it?** This decides the final aspect ratio and whether music makes sense.
- **Which hand** should she wave with, if it matters?
- **Sound or music?** A song that meant something to her could be lovely — or keep it silent.
- **Any text?** For example her name or a short message at the end.
- **Ending:** hold on the smile (current plan), or ease back to her neutral expression so it loops seamlessly?
- **Tone:** is this a memorial piece? If so, I'd soften the treatment slightly — a gentler, more nostalgic pace and possibly a slow fade at the end.
