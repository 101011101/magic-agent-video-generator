- Copy this string character for character. Note the **em dash (—)** after "Sweet Crumbs" and the **en dash (–)** in "7am–3pm".

- "St" has no period.
- Suggested layout: "Sweet Crumbs" larger on the first line, "Open 7am–3pm, 12 Elm St" smaller below it. If you split it across two lines like this, keep the em dash at the end of line one or confirm you're OK dropping it — otherwise keep it as one line.
