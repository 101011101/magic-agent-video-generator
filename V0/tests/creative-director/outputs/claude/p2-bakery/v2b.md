# Script: Sweet Crumbs — "First Batch" (20 s)

**Format:** Short commercial, 6 shots + end card
**Duration:** 20 seconds (≈16 s footage + 4 s end card)
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical; all key action and text inside the central safe area (crops cleanly to 4:5 and 1:1)
**Audio:** Light, upbeat acoustic guitar track (warm, unhurried, ~95 BPM) with subtle ambient sound effects. No voiceover, no dialogue. Fully understandable with sound off.
**On-screen text:** End card only. No legible signs, labels or packaging text inside any generated footage.

---

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Dawn outside (0:00–0:02.5)
**Visual:** Exterior of a small neighbourhood bakery at sunrise. Soft golden light hits a large front window; inside, warm lights glow. The street is quiet. Slow push-in toward the window. No readable sign — the window is framed so any signage is out of shot or softly out of focus.
**Sound:** Music fades in. Faint birdsong.

### Shot 2 — Hands at work (0:02.5–0:05)
**Visual:** Close-up of a baker's floured hands kneading dough on a wooden counter. A puff of flour drifts up through a beam of morning light.
**Sound:** Soft thump of dough on wood.

### Shot 3 — Out of the oven (0:05–0:08)
**Visual:** Oven door opens; a tray of golden croissants slides out toward camera. Steam curls up and catches the light. Slow motion.
**Sound:** Oven door creak, gentle whoosh of warm air.

### Shot 4 — Texture close-ups (0:08–0:11)
Two quick macro beats (1.5 s each):
- **4a:** A croissant torn in half, flaky layers pulling apart, crumbs falling.
- **4b:** Icing drizzled over a warm cinnamon roll, glistening.
**Sound:** Soft crackle of pastry; music lifts.

### Shot 5 — The counter (0:11–0:14)
**Visual:** A display case filled with muffins, bread loaves, cookies and pastries in warm light. A hand places a final loaf on the shelf. No price tags or labels visible.
**Sound:** Music continues.

### Shot 6 — First customer (0:14–0:16)
**Visual:** The door opens (bell above it swings), a smiling customer steps in. Cut to a mid-shot of a cup of coffee and a croissant on a small plate, slid across the counter toward camera. Warm, welcoming smile from the baker, slightly out of focus behind.
**Sound:** Door bell jingle. Coffee cup set down on saucer.

### End card (0:16–0:20)
**Visual:** Clean, warm neutral background (soft cream or light toast colour), optionally with a subtle blurred still of the pastry case behind it. Text appears with a gentle fade-in and holds, fully static, for at least 3 seconds.

**Text (rendered as a typographic overlay — not generated in AI imagery), character for character:**

> Sweet Crumbs — Open 7am–3pm, 12 Elm St

**Typography:** Friendly serif or soft hand-lettered-style font in a warm dark brown. "Sweet Crumbs" may be set larger on its own line, with "— Open 7am–3pm, 12 Elm St" beneath, *only if* the line break keeps every character exactly as written (em dash after "Sweet Crumbs", en dash in "7am–3pm", comma after "3pm", "St" with no period).
**Sound:** Music resolves on a soft final chord and fades out by 0:20.
