# Video Script Brief: Sweet Crumbs Bakery Ad

## Goal and core idea
Create a short promotional ad for a local bakery called **Sweet Crumbs**. The ad must make viewers crave fresh-baked goods and want to visit, and it must leave them knowing exactly when the bakery is open and where it is. Core idea: fresh, warm, homemade baking every morning, just down the street — come in.

## Audience and platform
- **Audience:** Local residents, commuters and passers-by near 12 Elm St; people looking for breakfast, coffee-and-pastry, or baked treats. Broad adult audience.
- **Platform:** Not specified. Assume social media (Instagram/Facebook Reels, TikTok) and possibly local digital displays. Must work with the sound off.

## Format, duration and aspect ratio
- **Format:** Short commercial: a sequence of short shots of the bakery and its products, ending on a static end card.
- **Duration:** 20 seconds total (stated by user). Suggested split: ~16 s of footage, ~4 s end card (end card must stay on screen long enough to read comfortably).
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical (assumed, for social). Keep key action and all text inside a central safe area so a 1:1 or 4:5 crop also works.

## Tone and style
- Warm, inviting, cosy, appetising, neighbourhood-friendly.
- Visual style: soft morning light, golden tones, close-ups of textures (flaky crusts, steam, dusting of sugar, crumb), hands at work (kneading, sliding trays from the oven), a welcoming storefront/counter.
- Pacing: gentle but steady; 5–7 shots before the end card.
- Audio: light, upbeat acoustic music; optional subtle ambient sounds (oven door, bell over the door). No voiceover required (see assumptions).

## User requirements (quoted exactly)
- "ad for my bakery"
- "20 sec"
- "the end card must say exactly 'Sweet Crumbs — Open 7am–3pm, 12 Elm St'"
- "dont spell anything wrong"

## Critical text rule
The end card text must be reproduced **character for character**:

> Sweet Crumbs — Open 7am–3pm, 12 Elm St

- Keep the em dash (—) after "Sweet Crumbs" and the en dash (–) in "7am–3pm".
- Keep lowercase "am"/"pm" with no spaces or periods, the comma after "3pm", and "St" without a period.
- Do not add, remove, reword, abbreviate or re-punctuate anything.
- The end card text should be rendered as a crisp text overlay/graphic, not generated inside AI imagery, so it cannot be misspelled.
- Any other on-screen text in the ad must also be spelled correctly. Prefer minimal or no other on-screen text to reduce risk; the bakery name, if shown elsewhere, must be exactly "Sweet Crumbs".
- No legible signage, labels or text should appear inside generated footage (e.g. storefront signs, packaging), since it may be misspelled.

## Uploads and how they must be used
- **Uploads:** None.
- All visuals must be newly created. Since there is no logo, storefront photo or product photo, the bakery and its products must be generic and must not imply a specific real location or brand look.

## Assumptions (not stated by the user)
- Products shown: a general mix of common bakery items — croissants, bread loaves, muffins, cinnamon rolls, cookies, coffee alongside pastries.
- Emphasis on mornings (fits the 7am opening): sunrise light, first batch out of the oven, early customers.
- No voiceover and no dialogue; music only. Message carried visually plus the end card.
- No logo exists; the end card uses clean, warm typography (e.g. a friendly serif or hand-lettered-style font) on a warm neutral or brand-free background.
- No prices, offers or promotions.
- Target region is English-speaking; time format as given by user.
- People shown (baker, customers) are generic; no specific identity.

## Missing information only the user can supply
- Logo, brand colours or fonts, if they have them.
- Real photos or footage of the shop, storefront, staff or products (to make the ad look like their actual bakery).
- Signature or best-selling products to feature.
- Where the ad will run (to confirm aspect ratio and whether sound will be heard).
- Whether they want a voiceover, a tagline, or a call to action beyond the end card (e.g. website, phone, social handle, "order ahead").
- Which days the 7am–3pm hours apply to (the end card is fixed as written, but the user should confirm it is accurate).
- Any special offer, launch, or seasonal theme to mention.
