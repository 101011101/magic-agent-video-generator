# SENDLY Logo Animation — Shooting Script

**Duration:** 6 seconds  
**Style:** Clean, minimal 2D animation on a white background.  
**Logo treatment:** Use the supplied orange circle, white paper plane, and navy SENDLY lettering without changing their colors, shapes, or proportions.  
**Transitions:** Continuous animation; no hard cuts.

| Scene / Shot | Timing | Shot type & camera movement | Action | On-screen text | Dialogue / narration | Sound & music |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Icon entrance / Shot 1** | **0.0–1.5 sec** (1.5 sec) | Static graphic shot, framed to accommodate the complete logo. Camera locked; no pan or zoom. | Begin on white. The orange circle and white paper plane appear together in their final position, fading in while scaling gently from 90% to 100%. Ease smoothly to a stop; no bounce. | None. | None. | A soft, brief airy whoosh accompanies the entrance. No music. |
| **2 — Wordmark reveal / Shot 2** | **1.5–3.0 sec** (1.5 sec) | Same framing and locked camera. | The navy wordmark fades in to the right of the circle, sliding approximately 12 pixels rightward into its original position. The icon remains still. Finish with the exact spacing and alignment of the supplied logo. | **SENDLY**, using the original logo lettering. | None. | A quiet, rounded chime sounds as the wordmark settles. No music. |
| **3 — Finished logo / Shot 3** | **3.0–6.0 sec** (3 sec) | Static hero shot of the complete logo. No camera movement. | Hold the finished logo, centered as a complete unit, with generous white space around it. No further animation. End on the fully visible logo rather than fading out. | **SENDLY** remains visible. No additional text. | None. | The chime decays naturally, followed by silence. No music. |

**Production note:** Animate the icon and wordmark as separate layers extracted from the supplied file. Keep the paper plane fixed inside the circle. A transparent-background version can use the same animation without the white background.
