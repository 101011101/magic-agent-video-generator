# Video Script Brief: Simple SENDLY Logo Animation

## Task
Write a concise, time-coded animation script using the brief below. Describe what appears on screen, how it moves, transition timing, audio, and the final frame. Do not generate the video itself.

## User’s Exact Requirement
> “make a simple animation of my logo”

## Supplied Upload
One logo file, described as:
> “a flat orange circle with a white paper plane inside it, and the word SENDLY in dark navy sans-serif letters to the right of the circle, on a transparent background.”

### Required Use of the Upload
- Use the uploaded logo as the primary visual asset, not as inspiration for a replacement.
- Preserve its orange circle, white paper plane, dark navy lettering, typography, proportions, and icon-to-wordmark arrangement.
- Use the actual colors in the file; do not invent color codes.
- Keep the complete logo readable and undistorted in the final frame.
- Do not assume the file contains independently editable layers. Favor animation of the complete logo; suggest component animation only if the asset supports it.

## Goal and Core Idea
Create a simple logo reveal that introduces SENDLY clearly and makes the existing logo recognizable. The idea to communicate is the brand’s visual identity—not an invented product benefit, slogan, or business claim.

## Audience and Platform
- **Audience:** General viewers encountering the SENDLY brand. Assumed; no specific audience was supplied.
- **Platform:** General-purpose digital use, such as a website or video intro. Assumed; no destination platform was supplied.

## Format and Technical Defaults
All specifications below are assumptions, not user requirements:
- **Format:** Short, standalone 2D logo animation.
- **Duration:** 5 seconds.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9.
- **Working resolution:** 1920 × 1080.
- **Frame rate:** 30 fps.
- **Background:** Plain white, providing contrast for the orange icon and navy wordmark.
- **Ending:** Hold the complete logo still for approximately the final 2 seconds.
- **Audio:** Silent; no voiceover or music.
- **Looping:** Not required.

## Tone and Style
Clean, minimal, polished, and restrained. Interpret “simple” as limited movement, uncluttered framing, and smooth easing. Avoid elaborate effects, busy backgrounds, dramatic camera moves, or unnecessary decorative elements.

## Suggested Animation Direction
As a default creative approach, reveal the complete logo with a gentle fade-in and a subtle scale-up into its centered final position, then hold. Preserve generous margins and the logo’s original proportions.

This approach is an assumption, not an explicitly requested animation style. Do not add a tagline, call to action, or other on-screen copy.

## Missing Information Only the User Can Supply
- Intended platform and placement.
- Target audience and usage context.
- Preferred duration, aspect ratio, and delivery specifications.
- Brand guidelines or mandatory background treatments.
- Preferred movement style or reference animations.
- Whether sound, looping, or transparent-background delivery is wanted.
- A layered or vector asset, if independently animated logo components are desired and the uploaded file does not support them.

Proceed with the stated defaults rather than inventing these details or delaying the script.

## Required Script Output
Provide a compact timeline with:
1. Time range.
2. On-screen visuals and composition.
3. Animation and easing.
4. Audio.
5. Final-frame description.

Clearly distinguish the user’s requirement from assumed production choices.
