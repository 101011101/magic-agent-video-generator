## Provisional concept: “Ordinary, Reframed”
An ordinary glass of water becomes cinematic through light, framing, and movement.

**Assumptions—not confirmed preferences:** This is a 15-second, vertical 9:16 Instagram Reel. “Cool” is interpreted as clean, cinematic, and visually polished. A drinking glass is the proposed subject, not something you requested. No promotional message or call to action is assumed.

### What to film
**Suggested setup:** A phone, a clear drinking glass, water, and a table beside a window. Use a desk lamp from the side if daylight is unavailable. No existing footage is required.

### Timed script

| Time | Shot-by-shot visuals | Exact on-screen text / optional voiceover | Editing and audio cues |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:02** | Start with a hand covering the lens. Pull it away quickly to reveal an empty glass on the table, filmed from a plain, eye-level angle. | **“Nothing special?”** | Reveal within the first half-second. Begin with a soft whoosh and a single musical beat. |
| **0:02–0:04** | Cut to a tight shot of the glass rim catching the light. Slowly move the phone a few centimeters sideways so the highlight travels across the rim. | **No text.** | Hard cut on the beat. Keep the movement controlled; avoid digital zoom. |
| **0:04–0:07** | Pour water into the glass. Frame tightly enough that the stream and rising water fill most of the screen. | **No text.** | Let the pouring sound come forward. Optional slow motion if the phone supports it. |
| **0:07–0:10** | Film slightly above the glass as the water ripples settle. Position the light to catch the surface. | **No text.** | Cut from the falling stream to the moving ripples. Add a subtle musical build. |
| **0:10–0:13.5** | **Visual payoff:** Reveal the filled glass from a low, three-quarter angle, with light shining through it and its shadow visible on the table. Make a small, steady push toward it. | **“Look closer.”** Optional VO: **“Same glass. Different perspective.”** | Land the reveal on the strongest beat. Hold long enough for the composition to register. |
| **0:13.5–0:15** | A hand approaches and fully covers the lens. End on the covered frame. | **No text.** | Fade text before the hand enters. Match the final covered frame to the opening so replay feels like another reveal. |

### Production notes
- **Shoot the ordinary wide shot first**, then move closer and lower for the cinematic shots.
- Lock focus and exposure for each shot; lower exposure slightly if the glass highlights look blown out.
- Use clean cuts, one consistent color treatment, and small white text placed away from Instagram’s interface.
- **Audio is an enhancement:** choose a restrained instrumental with a beat change near 10 seconds. If using music, check usage rights for the intended account and post.
- The concept works without sound: **ordinary view → striking details → polished reveal**. No user-specific wording or branding is needed for this draft.
