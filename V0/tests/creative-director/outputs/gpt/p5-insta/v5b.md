## Baseline — first-frame state

**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080 × 1920, 24 fps. **Total duration: 49 seconds.** Designed as a visually striking Instagram video.

**Style:** Cinematic photorealism with seamless magical-realism effects, tactile wet surfaces, restrained film grain and soft highlight bloom. No cartoon rendering.

**Character:** One young adult with medium-brown skin and short, tousled dark hair. Their appearance remains consistent throughout. Oversized charcoal jacket, plain black shirt, dark trousers, spotless white sneakers, initially with two white shoelaces. They begin just inside the corner-store doorway, holding a plain kraft-paper cup in their left hand. Their expression is neutral.

**Products and objects:**
- One unbranded paper cup; its appearance remains unchanged.
- An ordinary shallow puddle beside the curb.
- A flickering pink corner-store sign reading **“OPEN,”** reflected in the puddle.
- Closed shop shutters, unpainted brick walls and a pedestrian crossing with flat white stripes.
- Rooftops with chimneys and hanging laundry. Initially, the laundry is still and non-glowing.
- No fish, luminous ripples, flower murals, floating stairs, drifting constellations or sparks.

**Environment:** A nearly deserted city street. The tiny corner store is the only visibly active business; neighboring shops have their shutters down. Rain-wet pavement and asphalt glisten. No other people or moving vehicles.

**Time and lighting:** Late night throughout; no dawn transition. Cool-white fluorescent light spills from the store. Dim street lighting and the flickering pink sign illuminate the wet surfaces.

**Palette:** Charcoal, slate, muted brick, cool white and localized pink reflections.

**Camera:** Stationary, street-level wide establishing shot. The street appears empty because the character is still inside the doorway.

**Audio:** Quiet nighttime city ambience, fluorescent hum, faint sign buzz and a subdued instrumental electronic beat. No dialogue or vocals.

**On-screen text:** Only the diegetic “OPEN” sign; no captions, logos or added overlays.

**Continuity rule:** Unlisted properties persist, including off-screen objects. The shoelaces change during the reset, before the homeward walk, but their colors are not clearly revealed until the final close-up.
