- **Ships:** Several vessels far offshore, initially travelling steadily in open water. Their navigation lights are on.
- **Reef:** A fixed dark reef offshore.
- **Products:** None.

- Dawn on a rocky beach during a retreating tide.
- Pools between the rocks reflect the pale sky; ribbons of kelp and broken shells lie along the shore.
- No storm or rain yet. The sea is relatively calm.
- Diffuse pearl-grey daylight illuminates the beach. Cottage interiors receive subdued window light; the operating lighthouse lamp supplies its own light.
- No fresh storm-cast driftwood is present yet.
