# Video Script-Writing Prompt

## Task
Write a complete, production-ready script for a **two-minute, dialogue-free short film** based on the requirements below. Use timed sequences, visual action, and musical direction rather than spoken narration.

## Goal and Core Idea
- Tell a clear visual story in which a lighthouse keeper discovers a mysterious glowing shell, uses it to restore the lighthouse during a storm and save ships, then returns it to the beach.
- Convey melancholy, quiet courage, and the temporary nature of a gift.
- **Assumed thematic interpretation:** Something borrowed from the sea helps protect others, then is returned. Suggest this through imagery without explaining the shell’s origin or powers.

## Audience and Platform
- **Audience — assumed:** General audiences who enjoy atmospheric, visually driven short films.
- **Platform — assumed:** Online video or digital short-film presentation. No specific service was named.

## Format and Technical Specifications
- **Format:** Cinematic narrative short film.
- **Duration:** Exactly 2 minutes, including any titles or credits if used.
- **Aspect ratio — assumed:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Script format:** Timestamped sequences totaling 120 seconds, with separate descriptions of visuals/action and score.
- **Dialogue:** None.
- **Audio:** Score only. No voice-over, spoken words, or standalone sound effects.
- **Titles/credits — assumed:** Omit unless requested, preserving the full runtime for the story.

## Tone and Visual Style
- Melancholy, restrained, atmospheric, and painterly.
- **Assumed visual approach:** Soft textures, expressive light, muted coastal colors, and luminous contrast around the shell and lighthouse lamp.
- Let framing, movement, facial expression, and music carry the story.
- Keep the successful rescue emotionally understated rather than triumphant.
- Preserve the mystery; do not add explanatory lore.

## User Requirements — Exact Quotations
1. “A short film, 2 minutes.”
2. “A lighthouse keeper finds a glowing shell on the beach at dawn.”
3. “She brings it home.”
4. “At night it hums and the lamp goes out.”
5. “She climbs the tower in a storm and uses the shell to relight it.”
6. “Ships are saved.”
7. “Morning again, she puts the shell back on the beach.”
8. “Melancholy, painterly, no dialogue, just score.”

## Required Story Execution
Preserve the stated sequence:
1. Dawn: the keeper discovers the glowing shell on the beach.
2. She brings it home.
3. Night: the shell hums and the lighthouse lamp goes out.
4. During a storm, she climbs the tower with the shell.
5. She uses the shell to relight the lamp.
6. Clearly show that the restored lighthouse light saves ships.
7. The following morning, she returns the shell to the beach.

Do not imply that the shell caused the outage unless necessary for the chosen interpretation; the user specified their timing, not a causal explanation.

### Audio Interpretation
To reconcile “it hums” with “just score,” represent the shell’s humming through a sustained musical motif within the score, supported by visual cues. Do not introduce a separate diegetic humming effect, storm effects, footsteps, or ship sounds.

## Uploads and Their Use
- **Uploads:** “None.”
- No uploaded footage, images, music, or reference material is available.
- Create the script from the written request alone; do not refer to nonexistent assets.

## Assumptions
All choices below are provisional, not user-stated requirements:
- General audience; online or digital short-film presentation.
- 16:9 landscape framing.
- A timeless coastal setting, with no specified country or historical period.
- An unnamed keeper; no added backstory is required.
- Painterly cinematic imagery rather than a prescribed live-action or animation technique.
- No explanatory on-screen text.
- Musical representation of the shell’s hum to preserve score-only audio.

## Missing Information Only the User Can Confirm
- Intended audience and specific release platform.
- Required aspect ratio, if different from 16:9.
- Preferred production medium: live action, animation, or another technique.
- Any mandatory branding, titles, credits, accessibility requirements, or production constraints.

Use the stated assumptions unless the user supplies these details; they do not prevent drafting.

## Output Requirements
Return the script with:
- Timestamped sequences totaling exactly 120 seconds.
- Concrete, filmable visual action.
- Score direction and transitions.
- Clear visual causality between relighting the lighthouse and saving the ships.
- No dialogue, voice-over, or added explanatory exposition.
