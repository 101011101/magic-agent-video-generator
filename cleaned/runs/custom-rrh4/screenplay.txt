LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD

1. EXT. FOREST PATH — DAY

Little Red Riding Hood’s mother stands beside the forest path with a wicker basket on her arm.
She tucks bread into the basket.
She nestles a small cake beside the bread.
                    LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD’S MOTHER
          Take these to Grandmother’s cottage, where she keeps her quilt and nightcap on her bed and a needle and thread in her bedside sewing basket, and stay on the forest path, where daisies grow along the edges.
Little Red Riding Hood ties her scarlet hood beneath her chin.
Little Red Riding Hood’s mother passes her the basket.
Little Red Riding Hood walks away from us along the forest path.
Daisies line both edges of the path.

2. EXT. UNDER AN OAK TREE — CONTINUOUS

Little Red Riding Hood walks toward an oak tree beside the path.
A wolf steps from behind the oak onto the path, blocking her way.
                    WOLF
          Where are you going?
                    LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD
          To Grandmother’s cottage beside the three birch trees.
                    WOLF
          Why not bring her flowers?
Little Red Riding Hood kneels beside the daisies on the right.
She plucks daisies from the edge of the path.
The wolf races away from us along the path while she keeps picking.
Little Red Riding Hood tucks the daisies into her basket.

3. EXT. THE COTTAGE’S GREEN FRONT DOOR — DAY   (CUT)

Grandmother’s cottage stands beside three birch trees.
The wolf runs toward the cottage’s green front door, away from us.
He knocks on the door.
He calls through the door in a sweet voice.
                    WOLF
          It’s Little Red Riding Hood!

4. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — CONTINUOUS   (CUT)

Grandmother lies beneath a quilt in the bed on the right.
A nightcap rests beside her pillow.
A sewing basket stands beside the bed.
                    GRANDMOTHER
          Lift the latch.
The latch rises on the green door at the back of the room.
The door swings inward.
The wolf rushes through the doorway toward the bed on the right.
He opens his jaws wide over Grandmother.
Grandmother disappears whole into his mouth.
The wolf swallows.
He climbs onto the bed.
He picks up the nightcap from beside the pillow.
He pulls the nightcap onto his head.
He draws the quilt over himself.
The wolf lies still with his eyes on the green door.

5. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — LATER   (CUT)

The green door swings inward beneath Little Red Riding Hood’s hand.
Little Red Riding Hood enters toward us, carrying her wicker basket.
She places the basket on the wooden table to the left.
She crosses right toward the bed.
The wolf’s large ears stick out from under the nightcap.
                    LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD
          Grandmother, what big ears you have!
                    WOLF
          All the better to hear you with.
The wolf’s large eyes follow her.
                    LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD
          What big eyes you have!
                    WOLF
          All the better to see you with.
Little Red Riding Hood grips her cloak.
                    LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD
          What big teeth you have!
                    WOLF
          All the better to eat you with!
The wolf springs up from beneath the quilt.
Little Red Riding Hood screams.
The wolf opens his jaws over her.
Little Red Riding Hood disappears whole into his mouth.
The wolf swallows.
He slumps back onto the bed.
His eyes close.
Loud snores rumble from his open mouth.

6. EXT. OUTSIDE THE COTTAGE — DAY   (CUT)

A woodcutter walks from left to right past the cottage, shears hanging from his belt.
The wolf’s snores carry through the green doorway.
The woodcutter stops.
He turns toward the green door.
He steps through the doorway, away from us.

7. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — CONTINUOUS

The woodcutter approaches the bed on the right.
His gaze settles on the wolf’s swollen belly.
He draws the quilt down from the belly.
He takes the shears from his belt.
He makes small, careful snips along the wolf’s belly.
The cut parts into an opening.
Little Red Riding Hood scrambles through the opening onto the quilt.
She reaches back into the opening.
Grandmother takes her hands.
Little Red Riding Hood pulls as Grandmother climbs free onto the bed.
Little Red Riding Hood and Grandmother tremble.
They hug.
The woodcutter returns the shears to his belt.

8. EXT. OUTSIDE THE COTTAGE — DAY   (CUT)

The woodcutter gathers stones from the ground into his arms.
Little Red Riding Hood gathers stones in the lifted hem of her cloak.
Grandmother gathers stones against her chest.
They carry the stones toward the green front door on the right.

9. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — CONTINUOUS

The woodcutter carries his stones through the green door toward the bed.
Little Red Riding Hood follows with stones cradled in her cloak.
Grandmother follows with stones in her arms.
The wolf snores with his eyes closed.
The woodcutter lowers his stones through the opening in the wolf’s belly.
Little Red Riding Hood tips her stones into the opening.
Grandmother adds her stones to the belly.
Grandmother lifts a needle and thread from her bedside sewing basket.
She passes the thread through the needle’s eye.
She stitches the wolf’s belly shut.

10. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — LATER   (CUT)

The wolf’s eyes open.
He climbs out of the bed toward us.
He stumbles forward under the weight of his belly.
He falls heavily onto the floor.
The wolf lies motionless, his eyes fixed and his chest still.

11. INT. GRANDMOTHER’S COTTAGE — LATER   (CUT)

Grandmother, Little Red Riding Hood and the woodcutter sit around the wooden table.
Grandmother lifts the small cake from the wicker basket.
She breaks the cake into three pieces.
She gives one piece to Little Red Riding Hood on her left.
She gives another piece to the woodcutter on her right.
She takes the remaining piece.
                    LITTLE RED RIDING HOOD
          I promise never to wander from the path again.
