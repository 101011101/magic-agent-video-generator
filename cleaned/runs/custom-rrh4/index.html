Story (prose → seed → blocking)

Little Red Riding Hood’s mother tucked bread and a small cake into a wicker basket. “Take these to Grandmother’s cottage, and stay on the forest path.” Red tied her scarlet hood and set off. Under an oak tree beside a patch of daisies, a wolf stepped out from between the trees and onto the path. “Where are you going?” he asked. “To Grandmother’s cottage beside the three birch trees,” Red replied. Inside the cottage, where Grandmother lay in bed wearing her nightcap, a needle and thread lay in Grandmother’s open bedside sewing basket. Her quilt covered her from chin to toes. “Why not bring her flowers?” suggested the wolf. While Red picked daisies, he raced ahead. At the cottage’s green front door, the wolf knocked and called in a sweet voice, “It’s Little Red Riding Hood!” “Lift the latch,” Grandmother answered from bed, tucked beneath her quilt. The wolf rushed inside, took Grandmother’s nightcap from her head, and swallowed her whole. He put on her nightcap, pulled her quilt over himself, and waited. Red arrived, pushed open the green door, and placed her basket on the wooden table. She approached the bed. “Grandmother, what big ears you have!” “All the better to hear you with.” “What big eyes you have!” “All the better to see you with.” Red gripped her cloak. “What big teeth you have!” “All the better to eat you with!” The wolf sprang up. Red screamed, but he swallowed her whole. Then he fell asleep, snoring loudly. A passing woodcutter heard the snores and entered through the green door. Seeing the wolf’s swollen belly, he took a pair of shears from his belt and carefully snipped it open. Red scrambled out, then helped Grandmother climb free. Trembling with relief, they hugged. They filled the sleeping wolf’s belly with stones gathered from outside the cottage. Grandmother stitched the wolf’s belly shut with a needle and thread from her bedside sewing basket. When he awoke, he stumbled and fell dead. Grandmother shared the cake with her rescuers. Red promised never to wander from the path again.
