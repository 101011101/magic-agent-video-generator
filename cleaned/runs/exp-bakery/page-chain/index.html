What this scene shows

Two frames, two changes of state, zero drift: the chain holds identity (the sheet), the room (the plate) and the sequence (one edit per beat) at once, which no grid did. The open questions are the ones the grid never reached: how many edits deep a scene can go before the picture degrades (scene 2 is ten frames), and the time budget per scene, which should set the frame count instead of the screenplay's beat count.
