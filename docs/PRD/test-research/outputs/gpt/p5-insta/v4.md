# SHOOTING SCRIPT — “SWITCH THE VIBE”

**Format:** Instagram Reel · 9:16 vertical · **24 seconds**  
**Concept:** An ordinary moment flips into a cinematic entrance with one finger snap.  
**Cast/location:** One person, one quiet street or textured wall. Shoot on a phone.  
**Visual style:** Muted opening → warm, high-contrast reveal → confident, clean finish.  
**Music:** A cleared electronic beat with a bass drop at **6 seconds**.

**Timing: 0–6 seconds**

**0:00–0:03 | 3 seconds**
- **Shot type:** Medium shot, waist up.
- **Camera movement:** Slow push toward the subject.
- **Action:** Subject stands against a plain wall, scrolling on their phone. They pause and look directly into the lens.

- **Dialogue/narration:** None.
- **Sound/music:** Quiet street ambience. A filtered electronic pulse begins.
- **Direction:** Keep the pose casual and the colors slightly desaturated.

### SHOT 2 — THE SWITCH
**0:03–0:06 | 3 seconds**
- **Shot type:** Extreme close-up of the subject’s hand.
- **Camera movement:** Quick push-in, stopping just before the snap.
- **Action:** Subject raises their hand and snaps their fingers. Cut precisely as the fingers connect.
- **On-screen text:** **“DIFFERENT ENERGY.”** Appears at 4 seconds.
- **Dialogue/narration:** None.
- **Sound/music:** The music briefly drops out. An exaggerated finger snap lands at 6 seconds.
