## Instagram Reel: “Nothing Special”
**Length:** 15 seconds · **Style:** cinematic, quick cuts, playful twist

**0:00–0:03 — The boring opening**  
Sit on your couch or stand in your room. Keep the shot still and ordinary.  
**Voiceover:** “Nothing special happened today.”  
**On-screen text:** `just a normal day`

**0:03–0:04 — The switch**  
Cover the lens with your hand. Cut to your hand pulling away somewhere new.  
**Audio:** The beat drops as the lens clears.

**0:04–0:10 — Make ordinary look expensive**  
Quick close-ups: shoes hitting the pavement, pouring a drink, grabbing your keys, sunlight on your face. Mix one slow-motion shot with fast cuts.  
**Voiceover:** “So I decided to film it like something did.”

**0:10–0:13 — The hero shot**  
Walk past the camera or look back over your shoulder. Hold the shot for a beat.  
**On-screen text:** `no occasion. just a vibe.`

**0:13–0:15 — The punchline**  
Hard cut to you back on the couch, eating a snack. Let the music stop.  
**Voiceover:** “Anyway. Back to doing nothing.”

**Caption:** “Low-budget day. High-budget energy.”
