# Script: VOLT — "Charged" (6 shots, ~15 s)

**Format:** Product commercial, exactly 6 shots
**Duration:** ~15 seconds
**Aspect ratio:** 9:16 vertical; can and logo kept in the central safe area (crops to 1:1 and 16:9)
**Audio:** Driving, bass-heavy electronic track (~128 BPM) with sound-design hits on every cut. No voiceover.
**Product reference:** The uploaded product photo is the reference image for **every** shot.

### Can lock (applies to all 6 shots)
- Matte black finish — not glossy, not grey, not silver
- 355 ml can, same proportions as the photo
- Neon green lightning-bolt logo — same shape, size, position, colour
- "VOLT" in white capitals — same font, spelling, size, placement
- Nothing else added to the can. Logo side faces camera and is readable in every shot. No hands, liquid, sparks or motion blur over the logo.

---

## Shot 1 — Reveal (0:00–0:02.5)
**Visual:** Total darkness. A thin line of neon green rim light traces up the edges of the can, revealing its silhouette. As the light reaches the top, the lightning-bolt logo and "VOLT" glow into view, front-on, centred.
**Camera:** Locked, slight slow push-in.
**Sound:** Low electric hum rising → sharp zap as the logo appears. Beat drops in.

## Shot 2 — Hero detail (0:02.5–0:05)
**Visual:** The can on a dark reflective surface, beaded with cold condensation. The camera orbits about 30° around the front of the can (never past the logo edge) so droplets catch the green light. Logo and "VOLT" stay fully legible throughout.
**Camera:** Slow arc orbit, then a quick push-in to finish.
**Sound:** Deep bass pulse; a single droplet "tick".

## Shot 3 — The crack (0:05–0:07.5)
**Visual:** Slightly high front angle: the logo faces camera while a finger (from behind/above the can, not crossing the label) pulls the tab. On the crack, a burst of fizz mist and small neon green electric sparks bursts *up and out from the top* — above the logo, not over it.
**Camera:** Speed ramp — real-time pull, slow-mo on the burst.
**Sound:** Loud can crack + fizz, followed by a crackle of electricity.

## Shot 4 — Energy (0:07.5–0:10)
**Visual:** A night-time city skate park lit with neon. An adult skater lands a trick and, in one move, raises the VOLT can toward camera, gripping it from the back so the logo and "VOLT" are fully visible. Face is secondary and out of focus; the can is the sharp focal point.
**Camera:** Handheld energy, whip into a stable hold on the can.
**Sound:** Board-landing slam timed to the beat.

## Shot 5 — Impact (0:10–0:12.5)
**Visual:** The can stands alone on a dark surface. A bolt of neon green lightning strikes down beside it and wraps around the can in an electric spiral — passing behind and around, never across the logo. Freeze-frame at the peak: the can lit in green, logo crisp and readable.
**Camera:** Speed ramp into freeze, slight dolly-in.
**Sound:** Thunderclap/electric strike → a hard cut to near-silence on the freeze.

## Shot 6 — Pack shot (0:12.5–0:15)
**Visual:** The can centred, logo forward, on a dark background with a soft neon green glow behind it and faint electric haze. Leave clear space above or below the can for an overlay.
**On-screen text (overlay, not generated in footage):** `VOLT`
**Camera:** Locked off; very slow push-in.
**Sound:** Final bass hit; electric hum fades out.
