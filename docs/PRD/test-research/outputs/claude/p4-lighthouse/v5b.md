# The Keeper and the Shell — Operational Chunks

## Baseline (first frame)

- **Character — The keeper** (locked): woman in her fifties, grey hair loose at her temples, long wool coat, knitted scarf. Slow, careful movement. No dialogue at any point.
- **Object — The shell** (locked when introduced): spiral shell, pale as pearl, with its own soft blue-gold light pulsing like a heartbeat inside its curve.
- **Object — The lighthouse** (locked): on a spur of black rock above the beach, white paint weathered to the color of bone, lamp room at the top with a great lens in a brass frame, iron door at the base, spiral stairs inside.
- **Location — The cottage** (locked when introduced): at the foot of the tower; kettle, single chair, table with a logbook and a cracked cup, windowsill.
- **Environment:** cold northern shore, dark sand, quiet sea with long slow waves, seaweed and broken driftwood on the tideline.
- **Time of day:** dawn.
- **Lighting:** grey and rose dawn light, light that seems to come from inside the clouds; lighthouse lamp dim.
- **Palette:** greys, rose, bone white, dark sand, muted sea blues.
- **Style:** painterly, like an oil painting — soft edges, heavy skies. Melancholy.
- **Audio:** score only, no dialogue. Ambient sea (waves hissing on sand). Score not yet started.
- **Aspect ratio:** not specified in the request — 2.39:1 or 16:9 widescreen (16:9 default).
- **Total duration:** 2 minutes (120 s).
