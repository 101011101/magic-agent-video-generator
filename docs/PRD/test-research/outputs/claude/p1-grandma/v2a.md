# Video Script Brief: Animated Photo of Grandma

## Goal and core idea
Bring a single still photo of the user's grandmother to life. The video must show her, as she appears in the photo, breaking into a warm smile and waving at the camera. The idea to get across: a cherished, familiar image of a loved one becoming briefly "alive" — a small, affectionate moment of connection.

## Audience and platform
- **Audience:** The user and their family (personal, sentimental use). Not a commercial or public-facing piece.
- **Platform:** Not specified. Assume it will be viewed on a phone and shared privately (messaging apps, family group chats), possibly posted to social media.

## Format, duration and aspect ratio
- **Format:** Single continuous shot, image-to-video animation of the uploaded photo. No cuts, no added scenes, no camera moves that reveal areas outside the original frame.
- **Duration:** ~5 seconds (user said "like 5 seconds"; 4–6 seconds is acceptable).
- **Aspect ratio:** Match the aspect ratio of the uploaded photo so nothing is cropped or invented at the edges. If a fixed ratio is required, use the closest standard ratio to the original (e.g. 4:3 or 3:4) and avoid cropping her face, hands or glasses.

## Tone and style
- Warm, gentle, tender, natural.
- Photorealistic and faithful to the original photo: same lighting (sunlit living room), same colours, same grain/quality.
- Movement must be subtle and believable for an elderly woman: slow, soft, unhurried. No exaggerated, cartoonish or uncanny motion.
- Optional: very slight, natural secondary motion (a blink, a small head tilt, a breath, light shifting slightly) to feel alive. No dramatic camera movement; at most an almost-imperceptible slow push-in.

## User requirements (quoted exactly)
- "animate this photo of my grandma"
- "so she smiles and waves"
- "like 5 seconds"

## Uploads and how they must be used
- **Upload:** One photo — an elderly woman with short white hair, round glasses and a green cardigan, sitting in an armchair in a sunlit living room, looking at the camera with a neutral expression.
- **Usage:**
  - The photo is the first frame and the only visual source. The video starts on the photo exactly as it is.
  - Her identity must be preserved precisely: face shape, features, short white hair, round glasses, green cardigan, armchair, living room and lighting must not change.
  - Action sequence within the shot: neutral expression (as in photo) → a natural, warm smile forms → she lifts one hand and gives a gentle wave toward the camera → hand lowers or settles, smile remains.
  - She stays seated in the armchair throughout. Eye contact with the camera is maintained.
  - Do not add people, objects, text, or change the background.

## Assumptions (not stated by the user)
- She waves with one hand (assume her right hand, or whichever hand is most visible/free in the photo).
- No audio is required; if audio is added, use only quiet room ambience. No music, no voice, no speech, no lip movement as if talking.
- No text, titles, captions or watermarks.
- Ending: she holds the smile in the final moment (a calm end frame suitable for looping or sharing). The video does not need to return to the neutral expression.
- Smile is closed-mouth or a gentle open smile; do not invent teeth details prominently if they are not visible in the photo.
- The wave starts around 1.5–2 s in, after the smile has begun, and finishes by ~4.5 s.

## Missing information only the user can supply
- Where the video will be shared (affects aspect ratio and whether music is wanted).
- Which hand she should wave with, if it matters to them.
- Whether they want sound or music (e.g. a song that meant something to her).
- Whether they want any text, such as her name or a short message.
- Whether the video should loop back to her neutral expression or end on the smile.
- Whether she is still living or this is a memorial piece, which would affect tone (e.g. softer, more nostalgic treatment).
